**Official PURSUE description** (Department of War / AARO, release 08):

This document is an administrative or programmatic record related to the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP), a Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)-administered program active from 2008 to 2012. AAWSAP’s official scope of work identified 12 technical research areas relating to potential aerospace threats over a time horizon of more than 40 years. As an administrative record, the file documents how AAWSAP was scoped, organized, tasked, funded, or described at a particular point in time.
This document is preparatory material for a scheduled senior leadership meeting involving Senator Harry Reid (D-NV) on 2 January 2009, intended to equip DIA leadership with concise status talking points on the AAWSAP contract in case he raised the subject. The document states that Senators Daniel Inouye (D-HI) and Harry Reid sponsored the July 2008 supplemental appropriations language directing DIA to investigate foreign advanced aerospace threats over a 40-year horizon, and it presents Reid’s interest in aerospace technologies as part of the background for the engagement. It further notes that Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies had been the only respondent to the August 2008 request for proposals, that its proposal was judged responsive, and that the contract had been signed on 22 September 2008. The document notes that DIA assessed the program to have produced reports of limited value, along with a recommendation that AAWSAP be transitioned to another agency.
