**Official PURSUE description** (Department of War / AARO, release 08):

This memorandum requests approval in principle for a Central Intelligence Agency-sponsored external research project focusing on Unidentified Flying Objects (UFOs). The proposed effort would convene an ad hoc panel of leading scientists to analyze and evaluate available UFO evidence and recommend any further government action warranted to address the matter. The memorandum describes the project as an important component of the office’s effort to improve intelligence on UFOs and support an Office of Scientific Intelligence task force working with the U.S. Air Force since 20 August 1952. Although the memorandum is undated, its reference to Dr. Max Millikan as Director of CENIS (a position he held 1951-1952) and to the ongoing Air Force collaboration supports a late-1952 date.
