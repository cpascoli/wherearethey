**Official PURSUE description** (Department of War / AARO, release 08):

This document is an administrative, programmatic, or contractor-produced record related to the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP), a Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)-administered program active from 2008 to 2012. AAWSAP’s official scope of work identified 12 technical research areas relating to potential aerospace threats over a time horizon of more than 40 years. As part of the broader program record, the file documents how AAWSAP was scoped, organized, tasked, funded, executed, or described at a particular point in time.
This file contains a program report produced by Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies (BAASS), summarizing its work under the AAWSAP contract through July 30, 2009, while also including substantial forward-looking discussion of planned or proposed work. It includes unresolved internal process questions and comparative assessments of equipment options.
In addition to documenting the production of a series of technical research papers, the report describes archival and documentary research concerning historical incidents reported to involve unidentified anomalous phenomena. The report also documents BAASS inquiries into parapsychological and paranormal phenomena, including research activities at a Utah site with a reported history of anomalous activity.
