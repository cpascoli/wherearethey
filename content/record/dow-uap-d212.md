**Official PURSUE description** (Department of War / AARO, release 08):

This file contains a U.S. Air Force Project 10073 Record Card and related correspondence, including drawn depictions, concerning a reported observation of an unidentified flying object from San Pedro, California, on November 30, 1966. Two observers described seeing a bright light in the direction of Catalina Island. The record card summarizes the reported object as white with a red center, brighter than the brightest star, and comparable in brightness to the moon. It records that the object moved northwest and disappeared slowly after an observation lasting approximately five minutes and 10 seconds.
The file also includes a March 15, 1967, Air Force letter requesting that one of the reporting individuals complete a technical questionnaire because the information initially received was insufficient for a scientific evaluation. In the completed questionnaire, the observer described seeing the object outdoors from San Pedro at approximately 5:45 p.m. Pacific Standard Time and stated that an airplane appeared to approach before the object began to disappear. The Air Force assessed the reported object as a possible hot-air balloon. However, the file does not document the basis for that assessment.
