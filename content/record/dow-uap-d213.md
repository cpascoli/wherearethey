**Official PURSUE description** (Department of War / AARO, release 08):

This file contains U.S. Air Force investigative records and related correspondence concerning two separate reported observations of unidentified flying objects in Southern California on June 17, 1949. The materials include an Office of Special Investigations report regarding an observation from La Jolla and a later Air Materiel Command inquiry into a report from Glendale.
In the La Jolla report, an observer described seeing one large, bright, round object at approximately 11:10 a.m. local time. The individual estimated that the object was about four miles away and 4,000 feet above the ground, compared its apparent size to a Douglas DC-4 aircraft, and described it as resembling a “chromium plated pie tin.” The report states that the object moved rapidly away, made a wide turn, and remained visible for approximately 30 seconds.
A separate report documents an observation from a moving automobile near Grand Central Airport in Glendale at approximately 3:30 p.m. local time. The reporting individual described two bright metallic, oval objects that reflected sunlight, approached from the northeast, traveled slowly across the valley, then tilted and departed rapidly toward the northwest. The objects remained in view for approximately one and a half minutes.
The Project 10073 Record Card associated with the Glendale observation assessed the reported objects as aircraft, stating that the available information did not indicate that they could not have been aircraft. The file does not document a comparable final evaluation of the La Jolla report.
