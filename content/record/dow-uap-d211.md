**Official PURSUE description** (Department of War / AARO, release 08):

This file contains a U.S. Air Force Project 10073 Record Card and associated materials concerning a reported observation of an unidentified flying object near Fontana, California, on August 21, 1956. A civilian Ground Observer Corps supervisor reported seeing one round, red object with a tail. The observer described the object as approximately the size of a basketball at arm’s length, moving eastward, and disappearing into fog after approximately two minutes. No photographs or physical evidence accompanied the report. The associated record card assesses the reported object as probably a jet aircraft, with the apparent tail likely a contrail, though lists the cause as undetermined at the time of the initial duty officer review.
