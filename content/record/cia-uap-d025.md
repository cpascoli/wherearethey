**Official PURSUE description** (Department of War / AARO, release 08):

This circa 2 October 1952 draft memorandum from Central Intelligence Agency Director Walter B. Smith to the Director of the Psychological Strategy Board transmits a proposal to the National Security Council concerning “Flying Saucers,” or Unidentified Flying Objects (UFOs). The memorandum states that UFO-related issues may have implications for psychological warfare, intelligence, and operations, and recommends that the Board discuss potential offensive and defensive psychological warfare considerations. Documents in the file summarize the issue, describe related Air Force investigative activity, and identify intelligence and research questions concerning the reported phenomena, including possible implications for U.S. national security.
The materials state that, since 1947, the U.S. Government had received approximately 1,500 official reports of UFO sightings, in addition to a substantial volume of letters, telephone calls, and press reports from the public. They further state that the Air Force considered approximately 20 percent of the official reports unexplained, with a higher unexplained rate reported for cases received during 1952. The attached proposal recommended a centrally administered research program under the Research and Development Board to address operational, intelligence, and psychological-warfare requirements identified by the Secretary of Defense, the Director of Central Intelligence, and the Director of the Psychological Strategy Board.
