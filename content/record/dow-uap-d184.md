**Official PURSUE description** (Department of War / AARO, release 08):

This document is an administrative or programmatic record related to the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP), a Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)-administered program active from 2008 to 2012. AAWSAP’s official scope of work identified 12 technical research areas relating to potential aerospace threats over a time horizon of more than 40 years. As an administrative record, the file documents how AAWSAP was scoped, organized, tasked, funded, or described at a particular point in time.
This document is preparatory material for a scheduled senior-leadership meeting involving Senator Harry Reid (D-NV) in May 2009, intended to equip the Director of the DIA with concise status points on the AAWSAP contract should Reid raise the topic. The document states that Senators Daniel Inouye (D-HI) and Harry Reid sponsored the July 2008 supplemental-appropriations language directing DIA to investigate foreign advanced aerospace threats over a 40-year horizon, and it presents Reid’s interest in unconventional aerospace technologies as part of the background for the engagement. It further notes that the request for proposals had sought work across 12 advanced technical areas, that Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies remained the sole bidder and contract awardee, and that Program Management Plans for all 12 areas had been approved. The talking points also state that approximately two research reports per technical area were expected by the end of July 2009 and that planning had begun for a prospective Fiscal Year 2010 university grant program in advanced aerospace research.
