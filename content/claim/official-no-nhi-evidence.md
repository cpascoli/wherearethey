The official counter-position in the debate: that the government offices charged with investigating UAP, from Project Blue Book to AARO, have found no verifiable evidence that any sighting involved extraterrestrial technology, and no evidence of a recovery or reverse-engineering program. It says what the investigations found, not what exists; those who dispute it argue that the evidence sits in programs AARO was never shown.
