During the USS Theodore Roosevelt's pre-deployment exercises, F/A-18 crews with newly upgraded radars reported objects almost daily, some of them near misses, and recorded the Gimbal and GoFast videos. The claim is that these were not ordinary objects. The official findings so far cover only the GoFast video, not the pattern as a whole.
