The central claim of the disclosure debate: that the United States, through government agencies and defence contractors, has recovered craft that were not made by humans, and has spent decades trying to reverse-engineer them, out of sight of Congress.

Almost everyone who makes it says they were told by others, or describes what they say they saw without producing a record; the official record so far contradicts it. The timeline below shows who has said it and how they say they know: first-hand accounts, accounts relayed from unnamed sources, and the official findings against it.
