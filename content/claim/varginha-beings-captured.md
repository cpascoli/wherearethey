The claim at the heart of the Varginha case: that after a craft came down near the city in January 1996, firefighters and military police captured two living beings, one of which was seen at the regional hospital, and that the U.S. Air Force flew the debris and beings out of Brazil.

The three women's sighting is not in dispute as an experience; what they saw, and everything that followed, is. The timeline runs from the 1996 reports and denials, through the Army's inquiry, to the 2026 retractions and the witnesses James Fox presented in Washington.
