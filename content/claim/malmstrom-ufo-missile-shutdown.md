On 16 March 1967 all ten Minuteman missiles of Echo Flight at Malmstrom Air Force Base, Montana, lost alert status within seconds; former officers say missiles at Oscar Flight went down a week later. The claim is that an object seen over the sites caused it.

The shutdown itself is documented. What caused it is the question: the launch officers and maintenance staff who say UFOs were present, against the Air Force's records and the account the Wall Street Journal attributes to AARO.
