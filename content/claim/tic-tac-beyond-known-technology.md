In November 2004 Navy pilots from USS Nimitz and radar operators on USS Princeton tracked and saw a white, wingless object off southern California. The claim is that what they saw moved in ways no aircraft, missile or drone of the time could: sudden drops of tens of thousands of feet, no visible propulsion, and acceleration beyond any known airframe.

The timeline shows who has said so and how they know, and the alternatives: an unknown U.S. or adversary program.
