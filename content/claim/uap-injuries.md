Witnesses at Rendlesham, in the Amazon and elsewhere describe burns, radiation-like symptoms and lasting illness after close encounters, and AAWSAP studied the effects of UAP on humans as one of its two tracks. The Stanford immunologist Garry Nolan says he was drawn into the subject by medical cases referred by the government.
