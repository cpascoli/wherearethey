The core of the December 2017 New York Times story: that the Pentagon ran a funded UAP program called AATIP and that Luis Elizondo led it until he resigned in 2017. The official record describes one DIA program, AAWSAP, also called AATIP, and denies both that AATIP was an official program and that Elizondo had responsibility for it.
