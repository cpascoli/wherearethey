The claim that crash retrievals recovered not only craft but their occupants: “biologics”, in David Grusch's word under oath. It runs from an FBI memo of 1950 relaying a third-hand story, through researchers' anonymous sources, to former officials testifying before Congress. None of those making it has produced a body or a record of one.
