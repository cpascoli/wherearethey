The claim that unidentified objects have repeatedly appeared over nuclear missile sites, weapons plants and storage areas, from the 1940s to the present, and in some accounts switched missiles off or on. Incursions over nuclear sites are documented in official records (such as the 2015 Pantex report); the interference accounts rest on testimony and are disputed for Malmstrom by the Air Force's own records.
