facts:
  - text: The intelligence community's counterpart to a Special Access Program. Defined in Intelligence Community Directive 906, a CAP is a top-level control system and any compartment or sub-compartment under it (examples include SI, TK and HCS). It is not a classification level but a set of access restrictions above ordinary classification. The DNI oversees CAPs for intelligence sources and methods, but not military operational programs.
