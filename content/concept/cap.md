A Controlled Access Program is the intelligence community's counterpart to the Pentagon's Special Access Program. Defined in Intelligence Community Directive 906, a CAP is a top-level control system together with any compartments under it (examples include SI, TK and HCS): not a classification level, but a set of access restrictions above ordinary classification, overseen by the Director of National Intelligence.

The term matters in UAP debates because whistleblowers allege that UAP information is held in compartments that even cleared officials and members of Congress cannot see.

*Sources: Intelligence Community Directive 906*
