A term from David Grusch's accounts for one of the most restricted kinds of special access program. It is in the archive because Grusch uses it to explain how a crash-retrieval program could be kept from most of Congress.

On Joe Rogan's podcast Grusch explains the two words: “waived means it's limited Congressional reporting” and “bigoted means it's like by name”. He says Harry Reid's 2009 request for such a program, naming Reid, Inhofe and others, made no sense for a program ostensibly studying Skinwalker Ranch; in his account AAWSAP's real purpose was to receive material. Under oath in July 2023 he said that under 10 U.S.C. 119 the Secretary of Defense can waive congressional reporting of a SAP, but that “the gang of eight is at least supposed to be notified” when a waived or “bigoted” unacknowledged SAP is created.

The waiver itself is in the statute (see Waived SAP). The “bigoted” label and what Grusch says about Reid's request rest on his account; no primary source on file confirms them.

*Sources: Joe Rogan Experience with David Grusch · July 26, 2023 hearing*
