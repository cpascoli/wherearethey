"Waived and bigoted" describes a waived special access program in which access is also limited to a named roster of individuals, the "bigot list", who have been formally cleared and briefed into the compartment. The term is used in UAP whistleblower accounts, Grusch's among them; its precise official usage has not been confirmed from a primary source here.
