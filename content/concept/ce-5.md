CE-5, or Close Encounters of the Fifth Kind, is a civilian contact practice, not an official program. Steven Greer coined the term for “human-initiated contact and communication with extraterrestrial life”: groups use meditation, visualisation, and sometimes lights or sound, to invite UAP to a chosen place. He promotes it through CSETI field trainings, his 1999 book *Extraterrestrial Contact* and a 2020 film.

Greer traces the protocols to his own experiences. In a short clip he describes meeting a being on a mountain in October 1973, and says “we created what's known as the CE-5 protocols”, which meant seeing the beings in the mind's eye and then “vectoring” them to a location. Separately, Jake Barber says a “psionic asset” can be used to summon UAP, and that “We know exactly what they're attracted to and we intentionally attract them”; he does not call this CE-5.

CE-5 falls **outside the archive's criteria**. It rests on Greer's personal account, and no government, military or scientific source has confirmed any contact.
