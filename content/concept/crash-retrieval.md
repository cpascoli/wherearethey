"Crash retrieval and reverse engineering" refers to the allegation that the United States, and possibly other nations, have recovered craft of non-human origin and run secret programmes, often called "legacy programs", to study and copy their technology. It was put before Congress most prominently by the former intelligence officer David Grusch in his July 2023 testimony, building on earlier claims about Roswell and books by former officials.

AARO's 2024 historical review found no empirical evidence that the government or private companies have reverse-engineered extraterrestrial technology, and said the companies named by interviewees denied it on the record. The claims, the people who make them and the counter-evidence are linked from this entry.

*Sources: Grusch's written statement (2023) · AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024)*
