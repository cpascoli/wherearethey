The Citizen Hearing was the largest civilian-organised attempt to stage the hearing Congress had not held since 1968. It happened only once. The panels mixed witnesses with official roles (military, government, aviation, police) with researchers and authors, including Richard Dolan, Stanton Friedman, Linda Moulton Howe, Grant Cameron, Antonio Huneeus, A. J. Gevaerd and Donald Schmitt. Only the first group meets this archive's criteria. Steven Greer also testified.

The witness list comes from the panel descriptions on the official session recordings. Statements made at the hearing were not given under oath before Congress.
