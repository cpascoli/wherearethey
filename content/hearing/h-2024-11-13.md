"Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena: Exposing the Truth" was a joint hearing of two House Oversight subcommittees on 13 November 2024, chaired by Reps. Nancy Mace and Glenn Grothman.

The witnesses were Rear Adm. Tim Gallaudet, who described a 2015 "URGENT SAFETY OF FLIGHT ISSUE" email that later vanished; Luis Elizondo, who testified that "UAP are real" and that the U.S. possesses UAP technologies; the journalist Michael Shellenberger, who delivered the "Immaculate Constellation" whistleblower report; and the former NASA official Michael Gold, who argued for scientific study free of stigma.

The Immaculate Constellation report was entered into the record; the Pentagon said it has no record of such a programme. The printed record is filed here.
