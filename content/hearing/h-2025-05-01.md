"Understanding UAP: Science, National Security & Innovation" was a nonpartisan briefing for members of Congress on 1 May 2025, hosted by the UAP Disclosure Fund and sponsored by the House Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets: a briefing, not a formal hearing, with no sworn testimony.

After opening remarks by Reps. Anna Paulina Luna, Eric Burlison and Tim Burchett (Nick Begich joined later), it had three panels, moderated by Luis Elizondo: science (Avi Loeb, Rear Adm. Tim Gallaudet and Eric Davis), national security (Chris Mellon and Kirk McConnell), and science and innovation (Michael Gold and Anna Brady-Estevez).
