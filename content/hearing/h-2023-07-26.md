"UAP: Implications on National Security, Public Safety, and Government Transparency", held on 26 July 2023 by the House Oversight Subcommittee on National Security, the Border, and Foreign Affairs, was the first hearing to hear UAP whistleblower and pilot testimony under oath.

The witnesses were Cmdr. David Fravor, who described the 2004 Nimitz "Tic Tac" encounter; Ryan Graves, who described regular encounters by his F/A-18 squadron off Virginia Beach and called UAP a national-security and aviation-safety problem; and David Grusch, who testified that he had been told of "a multi-decade UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering program" and said he could give details only in a secure setting. Members including Tim Burchett, Anna Paulina Luna and Jared Moskowitz pressed for more disclosure.
