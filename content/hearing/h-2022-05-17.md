The House Intelligence Committee's subcommittee on counterterrorism, counterintelligence and counterproliferation, chaired by Rep. André Carson, held this open hearing on 17 May 2022, the first public congressional hearing on UFOs in more than 50 years. Full committee chair Adam Schiff also spoke.

The witnesses were Ronald Moultrie, Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security, and Scott Bray, Deputy Director of Naval Intelligence. Bray reported that the UAP database had grown to about 400 reports, showed videos, and testified: "We have not had a collision. We have had at least 11 near misses." He said the task force had no material and had detected nothing to suggest anything non-terrestrial in origin.

Rep. Mike Gallagher used the hearing to have the "Admiral Wilson memo" entered into the record. The transcript is filed here.
