"Restoring Public Trust Through UAP Transparency and Whistleblower Protection" was held on 9 September 2025 by the House Oversight Committee's Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets, chaired by Rep. Anna Paulina Luna.

The witnesses were the Air Force veteran Jeffrey Nuccetelli, who described the 2003 "Red Square" incursions over missile-defence sites at Vandenberg; Navy Chief Alexandro Wiggins; the journalist George Knapp, who spoke about Bob Lazar, Area 51 and decades of official denials; the Air Force veteran Dylan Borland, who described a triangular craft at Langley in 2012; and Joe Spielberger of the Project On Government Oversight, the minority witness, on overclassification and whistleblower protection.

The record includes a letter to the Secretary of Defense about alleged reprisals against David Grusch. The transcript is filed here.
