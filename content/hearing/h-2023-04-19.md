A hearing of the Senate Armed Services Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and Capabilities, chaired by Senator Kirsten Gillibrand, on 19 April 2023, with AARO's founding director Sean Kirkpatrick as the witness.

Kirkpatrick described how AARO approaches the few cases that show potentially anomalous characteristics, testing and modelling its analyses and peer-reviewing them with government, industry and cleared academic partners, and stated for the record that AARO "has found no credible evidence thus far of extraterrestrial activity, off-world technology or objects that defy the known laws of physics."
