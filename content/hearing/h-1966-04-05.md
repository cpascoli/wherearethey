The first open congressional hearing on UFOs: a one-day session of the House Armed Services Committee on 5 April 1966, chaired by L. Mendel Rivers. It was prompted by the March 1966 Michigan sightings, which the Air Force consultant J. Allen Hynek had partly put down to "swamp gas", and by House Minority Leader Gerald Ford's call for an investigation.

The witnesses were Air Force Secretary Harold Brown, Project Blue Book chief Maj. Hector Quintanilla and Hynek, who urged a serious civilian scientific study. The record includes the Air Force Scientific Advisory Board's review of Blue Book, by a committee chaired by Brian O'Brien with Carl Sagan as a member, which recommended contracting university teams to investigate sightings.

The hearing led to the Air Force's contract with the University of Colorado, the Condon study (1966–1968), whose report was later used to justify closing Blue Book. The full printed record is filed here.
