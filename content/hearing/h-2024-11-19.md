A hearing of the Senate Armed Services Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and Capabilities on 19 November 2024, chaired by Senator Kirsten Gillibrand with Senator Joni Ernst as ranking member, with AARO's new director Jon Kosloski as the witness.

Kosloski presented case studies AARO had resolved, including the GoFast video, which AARO assessed with high confidence showed an object at about 13,000 feet rather than skimming the water, its apparent speed an effect of parallax, a 2018 drone video near an erupting Mount Etna, which he said showed an object "170 meters away from the plume" (AARO's written release the same day says a balloon about 170 kilometres from the caldera), and the 2013 Aguadilla, Puerto Rico video, assessed as two balloons or sky lanterns rather than a transmedium object. He added that AARO does not believe every object is a bird, a balloon or a drone: "we do have some very anomalous objects."
