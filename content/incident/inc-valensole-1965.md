On the morning of 1 July 1965 Maurice Masse, a lavender farmer at Valensole in Provence, said he came upon a small craft, about the size of a car, standing in his field, with two child-sized figures beside it; one pointed an object at him and he found himself unable to move until they climbed back in and the craft flew off. The gendarmerie took his statements and recorded a star-shaped mark in the ground that hardened like cement; he later said lavender would not grow back on the spot for years.

It is France’s best-known close encounter, and one of the few cases GEIPAN classifies D, unexplained, on the strength of the gendarmerie’s reports and its strangeness, though without an investigation of its own. It rests on a single witness.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 1965-07-00050, classified D (unexplained)*
