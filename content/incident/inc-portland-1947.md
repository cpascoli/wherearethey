In the early afternoon of 4 July 1947, after the Portland police received a score of calls about disks overhead, officers in at least two prowl cars, the harbor patrol and private citizens reported fast, flashing objects over the city, and sheriff's deputies across the river in Vancouver, Washington, saw a group of 20 to 30. Several of the police witnesses were World War II veterans and civilian pilots. That evening a United Air Lines crew reported disks near Boise.

The reports went into the Air Force's files. Its analyst found no astronomical explanation and offered no definite hypothesis, mentioning foil dropped during Independence Day celebrations only as an unlikely possibility. Names of witnesses are blacked out in the Blue Book copy; those above come from the newspaper report it preserves.

*Sources: Project Blue Book case file (U.S. National Archives) · Oregon Journal, 5 July 1947*
