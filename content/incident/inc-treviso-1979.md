On 18 June 1979, an Italian Air Force reconnaissance pilot was directed onto an unknown target over Treviso and photographed it with his aircraft's cameras. The Air Force's own summary table confirms only the minimum: Air Force personnel reported a black, cylindrical object at about 3,000 metres that morning, and the event remains catalogued as unidentified.

The pilot, Maresciallo Giancarlo Cecconi, was named by the Treviso press in 1984 and later gave his account to investigators of CISU, the Italian Centre for UFO Studies. In 1984 the Defence Ministry reportedly told a researcher that its photo-interpreters had identified a balloon made of black plastic bags. Yet the Air Force's 1986 extract and its current archive both list the case as unidentified, and in 1994 the Air Force said it no longer held the photographs. The ministry letter is known only from CISU's reproduction, so both conclusions are kept here side by side.

*Sources: Aeronautica Militare, Archivio OVNI 1972–1990, p. 106; Marco Orlandi, "Caso Cecconi: visto, intercettato, fotografato", UFO – Rivista di informazione ufologica (CISU) n. 17, February 1996*
