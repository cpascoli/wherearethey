Three game wardens (gardes-chasse) on a night patrol in the Haute-Saône in May 1989 watched a set of three bright lights that hovered, shot upwards in zigzags, returned and left again in silence. GEIPAN ruled out the military jet that flew over soon after and could not identify the lights.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 1989-05-01176, classified D (unexplained)*
