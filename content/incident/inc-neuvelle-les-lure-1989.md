Three game wardens (gardes-chasse) on a night surveillance round in the Haute-Saône on 30 May 1989 watched a set of three bright, projector-like lights. It is in the archive as one of GEIPAN's unexplained cases, with several trained observers on duty.

According to GEIPAN's summary, the lights gave off very bright flashes and stayed motionless for some twenty seconds. They then shot up vertically at very high speed in a zigzag, came back over the witnesses, stopped at their starting point, showed a red light in the middle and left again, changing direction several times.

GEIPAN checked the obvious explanations. A Jaguar fighter passed shortly afterwards, but it could not be confused with the silent phenomenon, and inquiries at the nearby air base could not confirm the sighting. GEIPAN's file is short, and it classifies the case D, unexplained.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 1989-05-01176, classified D (unexplained)*
