In June 2026, federal special agents at an observation post at a sensitive national security site in the western United States watched, through binoculars and a handheld thermal monocular, a silent, matte black triangle with a wingspan of roughly nine to fifteen feet rise over a ridge, hover, then bank into a canyon at high speed. A third official at a separate post first detected it on long-range thermal equipment. A second observation followed the next evening, and a ground search found no debris. The account was released in PURSUE release 07 as an AARO memorandum and an FBI interview report.

AARO says the case warrants further investigation because it matches other incidents reported in the western United States, such as the 2025 orb encounters at a western military facility, but it reaches no conclusion about what the object was. The two records differ on detail: the agents' own statement compares it to a delta-wing drone and puts the sighting at about three seconds, while AARO's debrief describes a ten-second climb and a twenty-second hover.

*Sources: AARO memorandum for record (DOW-UAP-D176) and FBI FD-302 (FBI-UAP-D043), PURSUE release 07*
