On the evening of 21 April 1991 Alitalia flight AZ284, an MD-80 registered I-DAWC flying from Milan to Heathrow with 57 people aboard, was descending through 22,000 feet over Kent when Captain Achille Zaghetti saw a light-brown, missile-like object about three metres long pass about 1,000 feet above the airliner in the opposite direction. He shouted a warning to his co-pilot, who also saw it, and asked London control whether they had anything on radar: the controller reported an unknown target 10 nautical miles behind him.

The Civil Aviation Authority investigated and confirmed a faint radar trace, the press reported a “near miss” with a missile, and the MoD, asked for help, ruled out British missiles, space debris and a weather balloon, could not identify the object, and closed the case as a UFO report. In 2008, when the files were opened, Zaghetti told Italian newspapers it had passed about 300 metres away and was definitely not a weather balloon, more like a fighter's long-range fuel tank. No video testimony from the crew has been found.

*Sources: MoD files DEFE 24/1953 (pp. 77–83: the captain's report, The Independent of 6 May 1991, the MoD minute) and DEFE 24/1955 (pp. 89–122), The National Archives · KentOnline, 20 October 2008*
