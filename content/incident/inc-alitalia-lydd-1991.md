On the evening of 21 April 1991 the crew of an Alitalia MD-80 flying from Milan to London, with 57 passengers aboard, reported a missile-like object passing close to the airliner at about 22,000 feet over Lydd in Kent. The report went to the Civil Aviation Authority, which asked the Ministry of Defence for help, and the press reported a “near miss” above an Army range.

The MoD ruled out British missiles, space debris and a weather balloon, could not identify the object, and closed the case as a UFO report. The captain's report itself is not in the released file, and the pilot is not named in it.

*Source: MoD file DEFE 24/1955 (pp. 89–91, 108–122), The National Archives*
