On the nights of 2 and 3 November 1957, drivers around Levelland on the high plains of West Texas reported a glowing object on or near the road, and their engines, headlights and radios failing as it came close; the newspapers carried more accounts than the Air Force could trace. It became one of the best-known cases of the November 1957 wave.

The Air Force's 1006th Air Intelligence Service Squadron interviewed six sources, among them Sheriff Weir Clem and a state highway patrolman, who saw only a streak or flash of light. The investigator offered the wet weather and gas flares as possible causes, rated one key witness unreliable, and forwarded the case as unresolved. The report was released in PURSUE release 08 in November 2026, with the witnesses' names.
