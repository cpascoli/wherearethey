Brazil's best-known UFO case. On 20 January 1996, in Varginha, a city in southern Minas Gerais near the Army's sergeants' school in Três Corações, three young women said they had seen a strange creature crouching by a wall. Stories followed of a craft crashing days earlier, of firefighters and soldiers capturing two beings, of one taken to the regional hospital, of a military police officer who died after touching one, and of the beings being flown out, possibly by the U.S. Air Force.

The official record contradicts it: the Army's 1997 inquiry found no military involvement and judged the creature most likely a local man, and Brazilian television still reports its conclusion as a case of mistakes and rumours. Two of the three soldiers whose anonymous testimony spread the story in the 1990s later retracted it, one saying he had been promised money. The three women have never changed their account, and in January 2026 filmmaker James Fox presented new named witnesses in Washington: Carlos de Souza, who says he saw a cigar-shaped craft come down on 13 January; the forensic examiner who performed the officer's autopsy and the pathologist who examined his tissue; the neurosurgeon Ítalo Venturelli, who says he saw a living being behind a partition at the hospital, and who had told Fox's team two years earlier only that he had seen a video; and the retired U.S. Air Force colonel Fred Claussen, relaying an air traffic controller's account of an unauthorised U.S. cargo flight to Campinas. Fox's documentary Moment of Contact (2022, updated 2026) tells the case.
