The PURSUE releases include about twenty U.S. military videos from over the United States and its coastal waters, most of them reported through Northern Command: aircraft and sensor footage off the East Coast and in the Atlantic from 2015 to 2020, the Gulf of America in 2019, the western United States, Columbus in Ohio, Eglin Air Force Base in Florida, an F/A-18 and a "fifth generation aircraft" in 2022–23, and a 2026 Army report. The oldest, from 1996, came from the Navy's UAP Task Force.

Most are short infrared clips of a single point of contrast, described by AARO without assessment. Two carry the pilots' own range-fouler reports, which pull in opposite directions: one observer with 28 years in the Air Force and Navy had never seen such flight characteristics, while another compared the object to a large deformed balloon drifting with the wind.

The Lake Huron shoot-down of February 2023 and the Coast Guard "Tic Tac" of April 2024 have their own entries.
