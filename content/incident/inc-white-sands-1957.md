On 3 November 1957, the morning after the first Levelland reports, two Army military police patrols at the White Sands Proving Ground reported bright objects: the first, at about three in the morning, a very bright egg-shaped object that came down near the old bunker area of the Stallion range; the second, that evening near Trinity Site, a pulsing light, red then white, that climbed to about 45 degrees above the horizon and stayed in sight for some twenty-five minutes.

The Air Force investigator who interviewed the soldiers, Captain Patrick O. Shere, also took the Kirtland tower report the next day. The report concludes that the first patrol saw the setting moon and the second Venus. It was released in PURSUE release 08 in November 2026, dated by the catalog to 13 November, the date of the report.

*Sources: DOW-UAP-D193 (1006th AISS report, PURSUE release 08, war.gov)*
