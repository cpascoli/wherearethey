On 12 February 2023, the third of three unidentified objects shot down over North America in as many days was brought down over Lake Huron by an Air National Guard F-16C. It came a week after the Chinese high-altitude balloon, when NORAD had tuned its radar to pick up slower, smaller objects.

The engagement itself is in the Department of War's PURSUE release 02 (PR071): 46 seconds of infrared footage in which, at the 20-second mark, AARO describes the object "fragmenting in a radial displacement pattern that suggests a high-energy event". The video was item 19 on the House Oversight Task Force's request list.

Four days later the President gave the intelligence community's assessment that all three objects were most likely private, recreational or research balloons. The release lists the Lake Huron video among unresolved cases.
