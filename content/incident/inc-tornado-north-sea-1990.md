On the evening of 5 November 1990 RAF Tornado GR1 crews flying between the UK and RAF Laarbruch in Germany reported a large aircraft-shaped object with white and blue lights that overtook a Tornado travelling at Mach 0.8 as it left UK airspace for Dutch control. The crew's signal reached the MoD the next day; six aircrew in all are said to have seen it.

Dutch military radar reported nothing, the crews took no radar lock, and the MoD did not investigate because the sighting was outside UK airspace. In 2002 a member of the public suggested the re-entry of a Russian rocket booster logged by RAF Fylingdales, and the MoD asked the RAF to check; the pilot, writing in 2003, rejected the idea. The pilot, who asked to remain anonymous, is not named in the files.

*Sources: MoD files DEFE 24/2041 (pp. 10–11, 108) and DEFE 24/2027 (pp. 34–36), The National Archives*
