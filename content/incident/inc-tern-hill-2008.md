On the night of 7 June 2008 soldiers of the Royal Irish Regiment on duty at Tern Hill barracks in Shropshire filmed a formation of coloured lights on a mobile phone. The footage reached The Sun, which reported the story later that month; the MoD obtained the video through the Army.

The UFO desk found the square shapes were pixels from the zoomed phone camera and the lights slow-moving, and a nearby hotel said it had released Chinese lanterns at a wedding that night. The case is typical of the lantern sightings that swelled the MoD's last two years of reports.

*Source: MoD file DEFE 24/2625 (pp. 107–112), The National Archives (opened June 2013)*
