Before dawn on 23 January 1982 two gendarmes of the motorway patrol, driving the A10 south of Paris, watched a pale luminous mass in the sky that lit up pylons, sheds and trees along their route for about 30 km, while they drove and when they stopped. The nearby air base had nothing flying and its radar switched off.

GEIPAN, re-examining the case decades later, found the lighting too focused and too far below the cloud base for a helicopter searchlight, and the case consistent enough to class D, unexplained. It rests on the gendarmes’ report; no field reconstruction is now possible.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 1982-01-00913, classified D (unexplained)*
