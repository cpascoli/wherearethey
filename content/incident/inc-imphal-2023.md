On the afternoon of 19 November 2023, the CISF security force at Imphal's Bir Tikendrajit International Airport, in Manipur, reported a small white object hovering above the control tower. Air traffic control suspended landings and take-offs for nearly three hours: two inbound IndiGo flights were diverted and three departures held. The object was visible to people on the ground until about 16:05, shortly before sunset, and then disappeared.

The Indian Air Force's Eastern Command said it had activated its air-defence response on the basis of the airport's visual reports, and that the object was not seen afterwards. According to defence sources quoted by ANI, two Rafale fighters searched the area in turn and found nothing.

What the object was has never been officially stated. The government's answer in Parliament gives the timeline and the disruption but no identification, and no radar or sensor data have been released, so nothing on record supports unusual performance. The press reported a drone as the likely explanation, but only from unnamed sources, so the case stays open.
