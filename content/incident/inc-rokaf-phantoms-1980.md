On the night of 31 March 1980, during the Korea–U.S. Team Spirit exercise, two Republic of Korea Air Force F-4 Phantoms returning to base over North Gyeongsang province saw a very bright light low in the sky. Its four crew, led by Lt. Col. Lee Seung-bae with Col. Lim Byeong-seon flying the second aircraft, say it darted towards Yeongil Bay and stopped, was invisible to both ground and airborne radar, and let the jets circle it at close range for about fifteen minutes, an oval or disc with coloured lights and a brilliant flash at its centre, before it left over the East Sea. They say they watched it for about forty minutes in all.

Both pilots are on record: Lee in a Monthly Chosun interview of 1992 and a report he wrote in 2016, Lim in a JTBC documentary of 2016 and later interviews. The case rests on their testimony; no Korean Air Force record has been released, and the accounts differ on the date, the object's size and the flight's route.
