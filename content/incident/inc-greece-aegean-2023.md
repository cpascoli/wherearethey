Three U.S. military mission reports and three matching sensor videos released in PURSUE describe unidentified objects in the waters off Greece between October 2023 and January 2024. The reports are titled "Greece"; the catalog places the October cases in the Aegean Sea and the January case in the Mediterranean, and AARO's descriptions say the videos were submitted by U.S. Central Command.

On 27 and 29 October 2023 operators over the Aegean reported small objects skimming just above the sea, one making repeated 90-degree turns at an estimated 80 mph and another heading straight for the coast; the infrared clips show the sensor tracking a spot of contrast until it is lost against the background or the land. On 25 January 2024 an operator reported a diamond-shaped object with something hanging beneath it, seen only in short-wave infrared — in the video, AARO describes an inverted teardrop with a trailing mass suspended below.

The records are unresolved cases with no performance measurements; speeds and shapes are the operators' impressions.

*Sources: DOW-UAP-D025, D033, D035 (local copies), PR028, PR034, PR035 (PURSUE)*
