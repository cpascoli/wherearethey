Two sightings in 1950 by RAF officers at the Royal Aircraft Establishment, Farnborough, the home of British test flying. On 14 August Flight Lieutenant Hubbard, an experienced pilot, saw a pearl-grey disc pass overhead making S-turns at high speed; on 5 September he and five other officers on the watch-tower watched a similar disc manoeuvre at long range over the Guildford–Farnham area.

Members of the Ministry of Defence's Flying Saucer Working Party interviewed the officers. They did not doubt their honesty but found it “impossible to believe” that an unconventional aircraft could cross so populous an area unnoticed by others, and attributed both sightings to illusion or ordinary aircraft.

*Source: Flying Saucer Working Party report, DEFE 44/119 (1951)*
