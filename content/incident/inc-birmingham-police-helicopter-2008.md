On the night of 2 May 2008 a West Midlands police helicopter on surveillance over the north-west of Birmingham was circled at close range by two blue-green lights; the pilot manoeuvred to avoid a collision and the lights followed. The pilot thought someone might have flown a lit radio-controlled model at them deliberately.

The UK Airprox Board, which investigates near-misses, traced every likely source and found none, accepted the experienced crew's account in full and recorded the cause as an apparent conflict with an unidentified aircraft. A copy of its report reached the MoD's UFO desk after the press reported the case in November 2008.

*Source: UK Airprox Board report 055/08, in MoD file DEFE 24/2450 (pp. 41–45), The National Archives (opened June 2013)*
