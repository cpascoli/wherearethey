On the night of 19–20 May 1986, air traffic controllers at São José dos Campos and São Paulo reported moving coloured lights and radar contacts over southeastern Brazil. The Brazilian Air Force scrambled interceptors from Santa Cruz and Anápolis. Several pilots reported lights or airborne radar contacts that kept their distance, and one target pulled away from a supersonic interceptor. On 23 May the Minister of Aeronautics, Brig. Octávio Júlio Moreira Lima, held a press conference about it, attended by five of the pilots and the flight controllers, and the episode became known as the "Official UFO Night".

The Air Defence Command's confidential report, now held by Brazil's National Archives, concluded that the phenomena were "solid and reflect a certain intelligence". It also documents the weakness of its own data: radars designed for air traffic control, manual tracking, and contacts often too faint to form tracks. No single explanation has been established; individual lights and radar contacts need to be assessed separately.

*Sources: Comando de Defesa Aérea, report of 2 June 1986 (Arquivo Nacional) · [Government of Brazil (2022)](https://www.gov.br/en/government-of-brazil/latest-news/2022/official-ufo-night-in-brazil)*
