On the evening of 3 October 1978, Hideichi Amano, a 29-year-old snack-bar owner and ham-radio enthusiast, was parked on a hilltop above Sayama, Saitama Prefecture, with his two-year-old daughter Juri. He said a bright light filled the car, the engine and headlights failed, and his daughter lay on the back seat as if in a trance, and a small humanoid figure with a round face, pointed ears and glowing eyes appeared beside him for several minutes, pressing a pipe-like object to his temple and emitting high-pitched sounds. Afterwards his watch was found stopped at 9.57 p.m.

Amano reported the encounter to the police, who did not believe him. The story first reached the public through a Japanese television programme in November 1978, and the investigator Jun-Ichi Takanashi's account appeared in the *MUFON UFO Journal* and *Flying Saucer Review* in 1978–79. Later retellings, following the writer Jenny Randles, add that under hypnosis Amano described being given an object he took to be a bracelet for contacting the being.

The case rests on Amano's testimony alone; there is no physical evidence or independent witness.
