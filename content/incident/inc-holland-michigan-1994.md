On the night of 8 March 1994, residents of Holland, on the Lake Michigan shore, made a number of 911 calls about moving lights. Holland police officer Jeff Velthouse saw coloured, oscillating lights through binoculars, and National Weather Service meteorologist Jack Bushong in Muskegon, who was on the phone with dispatch, said he was tracking three large radar returns in a shifting triangle over the lake.

The police report records the observations and Bushong's account but no identification.

*Sources: Holland Police Department report 940308035 (via WOOD TV8, 2021)*
