Late on Friday 13 December 2024, during a wave of drone sightings in the U.S. Northeast, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base near Dayton, Ohio, closed its airspace for about four hours after small unmanned aerial systems were seen over and around the base. A tower recording obtained by The War Zone warns a medical transport flight of "heavy UAS movement on the base".

The base's public affairs office confirmed the sightings and said the drones did not affect base residents, facilities or assets. No origin was announced. Like the Langley incursions of December 2023, the episode raised questions about the defence of U.S. bases against small drones. The base described them only as small unmanned aerial systems.

The first official record came in November 2026, when the FBI released three reports by the Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI) to The Black Vault. They cover sightings on 11–12, 13–14 and 16–17 December: small four-rotor drones, some in synchronized formations of four, seen by Security Forces patrols and by AFOSI's own agents, with air traffic control closing the airspace at 2230 on the 13th. One patrol reported radios that would not transmit while the drones were near. The reports also record a sighting found to be mistaken and, on the 16th, air traffic control and airfield management seeing nothing. They call the craft small drones throughout, offer no explanation, and say the operators were not identified.
