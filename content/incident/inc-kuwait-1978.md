Between 9 November and 14 December 1978, Kuwait recorded eight UFO sightings, several of them near a Kuwait Oil Company gathering centre in the northern oil fields. The government appointed a committee of experts from the Kuwait Institute for Scientific Research; its report, released on 20 January 1979, ruled out espionage devices, did not rule out an extraterrestrial origin, and urged the government to protect the country's airspace and oil resources. Its release coincided with front-page stories, with photographs, of a new sighting over Kuwait City on 21 January.

The U.S. Embassy reported all this to Washington on 29 January, adding an account it had from a senior oil company official: at the first appearance over the northern fields, the automatic pumping equipment shut down and restarted by itself when the object vanished, something the KISR report did not address. A second cable that day explained why the air defence radars had seen nothing: they were run only in the mornings and were off. No explanation for the sightings is on record; the committee's report and the Kuwaiti press coverage are known here only through the embassy's summary.

*Sources: U.S. Embassy Kuwait telegrams KUWAIT 0486 and KUWAIT 0508 (29 January 1979), NARA Central Foreign Policy Files (AAD)*
