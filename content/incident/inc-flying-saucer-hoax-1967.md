On the morning of 4 September 1967 six small saucer-shaped objects were found in a straight line across southern England, from the Isle of Sheppey in Kent to Clevedon on the Bristol Channel. For a few hours the discovery was treated as a possible real event: police cordoned off the sites, bomb disposal teams opened some of the saucers, one was flown by helicopter to RAF Manston, and the MoD sent its UFO investigator to Bromley.

The saucers were a rag-week stunt by engineering students at Farnborough Technical College. The case surfaced in the MoD files thirty years later, when a television producer researching it asked the ministry for help.

*Sources: The National Archives' highlights guide (March 2011) · MoD file DEFE 24/1986 (pp. 305–311)*
