At about 23:00 on 11 November 1979, the crew of TAE flight 297, a Supercaravelle carrying 109 passengers from Salzburg to the Canary Islands, saw red lights that seemed to approach the aircraft at collision speed and then follow it. Radar stations in Madrid and Barcelona could offer no explanation, and Capt. Francisco Javier Lerdo de Tejada diverted to Valencia-Manises, often described as the first emergency landing of a commercial flight because of a UFO.

A Spanish Air Force Mirage F1 flown by Capt. Fernando Cámara was scrambled from Los Llanos Air Base; he reported pursuing a bright, colour-changing object at up to Mach 1.4, with his avionics apparently jammed.

The Spanish Air Force file, later declassified, concluded the objects were of unknown nature and may have been optical illusions by the crews. Researchers such as Juan Antonio Fernández Peris have attributed the lights to industrial flare stacks and bright stars, explanations the witnesses reject.
