  - text: Transportes Aéreos Españoles flight 297 (Salzburg to Tenerife, 109 passengers), captained by Francisco Javier Lerdo de Tejada, diverted to Valencia-Manises after the crew saw red lights that seemed to approach and follow the aircraft. A Spanish Air Force Mirage F1 was scrambled from Los Llanos to intercept.
    status: documented
  - text: The Spanish Air Force file, declassified in 1994 and published in its virtual library, concluded the objects were of unknown nature and may have been optical illusions by the crews. Later researchers proposed bright stars and refinery flare stacks as explanations.
