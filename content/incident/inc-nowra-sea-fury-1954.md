On the night of 31 August 1954, Royal Australian Navy pilot Lt J. A. O'Farrell was returning to the naval air station at Nowra, New South Wales, in a Hawker Sea Fury when he noticed one, then a second, bright light flying near his aircraft. He radioed Nowra, which confirmed that the objects were showing on radar; they then departed to the north-east at high speed.

O'Farrell's detailed report and the radar operator's confirmation survive in the RAAF's UFO records at the National Archives of Australia, which says the nature of the objects "still remains a mystery".

*Sources: National Archives of Australia (2018)*
