In December 2018 a forward-looking infrared sensor aboard an uncrewed U.S. Air Force aircraft over the Mediterranean recorded an object that appeared to fly through the plume of superheated gas and ash from an eruption of Mount Etna in Sicily. AARO, working with intelligence-community, scientific and international partners and a volcanologist, used full-motion video analysis, 3D modelling and pixel-level examination, and concluded with moderate confidence that the footage shows a balloon drifting with the wind about 170 kilometres from the caldera; the intense atmospheric conditions near the volcano distorted the image so that it seemed to cross the plume.

Jon Kosloski presented the case at the Senate hearing of 19 November 2024, where he said "170 meters"; AARO's published release, posted the same day, says kilometres.
