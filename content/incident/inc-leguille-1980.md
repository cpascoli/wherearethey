Three people near Royan on the Atlantic coast watched a dazzling orange light move silently under low cloud for three-quarters of an hour in March 1980, and reported it to the gendarmerie, which documented the case with sketches and photographs. GEIPAN tested two searchlight explanations and found neither fitted the brightness and colour described.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 1980-03-00751, classified D (unexplained)*
