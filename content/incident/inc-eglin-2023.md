On 26 January 2023, a military pilot on a mission over the Eglin Air Force Base training range in the Gulf of Mexico reported four objects on radar, apparently flying in formation. Approaching one, the pilot saw a rounded, paneled object and took two infrared and electro-optical still images; the aircraft's radar failed during the approach, and its video recorder was already out of action.

The case reached Congress through a protected disclosure to Rep. Matt Gaetz, whose district includes Eglin. Gaetz, Tim Burchett and Anna Paulina Luna say they were initially refused access, then saw the image and met one crew member. At the July 2023 House Oversight hearing Gaetz described a diamond formation of four craft and an "orb" that disabled the pilot's sensors, called the image impossible to attribute to any human capability, and urged that the evidence be subpoenaed. Luna has said the experience led to her declassification task force.

In October 2023 AARO published a resolution of a case with the same date and details: the object was very likely a balloon, possibly a large commercial lighting balloon with black and white hemispheres. AARO found the radar failure coincidental and the video equipment already broken. Two partner agencies independently agreed it was not anomalous. The three other radar objects were never analysed.
