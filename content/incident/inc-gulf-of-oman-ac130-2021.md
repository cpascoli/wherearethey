On the night of 8 September 2021, a U.S. special-operations AC-130 gunship was running a live-fire training sortie over the Gulf of Oman. After dropping a water-activated flare as a mock target, the combat systems officer swung the infrared sensor back to the flare and saw two round, "cold" objects about four feet across hovering just above the water beside it, which then sped away without changing altitude.

Over the rest of the sortie the crew logged about 25 similar sightings in infrared, estimating speeds from 250 to 1,300 mph, and reported the objects flying in formations and seeming to react when the gunship's weapons were used. The aircraft's video recorder failed, so the clips released with the report are secondary recordings that limit any analysis.

The report is an unevaluated intelligence information report; it warns that the descriptions are the crew's subjective impressions. It was released in PURSUE in 2026 and is one of many U.S. military reports from the CENTCOM region.
