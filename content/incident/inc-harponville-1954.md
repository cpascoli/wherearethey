A daylight landing report from the great French UFO wave of autumn 1954: two workmen on bicycles in the Somme saw a dark domed object on the ground in a field, which rose silently with a trail of smoke as they approached. They reported it to the gendarmerie at once, which found no traces. GEIPAN classifies it D.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 1954-09-00008, classified D (unexplained)*
