In the October 1954 French UFO wave, soldiers on night duty at the radar station of Doullens in the Somme watched a coloured light emerge from low cloud, stop dead overhead for several minutes and leave at high speed; the radar was not running that night. Three military witnesses gave accounts that differ in detail but agree on the stop and the speed. GEIPAN classifies the case D: strange, of medium consistency, though old.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 1954-10-09217, classified D (unexplained)*
