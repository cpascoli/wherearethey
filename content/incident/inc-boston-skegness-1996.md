In the early hours of 5 October 1996 police officers in Skegness and Boston, and the crew of a tanker in the Wash, watched bright, stationary, multicoloured flashing lights until dawn, while a stationary plot appeared on a civil radar over Boston. The coastguard passed the reports from agency to agency, the press reported a radar-confirmed UFO, and Labour MP Martin Redmond asked why no RAF aircraft had been scrambled.

An RAF Wing Commander spent eight working days on the case, interviewing the observers and consulting the Royal Greenwich Observatory. He traced the radar plot to a permanent echo, almost certainly the Boston Stump church spire, and the lights seen from land to Venus rising, but left the tanker crew's two sets of lights unexplained.

*Source: MoD file DEFE 24/2032 (pp. 419–450), The National Archives (opened March 2011)*
