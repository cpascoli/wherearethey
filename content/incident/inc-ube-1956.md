On the afternoon of 17 December 1956, a USAF F-86D pilot, practising intercepts with his wingman near Ube in western Japan, got a radar contact larger than his wingman's, closed to within five nautical miles without gaining further and, by his account, saw it on and off for three to four minutes; its radar return then left his scope at an estimated 1,500 to 1,800 knots. No ground radar or second aircraft recorded it. Air Technical Intelligence Liaison Officers debriefed the pilots and drew no conclusion.

The report was released in PURSUE release 08 in November 2026.

*Sources: DOW-UAP-D205 (FEAF ATILO report, PURSUE release 08, war.gov)*
