On the afternoon of 3 June 1967, two Spanish Air Force pilots flying a T-33 from Torrejón to Talavera saw a brilliant object hanging some 5,000 feet above them over Montánchez. They tried and failed to reach it. Two air-defence radar stations picked it up, and two F-86 Sabres were scrambled from Torrejón with cameras. Every crew saw an object that seemed to change shape with the viewing angle, and most thought it might be a balloon. Only one poor photograph survives.

When the file was declassified in 1992, the Air Force's intelligence section said the radar returns proved a real, slow-moving solid object. It judged a sounding balloon the likeliest explanation, while noting that no one had checked whether a balloon had been launched. The pilots' names are blanked in the released file.
