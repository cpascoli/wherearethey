Japan's best-known close encounter. On the evening of 23 February 1975 two eight-year-old cousins in Kōfu, Yamanashi Prefecture, reported that an orange object chased them, landed in a vineyard, and released a small, wrinkled, faceless figure that tapped one of them on the shoulder. Family members and a local caretaker described lights and a rotating silver object in the sky the same evening, and an investigation with the regional newspaper found broken concrete posts, flattened netting and marks in the vineyard. The case became national news.

The evidence is the children's account, the adults' sightings and the physical traces; no official investigation is on record, and sceptics point to undamaged vines and the impossible fit of the object between the posts. In February 2025, for the 50th anniversary, one of the former boys, who had long stopped giving interviews, spoke again on television and attended a commemorative event in Kōfu. The witnesses, private individuals and children at the time, are not named here.
