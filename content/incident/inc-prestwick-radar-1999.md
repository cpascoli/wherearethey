At 10.25 am on 15 February 1999 the senior air traffic control officer at Prestwick, the Scottish and Oceanic control centre, watched a primary radar contact he put at ten miles wide and two miles long travel from the Turnberry beacon south-west towards Belfast, covering some fifty miles in about a minute, several times the speed of any aircraft. He reported it within the hour through the military air information service, and the MoD treated it, with a Derbyshire police report of the same week, as a credible-witness case.

The air defence staff checked their own activity and had the recordings of three radars examined, at Buchan, West Drayton and Prestwick itself. None showed traffic matching the report, and the case was closed as of no further air defence interest. The file does not say what the Prestwick display had shown or why the recordings differed from it.

*Source: MoD file DEFE 24/2008, pp. 274–276, 298–301 (The National Archives, opened February 2010)*
