On 12 July 2022 a U.S. MQ-9 Reaper drone filmed a small, apparently spherical object passing through its camera's field of view over the Middle East. The Pentagon released the redacted clip, titled "Middle East Object", on 19 April 2023, the day AARO director Sean Kirkpatrick played it at a Senate Armed Services subcommittee hearing as an example of the round objects that make up most of AARO's reports.

Kirkpatrick was candid about its limits: the short video was essentially all the data on the event, and it would be virtually impossible to identify the object from it. The case stayed unresolved for lack of data, with no claim of anomalous performance.

In October 2023 the open-source investigators at Bellingcat matched the terrain to an area northeast of Deir ez-Zor in Syria and, using buildings in the frame for scale, put the object at no more than about 43 centimetres across — consistent with a balloon. The apparent speed, they argued, is a parallax effect of the moving drone.
