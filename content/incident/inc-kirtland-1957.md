Late on 4 November 1957, at the height of the wave that began with Levelland, the two air-traffic controllers on duty in the tower at Kirtland Air Force Base in Albuquerque watched a light descend near the runways. Through binoculars it looked like an automobile standing on end, with a white light at its base. It moved slowly past the base's floodlit “Drumhead” restricted area and then climbed away steeply. The base radar showed an unidentified return that looked like an ordinary aircraft.

The Air Force investigator rated the two controllers competent and reliable, and suggested an unidentified aircraft confused by the runways. The report was released in PURSUE release 08 in November 2026, with the controllers' names.

*Sources: DOW-UAP-D195 (1006th AISS report, PURSUE release 08, war.gov)*
