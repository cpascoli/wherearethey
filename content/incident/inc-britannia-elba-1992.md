In the spring of 1992 the crew of a Britannia Airways Boeing 737-300 reported a near miss near the island of Elba with an object they judged to be travelling at Mach 3. The Civil Aviation Authority thought the description characteristic of a radar-guided missile and offered, through the Department of Transport, to take it up with the Italian authorities if the Ministry of Defence agreed.

The MoD's UFO desk received the letter in error and sent it on to Defence Intelligence's plans staff, keeping a copy for DI55 because of its likeness to the Alitalia case of April 1991. The pilots' report itself and any answer are not in the file, so the date of the encounter and what, if anything, was done about it are unknown.

*Source: MoD file DEFE 31/181, pp. 351–352 (The National Archives, opened March 2009)*
