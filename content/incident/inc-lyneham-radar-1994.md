A night shortly before Christmas in the mid-1990s at RAF Lyneham, the RAF's Hercules base: the approach controller saw a large stationary return on his radar near the runway centreline, steered two C-130s around it, and watched it break up and scatter at great speed; soon afterwards two airmen on guard rang air traffic control to describe a bright light bursting apart in the same way.

The account comes from the controller's own statement, written years later and attached to Freedom of Information requests in 2008–09; his original report to the UFO desk has not been found among the released papers. The controller and the airmen are not named in the files.

*Source: MoD file DEFE 24/2454 (pp. 155–160), The National Archives (opened June 2013)*
