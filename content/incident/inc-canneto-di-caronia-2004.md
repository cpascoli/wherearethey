Fires that broke out in homes, appliances and cars in Canneto, a hamlet of Caronia on Sicily's Tyrrhenian coast, from 2004 onwards. A court has since found that the fires of 2014 were set by hand: in 2022 the Tribunale di Patti convicted a local resident at first instance of arson and fraud, and his father of complicity in one fire and of fraud to obtain public aid. The press reports that the magistracy had already archived the first series, in 2007, as deliberate and human.

The case belongs here because of what the government's own study group said in between. A Prime Minister's ordinance of April 2005 set it up to find the cause of the "anomalous electrical phenomena", with the Navy and the Air Force. According to L'Espresso, its confidential report of 2007 blamed high-power electromagnetic pulses from an unknown source at sea, ranked secret military technology and UFOs as candidates, and gathered local UFO sightings. The report itself has not been published, and we have not seen it.

*Sources: OPCM 29 April 2005 no. 3428 (Gazzetta Ufficiale no. 107, 10 May 2005) · L'Espresso, 25 October 2007 · LiveSicilia, 11 September 2008 · Guida Sicilia on the 2015 arrest and Gazzetta del Sud on the 2022 judgment*
