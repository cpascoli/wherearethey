In the fall of 1976 Capt. Bruce Fenstermacher was on alert as combat crew commander in the Romeo launch control facility of the 400th Strategic Missile Squadron at F.E. Warren Air Force Base, Wyoming, when his security controller reported a large, silent, pulsating white object, shaped like a fat cigar with red and blue lights, directly above the facility. Over the next hour and a half it moved from one launch facility to another along the access road, while the security alert team sent to investigate never reached it, and finally rose until it looked like a star and vanished.

Fenstermacher says the SAC command post treated the reports as a joke until he forced it to log them, that a neighbouring crew had seen the same thing and refused to report it, and that his notes were later destroyed and he was told the incident had officially never happened. The missiles were not affected. His account, given at the 2010 National Press Club conference and the 2013 Citizen Hearing, is the only source; no record of the event has been released.

*Sources: National Press Club (2010) · Citizen Hearing (2013)*
