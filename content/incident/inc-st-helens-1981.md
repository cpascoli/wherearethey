Early on 17 March 1981 a St. Helens, Oregon, police sergeant and two Oregon State Police officers watched a light over the Columbia River for about 40 minutes, and taped their radio exchange with a CB operator across the river. The Center for UFO Studies interviewed the officers and analysed the tape. No official explanation was given.

*Sources: National Enquirer (Joe Mullins), 1981 · Greg Long, “Columbia River UFO 3/17/1981” (Pacific Harbor Publishing, 1996), via [sacred-texts.com](https://www.sacred-texts.com/ufo/columbia.htm)*
