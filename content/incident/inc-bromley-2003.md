In the early hours of 17 November 2003 a family in Bromley filmed a group of twenty to thirty red flashing lights moving across the sky with a whirring sound and called the police. The officer who attended, and a police helicopter crew in the air at the time, also saw the lights; the officer judged them faster than any aircraft, the helicopter crew suggested aircraft on the approach to Heathrow, which military controllers ruled out.

Because police officers had seen it, the MoD's UFO desk asked air defence staff to check the radar; RAF Neatishead found nothing but routine traffic, and the case was closed as of no defence interest. The witnesses are not named in the files.

*Source: MoD file DEFE 24/2036 (pp. 176–179), The National Archives (opened March 2011)*
