The U.S. Africa Command area contributes only a few records to the PURSUE releases: three brief infrared clips in which a point of contrast crosses the sensor's view. Two, dated 16 and 18 October 2020, come from the collection AARO found on a classified network, where chain of custody is often unestablished; the third, two seconds long, was submitted by AFRICOM from Djibouti in 2025 with no description from the reporter.

A mission report titled "Djibouti, 2025" (DOW-UAP-D008), describing two "white hot" UAP at about 240 knots, is not included here: its grid reference places the sighting in the eastern Mediterranean north of Egypt, as the catalog's own location says.

*Sources: DOW-UAP-PR043, PR081, PR082 · DOW-UAP-D008 (local copy)*
