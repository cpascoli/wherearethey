In November 2004, radar operators on the cruiser USS Princeton, part of the USS Nimitz carrier strike group training off southern California, tracked anomalous objects for about two weeks. On 14 November, F/A-18F Super Hornets led by Cmdr. David Fravor, with Lt. Cmdr. Alex Dietrich flying a second jet, were vectored to one of the contacts. The four aircrew watched a white, wingless object about 40 feet long, shaped like a Tic Tac, over a disturbance in the water for roughly five minutes; Fravor says it mirrored his descent and then accelerated out of sight.

Later that day Lt. Chad Underwood filmed an object with his jet's infrared targeting pod, after Fravor says the jet's radar showed signs of being jammed. The "FLIR1" video was published by *The New York Times* in December 2017, alongside the first reporting on the Pentagon's AATIP, and officially released by the Department of Defense on 27 April 2020, with the objects in the videos still "characterized as 'unidentified.'" Other members of the strike group, including Princeton radar operators Kevin Day and Gary Voorhis, Jason Turner, P. J. Hughes and Ryan Weigelt, have described related observations. Fravor says the incident was never investigated at the time until Jay Stratton, for the DIA's AAWSAP program, contacted him in 2009, and that the tapes were never seized. Hughes says the Hawkeye's data bricks were taken by his commanding officer and two men in Air Force uniforms and never came back. The two accounts stand side by side.

The official position is that the case is open. Asked under oath in May 2022, Deputy Director of Naval Intelligence Scott Bray cited Nimitz as the case that "simply remains unresolved", while saying he could not point to an object that was definitively not man-made; AARO has published no resolution. Tim Gallaudet says AARO suggested in a meeting that the Tic Tac was American technology, and that Sean Kirkpatrick, AARO's former director, called it a DARPA experiment in a talk, a line Gallaudet, Fravor and Dietrich reject and Kirkpatrick has not stated on the record. Marik von Rennenkampff keeps a 5–10% chance that it was U.S. technology because of the pitot-tube-like protrusions the pilots describe; Fravor doubts a secret of that size could be kept for 16 years. Sceptics such as Mick West suggest the video shows a distant aircraft distorted by the sensor and that parallax and instrument limits explain the apparent behaviour; Fravor rejects this from the original tapes. He testified about the encounter under oath to the House Oversight Committee in July 2023.
