A reported abduction of four art students, Jim and Jack Weiner, Charlie Foltz and Chuck Rak, on a canoe trip in Maine's Allagash wilderness in August 1976. It is in the archive as a well-known case told on camera by two of the four; none of them is an official witness, and nothing on file corroborates it.

In a 2026 interview, Weiner and Foltz describe a flashlight signal at Eagle Lake that brought a glowing sphere toward their canoe. Their next memory is the shore, with a fire built to burn about three hours already out. The next day a ranger suggested they stop smoking whatever they had smoked. The case resurfaced in the late 1980s, when a neurologist treating Jim Weiner's temporal lobe epilepsy pointed him to John Mack and Raymond Fowler. Under Fowler, all four underwent separate hypnotic regressions that describe examinations by small grey beings; Weiner says small details differed between them.

The account rests on the witnesses and on hypnosis. Fowler wrote it up as *The Allagash Abductions* (1993). No official record of the case has been located in the archive.

*Sources: The Allagash abduction: Jim Weiner & Charlie Foltz (American Alchemy, 2026)*
