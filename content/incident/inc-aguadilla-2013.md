In April 2013 the infrared camera of a U.S. Customs and Border Protection aircraft filmed an object near Rafael Hernández Airport in Aguadilla, Puerto Rico, that seemed to travel at high speed, split in two and plunge into the sea and back out. Long cited as a transmedium case, it was presented by AARO's director Jon Kosloski at the Senate hearing of 19 November 2024 as resolved.

AARO concluded with high confidence that the video shows two separate objects close together, most likely a pair of balloons or sky lanterns drifting at about seven knots and descending over the airport, with no anomalous performance. The split was an effect of the changing angle of view, the speed was parallax, and the apparent entry into the water was the point where the objects reached the temperature of the sea and the camera could no longer tell them apart.
