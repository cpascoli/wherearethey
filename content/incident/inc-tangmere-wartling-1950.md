On 1 June 1950 the pilot of an RAF Meteor reported a circular, bright metallic object passing him at very high speed over the Portsmouth area, and the radar station at Wartling in Sussex logged an unusual fast-moving response at about the same time. It is one of three cases reported by RAF officers that the Ministry of Defence's Flying Saucer Working Party investigated.

The working party found the two reports ten minutes apart, explained the radar return as interference from another transmitter and the sighting as most probably a balloon, and used the case to support its conclusion that no further investigation was needed. The pilot is not named in the report.

*Source: Flying Saucer Working Party report, DEFE 44/119 (1951)*
