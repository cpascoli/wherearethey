On the afternoon of 2 July 2008 an unidentified object was reported hovering at high altitude directly over Harare International Airport in Zimbabwe. A CIA information report disseminated the next day — "not finally evaluated intelligence" — says it was observed possibly by both radar and optical means, and relays the observers' description of a disc-like object with a hollow centre and rotating lights underneath, "beams" coming from it, and a rapid climb out of sight after its lights changed colour.

The report notes that those aware of the incident debated whether it was a foreign reconnaissance device or something extraterrestrial, and that, regardless of its origin, the sighting fed into a decision to place a Zimbabwean unit on high alert amid what the report calls perceived aggressive foreign posturing. The observers and the unit are redacted. The cable was approved for release under Section 1842 of the FY2024 National Defense Authorization Act and published in the third PURSUE release (June 2026).

The report records what was relayed, not an analysis: there is no published radar data, imagery or follow-up assessment.
