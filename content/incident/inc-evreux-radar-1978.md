On the evening of 23 April 1978 the duty officer of the French Air Force base at Évreux and the staff of its control tower watched a red light in the north-western sky, and the base radar picked up a weak echo in the same direction for seven minutes. The French space agency’s UFO office investigated and could not identify it; the echo is weak and unconfirmed by a second radar, which keeps the case at D1.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 1978-04-01879, classified D (unexplained)*
