The clearest official evidence of UAP activity around China, Japan and Korea comes not from those governments but from U.S. military sensors operating in the region. Open each linked record for the official description.

Most are short infrared videos that AARO describes only as an “area of contrast” tracked by the sensor, with no assessment. Some came from user-uploaded clips on a classified network, found in answer to a 2026 congressional request. Kevin Campbell, a former DISA contractor who monitored the drone feeds, describes small spheres skimming the water there in 2023–24 and believes one of his sightings is among the requested videos; no record confirms which.

*Sources: PURSUE records (Department of War, 2026) · Americans for Safe Aerospace interview with Kevin Campbell (2026)*
