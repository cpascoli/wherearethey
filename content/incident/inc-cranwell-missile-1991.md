On 10 September 1991 a qualified flying instructor and his student, flying a Tucano trainer from RAF Cranwell at 20,000 feet, both saw what they took for a missile shoot up from about five miles away to 25,000 feet, where it appeared to explode. The RAF's duty staff logged it as a possible missile sighting: the military air traffic organisation was to examine its radar tapes, and civil and RAF police opened enquiries.

The one page on the case in the Defence Intelligence files is that daily summary, copied to DI55 with a note that it had been discussed with the UFO desk. No result of the radar check or the police enquiries is on file.

*Source: MoD file DEFE 31/180, p. 148 (The National Archives, opened March 2009)*
