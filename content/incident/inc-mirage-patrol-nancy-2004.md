A brief military aviation case from GEIPAN’s files: in October 2004 the crews of a French Air Force Mirage IV patrol saw an aircraft close behind one of them for 15 to 20 seconds, which then vanished; the controllers had nothing on radar. GEIPAN’s file is short and the case remains unexplained.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 2004-10-01631, classified D (unexplained)*
