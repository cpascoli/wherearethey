On the evening of 11 September 2010, according to the Inner Mongolia Morning Post, air traffic controllers of the Hohhot area-control office detected an unidentified object about 40 km east of Baotou at 20:00 and warned Baotou Airport. From about 21:14 three inbound flights from Beijing and Shanghai were delayed and circled; Air China CA1107 diverted to Ordos and Juneyao 1137 to Taiyuan. At 22:00 the airport was told that flights could operate normally again.

It was the second disruption at a Chinese airport attributed to a UFO in 2010, after Hangzhou Xiaoshan in July. The operational facts come from the contemporary regional press; no statement by the Civil Aviation Administration or the air traffic authority, and no identification of the object, has been found.

*Sources: Inner Mongolia Morning Post (13 Sep 2010, republished by Sina and Hinews) · [Peter Greenberg (2010)](https://petergreenberg.com/2010/10/08/ufo-shuts-down-chinese-airport/)*
