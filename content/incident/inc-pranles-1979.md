In July 1979 in the Ardèche a couple, the husband a scientist specialising in light and colour, watched a stationary yellow-orange light on a ridge send a blue beam rotating steadily around it for 45 minutes; their neighbours saw the light from their house too. GEIPAN found no natural or man-made explanation and gives weight to the main witness’s expertise; he has since died, and no reconstruction is possible.

*Source: GEIPAN (CNES), case 1979-07-00644, classified D (unexplained)*
