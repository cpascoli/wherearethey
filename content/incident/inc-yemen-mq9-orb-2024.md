At the House Oversight task force's hearing on 9 September 2025, Rep. Eric Burlison played a video he said he had been given. He described it as filmed on 30 October 2024 by an MQ-9 Reaper tracking an orb-like object off the coast of Yemen, while a second, unseen MQ-9 fired a Hellfire missile at it. In the clip a missile streaks in, appears to strike the object, and the object keeps moving.

Asked about it, the journalist George Knapp said he had heard of the event but had not had the footage, and described the missile as hitting the object and bouncing off; Burlison used the video to press his complaint that Congress is denied such material.

The video was not released or authenticated by the Pentagon or AARO, and nothing public establishes its source, the platform or what the object was. What happened in the frames rests on the descriptions given at the hearing.
