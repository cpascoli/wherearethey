In the early hours of 16 April 1978 police forces across southern England and South Wales relayed reports of bright objects with glowing trails crossing the sky. The RAF plotted the reports, found they described something moving from south-west to north-east, and RAF Fylingdales, the ballistic-missile early-warning station, confirmed that three pieces of space hardware had decayed from orbit at the time.

The RAF cited the case in its briefing for the House of Lords debate on UFOs in January 1979 as an example of how such reports were investigated and explained. Re-entering space debris recurs as an explanation in the MoD files, as in the Cosford sightings of 1993.

*Source: MoD file DEFE 24/2048 (pp. 108–138), The National Archives (opened March 2011)*
