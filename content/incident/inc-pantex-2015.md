The Pantex Plant near Amarillo, Texas, is the main U.S. facility for assembling, disassembling and maintaining nuclear weapons. On the morning of 1 September 2015 its ground surveillance radar picked up an unknown object moving slowly north across open ground west of the plant. Under its plan for unmanned aircraft, the Protective Force locked the gates to the security areas and sent officers after it.

A lieutenant and a Security Police Officer followed the object for several miles and watched it through binoculars; they heard no sound, saw no propulsion and reported that it sped up and changed direction as they pursued it, before they lost it near the county roads north of the plant. Officers in an armoured vehicle tracked it on a remote weapon-system camera at 100–200 feet. Witnesses gave its size as about four feet by two, but disagreed on its colour. The radar video was sent to Sandia National Laboratories and the evidence to the FBI; the report, released by the Department of Energy through PURSUE in 2026, draws no conclusion about what it was.

*Sources: Pantex Unidentified Object Incident Report (Department of Energy, 2015; PURSUE releases 02 and 04)*
