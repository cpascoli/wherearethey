In February 1977, fourteen children at Broad Haven Primary School in Pembrokeshire, Wales, said they saw a yellow, cigar-shaped craft hovering in a field near the school in broad daylight.

When the headmaster separated them, the children drew strikingly similar pictures of a metallic craft. The case became part of the "Dyfed Triangle" wave of sightings reported in west Wales that year. The historian David Clarke has revisited the case and its local context.
