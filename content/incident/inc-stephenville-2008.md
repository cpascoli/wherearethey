On the evening of 8 January 2008, residents of Stephenville, Selden and Dublin in Erath County, Texas, about 100 miles south-west of Dallas, reported a large, silent, brightly lit object that hovered and then moved off at great speed; several said fighter jets chased it. Witnesses quoted at the time included a private pilot, Steve Allen, and a constable, Lee Roy Gaitan. The local Stephenville Empire-Tribune broke the story and counted about 40 witnesses.

Military officials first said no fighters had been in the area and suggested an optical illusion such as sunlight reflecting off airliners. On 23 January the Air Force Reserve corrected itself: about ten F-16s from the 301st Fighter Wing at Fort Worth had been on night training flights that evening, and the earlier denial was put down to an internal communications error. As far as the Reserve knew, the pilots reported nothing unusual and had not been interviewed.

MUFON investigators later analysed FAA radar data obtained under FOIA. They report the F-16s' tracks and, separately, unidentified returns without transponders in the area at the time of the main sightings; that analysis is theirs, not an official finding. No official investigation of the object itself is on record.

*Sources: NPR (16 and 24 January 2008) · Associated Press (23 January 2008) · G. Schulze and R. Powell, Stephenville Lights: A Comprehensive Radar and Witness Report Study (MUFON)*
