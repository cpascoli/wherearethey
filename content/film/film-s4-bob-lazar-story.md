A 2026 documentary in which Bob Lazar tells his own story: from his early interest in science to his claim that in 1988 he was hired to work at S4, a classified site within the Area 51 range, where he says he and 22 other scientists reverse-engineered extraterrestrial craft. It relies on detailed recreations of what Lazar describes, with interviews with the journalist George Knapp, Gene Huff and Lazar's wife Joy. It follows Jeremy Corbell's *Bob Lazar: Area 51 & Flying Saucers* (2018).

The film presents Lazar's account; his claimed degrees and his employment at S4 have never been verified, and he is outside this archive's sourcing criteria as a witness. The trailer plays here from the producers' (Project Gravitaur) YouTube channel.
