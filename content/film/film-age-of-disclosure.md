The documentary with the largest group of officials on record: members of Congress, a former Director of National Intelligence, the former UAP Task Force director, and Navy pilots from the Nimitz and Roosevelt encounters. What they say in the film is testimony and is marked as claimed. The film doesn't present documents or data that can be checked independently, which is the main criticism.
