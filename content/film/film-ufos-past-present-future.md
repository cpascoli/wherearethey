*UFOs: Past, Present, and Future* is a 1974 American documentary, written by Robert Emenegger, produced by Allan Sandler and narrated by Rod Serling, with Burgess Meredith and José Ferrer. It reviews sightings from the post-war years onward, with interviews including J. Allen Hynek, Col. Robert Friend and Col. William Coleman of Project Blue Book and Col. George Weinbrenner of Foreign Technology at Wright-Patterson. It was re-released in 1976 and 1979 as *UFOs: It Has Begun*, around *Close Encounters of the Third Kind*.

The filmmakers say they made it with the cooperation of officials at Norton Air Force Base and were promised Air Force footage of a landing at Holloman Air Force Base for the finale. The footage never came, and the film ends instead with a dramatised Holloman landing narrated by Serling. A later claim that a few seconds of real footage appear in it was debunked: the shot is part of the reenactment. The writer Jason Colavito notes that sources conflict on who commissioned the film.
