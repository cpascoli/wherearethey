A Project Blue Book case file, catalogued by the National Archives as “July 1947, Incident Number 12”, that gathers the reports of 4 July 1947 from Portland, Oregon, and nearby, with the Air Force's evaluations of them and of the United Air Lines crew's sighting near Boise, Idaho, the same evening. Our copy is the 59 page images from the NARA catalog, OCR'd for search.

It includes press clippings from the Oregon Journal of 5 July 1947, the reports of Portland policemen, harbor patrolmen and Vancouver deputies, and an analyst's note that found no astronomical explanation. Names of witnesses are blacked out in the sanitized copy.

*Source: U.S. National Archives, Project Blue Book (local copy)*
