Five quarterly histories of the 341st Strategic Missile Wing at Malmstrom Air Force Base, covering January 1967 to March 1968, released under the Freedom of Information Act to researcher John Greenewald in 2001. They document the investigation of the 16 March 1967 Echo Flight shutdown, in which all ten Minuteman I missiles lost strategic alert within seconds of each other, and the engineering tests that followed. Our copy is the FOIA release as published by The Black Vault; the scans are partly legible and the OCR text is imperfect.

*Sources: 341st Strategic Missile Wing histories (FOIA release, 2001)*
