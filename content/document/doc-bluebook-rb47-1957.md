The Project Blue Book file on the RB-47 encounter of 17 July 1957, filed under "Forbes AFB, Kansas, July 1957" in the National Archives' sanitized Blue Book case files (microfilm T1206, roll 28). Its 56 pages hold the record card, the 55th Strategic Reconnaissance Wing's four-page message amplifying the crew's CIRVIS report (signed by the wing intelligence officer), Air Defense Command and Air Technical Intelligence Center correspondence, a Civil Aeronautics Administration letter on two airliner near-misses that month, the pilot's Airborne Observer's Data Sheet, and later additions: a 1971 reprint of James McDonald's article and Philip Klass's 1971 paper "The RB-47 UFO Case -- A New Explanation", with a 1972 covering letter.

The file shows the case's contradictions: ADC carried it as unknown, an ATIC analyst found it hard to dismiss, and the record card closed it as an airliner by citing a different near-miss. Our copy was assembled from NARA's page images; its OCR is imperfect, and the record card and the message's last two pages were transcribed by hand.

*Sources: National Archives, Project Blue Book (NARA ID 28970538)*
