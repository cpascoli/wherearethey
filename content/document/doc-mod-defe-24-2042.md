A Ministry of Defence file opened at The National Archives in August 2010. Its heart is a 2002 case before the Parliamentary Ombudsman: an applicant for the MoD's Rendlesham Forest file, backed by his MP, complained that three documents had been withheld as internal advice. The Ombudsman agreed they were advice but recommended their release, the MoD accepted, and the file holds both the submission that weighed the decision and the documents themselves: the Ministry's 1983 briefing on the Halt memo, a 1985 ministerial letter, and a former MoD official's argument that the case could hardly be without defence significance.

The rest of the file is later correspondence of the UFO desk, including the same correspondent's 2003 sighting reports.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 24/2042/1; page numbers are those of the Archives' PDF.*
