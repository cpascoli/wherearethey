The Project Blue Book files on the sightings of March 1954 at Nouasseur Air Depot (later Nouasseur Air Base), near Casablanca, French Morocco, from the National Archives' sanitized Blue Book case files (microfilm T1206, roll 20). Our copy joins three NARA file units: two record cards for 5 March (NARA ID 28957771); the case file proper (28957774), with the later record card, the 736th AC&W Squadron's messages and controller's log, the 1975-1 AACS detachment's tower log, the base intelligence officer's report on AF Form 112 and the OSI special inquiry with the crews' interviews; and the first two pages of 28957904, the record card and message for the F-86F sighting of 12 March (the rest of that unit belongs to an unrelated Greenland case).

The pages are poor microfilm images, several printed in negative; our copy was assembled from NARA's page images, and the record cards, the OSI synopsis and the paragraphs cited in our files carry a transcription made from the scan above their OCR.

*Sources: National Archives, Project Blue Book (NARA IDs 28957771, 28957774, 28957904)*
