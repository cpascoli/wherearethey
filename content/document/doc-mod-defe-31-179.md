A file of DI55, the Defence Intelligence Staff branch that looked at UFO reports for anything of defence interest, covering January 1990 to June 1991 and opened at The National Archives in March 2009. Its best-known pages are the UFO desk's brief for ministers on the Calvine photographs, the first official account of the case, written four days after the negatives arrived and the day they went back to the newspaper.

The file also holds the checks run after a report from Belfast Airport in December 1989 of a missile-shaped object with a green flame, seen for two seconds by one observer near the airport: no fixed-wing aircraft, two Army helicopters nearby. That report rests on a single civilian witness and is not filed as a case here.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 31/179/1; page numbers are those of the Archives' PDF (the March 2009 highlights guide's numbers run one higher).*
