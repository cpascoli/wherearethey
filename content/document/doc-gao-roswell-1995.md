The General Accounting Office's 1995 report to Congressman Steven Schiff of New Mexico, who had asked what government records existed about the 1947 Roswell crash. It found two contemporary records: a July 1947 Roswell Army Air Field history and an FBI message saying the recovered disc and balloon were being flown to Wright Field for examination. A search of Wright Field's records for 1947–1950 found nothing about Roswell, and the GAO learned that Roswell Army Air Field's administrative records and outgoing messages for the period had been destroyed without a recorded authority.

The report is evidence both for the Wright Field connection in the Roswell story and for the absence of any surviving record of what was examined there.
