The National Military Command Center's record of the night of 7–8 November 1975 at Malmstrom AFB, Montana, the best-documented night of the autumn 1975 incursions over northern SAC bases. NORAD's height-finder radar tracked slow-moving targets over Lewistown while missile-site crews reported lights and jet-like sounds; two F-106 interceptors were scrambled and never made contact. NORAD ruled out northern lights and carried the track as a fade remaining unknown. The DDO update condenses the memorandum for distribution.

Our copy is three scanned pages from a Department of Defense release (Office of the Assistant Secretary of Defense, Freedom of Information and Security Review, case 97-F-1677, September 1997), as published by The Black Vault, a private archive; parts of the update are redacted.
