The official stenographic transcript of the open session of the Senate Armed Services Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and Capabilities on 19 April 2023, chaired by Sen. Kirsten Gillibrand with Sens. Joni Ernst and Jacky Rosen, and the first public testimony by AARO director Sean Kirkpatrick.

Gillibrand pressed on AARO's funding, its sidelining during the February 2023 shoot-downs, radar filters and the still-missing public reporting portal. Kirkpatrick set out AARO's process and trend data — about half of reports are spheres, typically one to four meters — showed one unresolved and one resolved MQ-9 video, and said most cases lack data rather than defy explanation, while some advanced signatures may point to foreign capabilities.
