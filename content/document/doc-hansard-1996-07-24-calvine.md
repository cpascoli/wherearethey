A written parliamentary answer of 24 July 1996, the MoD's only public statement on the Calvine photograph at the time. Asked by Martin Redmond MP what the department made of the photograph and who removed it from the Secretariat (Air Staff) 2a office, the defence minister Nicholas Soames said the negatives had been examined, judged to contain nothing of defence significance, and not retained, and that the MoD had no record of any prints made from them.

The answer did not address who removed the print from the office. In 2022 the Armed Forces minister James Heappey repeated it to MPs and said the only surviving MoD papers on the case are in file DEFE 24/1940/1.
