The President's remarks of 16 February 2023 on the three unidentified objects shot down over Alaska, the Yukon and Lake Huron on 10–12 February, after the Chinese high-altitude balloon. He gave the intelligence community's assessment that they were most likely private, recreational or research balloons, said there was no evidence of a sudden increase in such objects, and announced new parameters for dealing with them.
