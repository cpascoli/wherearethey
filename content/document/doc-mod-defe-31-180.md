A file of DI55, the Defence Intelligence Staff branch that looked at UFO reports for anything of defence interest, covering June 1991 to March 1992 and opened at The National Archives in March 2009. Alongside the sighting reports copied to it by the UFO desk it holds the branch's own minutes: on the Tornado crews who were overtaken over the North Sea, on the Calvine photographs, on the Cranwell “possible missile”, and on the airship that filled the desk's post in early 1992.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 31/180/1; page numbers are those of the Archives' PDF (the March 2009 highlights guide's numbers run one higher).*
