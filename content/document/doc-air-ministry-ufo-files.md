Two older UFO files that survived the Air Ministry's five-yearly destruction of such papers. AIR 20/7390 collects reports of unidentified aircraft and objects made to the Air Ministry in 1950–54, among them radar tracks the RAF could not identify at the start of the Cold War; AIR 2/19086 holds Air Staff policy papers of 1970–75, including the decision to stop destroying UFO files.

*Sources: The National Archives, AIR 20/7390 and AIR 2/19086 (our copies are The Black Vault's mirror); descriptions from The National Archives' UFO research guide (2013)*
