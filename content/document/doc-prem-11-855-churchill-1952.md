A short Prime Minister's Office file: Winston Churchill's famous one-line minute of July 1952, written days after radar and visual reports of UFOs over Washington DC made world headlines, and the Air Ministry's reply that the subject had been fully studied in 1951 and explained. Its mention of a 1951 study was the first public clue to the Flying Saucer Working Party, whose report was found only in 2001. A 1955 exchange with Duncan Sandys about an Air Ministry intelligence article completes the file.

*Source: The National Archives, PREM 11/855 (our copy is The Black Vault's mirror); release date from The National Archives' UFO research guide (2013)*
