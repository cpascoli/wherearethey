The printed record of "Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena: Exposing the Truth", the joint hearing of two House Oversight subcommittees on 13 November 2024, chaired by Reps. Nancy Mace and Glenn Grothman. The witnesses were Rear Adm. Tim Gallaudet, the former Pentagon official Luis Elizondo, the journalist Michael Shellenberger and the former NASA official Michael Gold.

Elizondo opened: "UAP are real. Advanced technologies not made by our government or any other government are monitoring sensitive military installations around the globe," adding that the U.S. possesses UAP technologies and is in a "multidecade secretive arms race". Gallaudet described receiving, in January 2015, a Navy email headed "URGENT SAFETY OF FLIGHT ISSUE" with the GoFast video attached, which later vanished from recipients' inboxes. Gold argued for scientific study and against stigma.

The record includes the "Immaculate Constellation" report entered by Rep. Mace, documents submitted by Rep. Burchett and questions for the record.
