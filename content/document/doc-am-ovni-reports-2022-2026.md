The Italian Air Force's tables of the OVNI reports it received from 2022 onwards, one file per year, published on its OVNI page a few months after each year ends (the 2026 table, uploaded in October 2026, runs to May). They have the same layout as the earlier tables: place, date, time, shape, colour, direction, speed, motion, altitude, weather, who sent the report and the Air Force's finding. There are only a few reports a year, nearly all from private citizens; two came from Air Force personnel.

The finding is almost always the same: the data gathered from the Air Force's offices showed no link with flight activity or any other known phenomenon, so the event is catalogued as O.V.N.I. One 2023 entry, a balloon-like object over Pesaro, notes a radiosonde launched from San Pietro Capofiume (Bologna) half an hour earlier and still files the event as O.V.N.I. The tables name no witnesses, and "O.V.N.I." records that no explanation was found, not extraordinary performance or origin.

*Sources: Aeronautica Militare, OVNI tables 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (copies from the Wayback Machine snapshot of the OVNI page, October 2026)*
