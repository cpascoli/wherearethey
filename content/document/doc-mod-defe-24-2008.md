A Ministry of Defence file of the UFO desk's sighting reports from February 1999 to January 2000, opened at The National Archives in February 2010: the standard forms faxed in by RAF stations, air traffic units and police, with names redacted, and the desk's referrals of the few reports from witnesses it rated credible.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 24/2008/1; page numbers are those of the Archives' PDF.*
