A Foreign Broadcast Information Service press note, produced for the CIA and released under FOIA in its UFO collection as C05516709. It translates and summarises the Soviet press reports on the 21 March 1990 air-defence alert near Pereslavl-Zalessky and Zagorsk, north-east of Moscow. The core is the material the chief of the Main Staff of the Soviet Air Defence Forces gave to *Rabochaya Tribuna*: an interceptor pilot's report, a radar post's log, officers' observations and his own assessment. It also covers the newspaper's editorial claims and other Soviet sightings of the period.

The note relays what the Soviet newspaper printed. The underlying military documents have not been published in full.
