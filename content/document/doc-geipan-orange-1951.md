  - text: "The commander of Base Aérienne 115 (Orange-Caritat) forwarded to the 4th Air Region the reports of two 5e Escadre de Chasse pilots, a lieutenant and a sergent-chef (names blanked in the released copy), who during a morning exercise at 11:30 saw \"un engin bizarre\" in the base's airspace. He wrote that both were experienced observers, \"des gens pondérés\", whose accounts were \"absolument semblables\", and that he had questioned them himself about an hour and a half afterwards."
    status: documented
    source: library/docs/doc-geipan-orange-1951.pdf
  - text: "The synthesis records that the object was brilliant, without relief and without smoke; it first appeared spherical and stationary at about 10,000 ft over the Serres–Veynes–Aspres area, then abruptly became oblong and moved away to the east, climbing, as the pilots closed on it. Its horizontal or climbing speed was greater than that of their Vampire jets, and it disappeared during the pursuit."
    status: documented

The 1951 French Air Ministry file on two Vampire pilots' sighting near Orange, published by GEIPAN with names blanked. It contains the base commander's letter, a synthesis of the observations and the pilots' signed statements.
