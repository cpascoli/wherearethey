Three State Department telegrams on the Moroccan sightings of 19 September 1976, as preserved in NARA's Central Foreign Policy Files (Record Group 59, Electronic Telegrams 1976) and served by its Access to Archival Databases: the Rabat embassy's request for information of 25 September (RABAT 5209, film D760363-0377), the Department's holding reply of 2 October (STATE 244918, D760372-0205) and its substantive answer of 5 October (STATE 247538, D760375-1289). All three were declassified in the State Department's systematic review of 4 May 2006; the same texts circulate in WikiLeaks' PlusD collection, which reproduces this NARA file.

The embassy's cable relays what the Royal Gendarmerie gathered from across the country, including the account of a Gendarmerie major who saw the object himself; the Department's answer offers no firm explanation but leans toward a spectacular meteor or a re-entering piece of satellite.

*Sources: National Archives, RG 59 Central Foreign Policy Files, Electronic Telegrams 1976 (AAD), documents 1976RABAT05209, 1976STATE244918, 1976STATE247538*
