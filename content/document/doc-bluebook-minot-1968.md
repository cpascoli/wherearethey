The Project Blue Book file on the night of 24 October 1968 at Minot Air Force Base, North Dakota, when missile-site maintenance and security personnel reported a bright light near a launch facility and a B-52 crew tracked an unidentified radar return that paced their bomber, photographing the radar scope. The file contains the record card, memos of telephone conversations between Blue Book and the base investigator, witness questionnaires, the B-52's radio transcript and analysis notes. Witness names are largely blanked in the release. Our copy was assembled from NARA's page images; the OCR text is imperfect.

*Sources: National Archives, Project Blue Book (NARA ID 302580672)*
