The Department of War's UAP/UFO page at war.gov/UFO hosts PURSUE, the U.S. government's public release of declassified UAP files under a February 2026 presidential directive. It publishes tranches of unresolved-case records from the Department of War, the FBI, NASA, the CIA, the State Department and other agencies every few weeks, starting on 8 May 2026, each with an official description and a link to the original file.

Every record it publishes is mirrored in this archive's Index and linked from the files it concerns.
