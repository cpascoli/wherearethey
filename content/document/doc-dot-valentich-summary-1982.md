The Department of Transport's summary report on the loss of Cessna 182L VH-DSJ and its pilot, Frederick Valentich, over Bass Strait on 21 October 1978. It sits at the front of the department's investigation file, now digitised by the National Archives of Australia as NAA: B1497, V116/783/1047 (319 pages). Our copy holds screen captures of its three pages and of a 1983 letter from the same file, with a transcription.

The report gives the flight's background (a night VMC flight plan to King Island and back, full tanks, four life jackets aboard), the recorded radio exchange in which the pilot described an unidentified object, the weather and the search. Its opinion as to cause is that the reason for the disappearance has not been determined.

A later letter in the file records that a Cessna 182 engine cowl flap from a serial range that includes VH-DSJ washed up on Flinders Island in May 1983; the department asked the navy's research laboratory whether it could have drifted there. The file does not, in the pages we hold, link the part to the aircraft.

*Sources: National Archives of Australia, NAA: B1497, V116/783/1047 (pages 5–7 and 18)*
