  - text: "Radar station EVA 3 made contact with an object apparently orbiting in the Talavera area at 16:26Z, and EVA 2 at 16:45Z, with a double echo on the height-finder. At 16:59Z two F-86s from Torrejón were scrambled with cameras and reached its vertical. All crews described a changing, very bright light-grey object — a deformed ball, a pyramid, two cones joined at the base — and generally agreed it might be a balloon. The Spanish Air Force file on the object followed by a T-33, two radar stations and two scrambled F-86 interceptors over Montánchez on 3 June 1967. It contains the air-defence command's report, the reports of the MATADOR and BOLERO radar station chiefs with their track logs, and the transcript of the radio tape. It also holds the reports of the F-86 and T-33 crews, a copy of the F-86 photograph and a press cutting from the *Hoja del Lunes de Badajoz* of 5 June 1967. Declassified in 1992. Published in the Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa under CC BY 4.0.

*Sources: Ejército del Aire, expediente 670603*
