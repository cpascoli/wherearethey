The transcript of the House Intelligence subcommittee's open hearing on UAP on 17 May 2022, chaired by Rep. André Carson, the first public congressional hearing on the subject in over fifty years. The witnesses were Ronald Moultrie, Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security, and Scott Bray, Deputy Director of Naval Intelligence.

Bray reported that since the 2021 preliminary assessment the UAP database had grown to "approximately 400 reports", showed videos and images including objects later identified, and told the committee: "We have not had a collision. We have had at least 11 near misses." Pressed on origins, he said "we have no material" and that the task force had "detected no emanations" that would suggest anything "nonterrestrial in origin", while acknowledging a small number of cases with unexpected flight characteristics.

Rep. Mike Gallagher used the hearing to have the "Admiral Wilson memo" entered into the record.
