English translations of witness statements written by Soviet officers and soldiers after the lights seen near the Strategic Rocket Forces sites at Usovo and Belokorovichi, Zhytomyr region, on 4 October 1982. Each statement carries a military-unit stamp. George Knapp says he obtained them in Russia in the early 1990s. He gave copies to the House Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets in 2025, and KLAS-TV published them in January 2026.

They are typed depositions only. There is no technical appraisal of the control-panel event and no conclusion from the investigating commission. Their provenance rests on Knapp, and no Russian or Ukrainian archive has certified them. They describe moving lights, not a structured craft over the base. They do not say that launch codes were entered or that the missiles prepared to launch.
