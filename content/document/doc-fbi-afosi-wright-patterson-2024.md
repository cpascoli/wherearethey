The first official record on file of the December 2024 drone incursions at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base: three investigative reports by the Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI), which the FBI released to John Greenewald Jr. of The Black Vault under the Freedom of Information Act on 13 November 2026. The Black Vault published them on 26 November 2026.

The reports cover three nights. They describe small quadcopter-type drones, some flying in synchronized formations of four, seen by Security Forces patrols and by AFOSI's own agents; the closing of the base's airspace at 2230 on 13 December; and one patrol's account of radios that would not transmit while the drones were near. They also record what did not hold up or was not found: one sighting judged mistaken, air traffic control and airfield management seeing nothing on 16 December, police patrols in Fairborn and Riverside seeing nothing, and a vehicle lead that came to nothing. The reports give no technical assessment of the radio trouble and no explanation of the flights. They call the craft small unmanned aerial systems throughout, and in every case say the operators were not identified.

Our copy is The Black Vault's PDF of the FBI release (12 pages: the cover letter and standard enclosures, then the seven released pages). The searchable text is the PDF's own text layer, which is imperfect; quotations here were checked against the page images. Names and some locations are redacted.

*Source: FBI FOIPA release 1708151-000, 13 November 2026 (AFOSI Forms 158, transmitted 8 September 2025), via The Black Vault*
