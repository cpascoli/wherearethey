The transcript of "Restoring Public Trust Through UAP Transparency and Whistleblower Protection", the hearing held on 9 September 2025 by the House Oversight Committee's Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets, chaired by Rep. Anna Paulina Luna with Jasmine Crockett as ranking member.

The witnesses were the Air Force veteran Jeffrey Nuccetelli, who described the 2003 "Red Square" incursions at Vandenberg; Navy Chief Alexandro Wiggins; the journalist George Knapp, who spoke about Bob Lazar, Area 51 and what he called institutionalised official lying; the Air Force veteran Dylan Borland, who described a 2012 triangular craft at Langley; and, for the minority, Joe Spielberger of the Project On Government Oversight, on overclassification and whistleblower protection.

The documents entered into the record include a letter to the Secretary of Defense about alleged reprisals against David Grusch.
