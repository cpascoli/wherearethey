The official printed record (Serial No. 118–53) of the 26 July 2023 hearing of the House Oversight Subcommittee on National Security, the Border, and Foreign Affairs, chaired by Glenn Grothman with Ranking Member Robert Garcia, published on GovInfo. It contains the witnesses' statements and the full questioning of David Grusch, Ryan Graves and David Fravor.

The transcript documents what was said under oath, not that the claims are true. The facts above record statements; Grusch's crash-retrieval claims remain unverified, and AARO's later Historical Record Report disputed them.
