The 1954 revision of the U.S. Air Force regulation on reporting unidentified flying objects, issued under Chief of Staff Nathan F. Twining. It replaced the first AFR 200-2 of 26 August 1953, issued in the months after the CIA-convened Robertson Panel recommended that the government reduce public interest in UFOs.

It put field investigations in the hands of the Air Defense Command's 4602d Air Intelligence Service Squadron, and allowed the press to be told about a sighting only once it had been identified as a familiar object; for unexplained cases, only the fact that the Air Technical Intelligence Center would analyse the data could be released. Our copy is the scan published on Wikimedia Commons, OCR'd for search.

*Sources: Department of the Air Force, AFR 200-2 (1954)*
