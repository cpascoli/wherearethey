The U.S. Air Force's earliest written statement on the Phoenix Lights: a Freedom of Information Act reply from Luke Air Force Base, dated 1 May 1997, to the researcher John Greenewald (The Black Vault). Luke said it held no records, that its F-16s were not involved, and that its staff had not attributed the lights to flares.

The flare explanation came later: in July 1997 the Maryland Air National Guard said its A-10s had dropped illumination flares over the Barry M. Goldwater Range that night, which accounts for the later lights but not, by most accounts, the earlier formation. Our copy is The Black Vault's scan; the letter's text recognition is poor, so it is paraphrased here except for its closing line.

*Source: Luke AFB, 56th Communications Squadron, 1 May 1997 (via The Black Vault)*
