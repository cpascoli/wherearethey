The official printed record of the House Armed Services Committee's hearing of 17 April 2024 on the Department of the Air Force's fiscal year 2025 budget request, with Secretary of the Air Force Frank Kendall, Air Force Chief of Staff Gen. David Allvin and Chief of Space Operations Gen. B. Chance Saltzman.

It is filed here for one exchange: Rep. Rob Wittman raised the December 2023 drone incursions that forced operations at Langley Air Force Base to shut down, and asked who is responsible for defending air bases against small drones. Kendall's answer dealt with threats to bases overseas and the Army-led air base defence study, without mentioning Langley.
