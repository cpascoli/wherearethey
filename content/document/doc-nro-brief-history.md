The National Reconnaissance Office's own unclassified history, published by its Center for the Study of National Reconnaissance for the agency's 50th anniversary and revised in 2018. It covers the founding of the NRO in 1961, its satellite and aircraft programs, and the 1992 declassification of its existence. It is filed here as the official source for the NRO's dates and for its oversight of the U-2 and A-12/SR-71 aircraft that the CIA links to UFO reports of the 1950s and 1960s.
