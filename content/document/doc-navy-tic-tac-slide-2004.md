A single, undated U.S. Navy briefing slide summarising the Nimitz “Tic Tac” case of 14 November 2004, with two frames from the FLIR1 video. The Navy posted it, unredacted, in its online FOIA reading room in January 2023, in a folder labelled “UAP INFO”, alongside 72 redacted “range fouler” reports from 2004 to 2021, one of them on the same incident; *The War Zone* reported the release on 13 January 2023.

It is the Navy's own summary of the case: the debriefs “validated the event”, but there was “very limited data to support technical analysis”, and the reporting stopped at 3rd Fleet because there was no process to share it. It does not say what the object was.

*Sources: U.S. Navy FOIA reading room (our copy: the Navy's PDF, OCR'd) · The War Zone, 13 January 2023*
