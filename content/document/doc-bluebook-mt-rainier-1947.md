The Project Blue Book case file on the sighting by the private pilot Kenneth Arnold near Mount Rainier on 24 June 1947, the report that began the modern UFO era, held by the National Archives in its sanitized version of the Blue Book case files. Our copy is the 61 page images from the NARA catalog, OCR'd for search.

It contains the record card, the Air Force's analyses and later summaries, Arnold's correspondence and statements, and press material from 1947. The Air Force concluded that the objects were a mirage; earlier, an analyst had suggested known aircraft.

*Source: U.S. National Archives, Project Blue Book (local copy)*
