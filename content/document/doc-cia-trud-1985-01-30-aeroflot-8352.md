An English translation of a Soviet newspaper article: the *Trud* report of 30 January 1985 on what the crew of Aeroflot flight 8352, a Tu-134 from Tbilisi via Rostov to Tallinn, saw near Minsk, followed by a commentary from the deputy chairman of a Soviet scientific commission on anomalous phenomena. The CIA released it in its UFO collection (document 0005516658, control number C00174708). Its page number and "CSO" line suggest it comes from a Joint Publications Research Service translation series.

The article is a correspondent's retelling of the crew's account, not an official investigation report. It names the four crew members and describes the controllers' remarks; it does not give the date of the flight.
