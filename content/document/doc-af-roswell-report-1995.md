The U.S. Air Force's first report on Roswell, prepared in 1994 by Col. Richard L. Weaver and 1st Lt. James McAndrew of the Secretary of the Air Force's declassification team after Representative Steven Schiff asked the General Accounting Office to find the records of the July 1947 events, and published in 1995 as a 994-page book with all its attachments.

It concludes that the debris collected by Maj. Jesse Marcel and Capt. Sheridan Cavitt on the Foster ranch most likely came from a Project MOGUL balloon train, a then Top Secret programme to detect Soviet nuclear tests with microphones carried by strings of balloons and radar targets. Its attachments are primary material in their own right: the sworn statements of Cavitt and of the Fort Worth weather officer Irving Newton, the transcript of Cavitt's interview, the affidavits collected by researchers, MOGUL flight records and the original Fort Worth photographs. Its sequel, *Case Closed* (1997), addressed the later accounts of bodies.

*Source: U.S. Air Force, Headquarters, 1995 (Air Force Historical Support Division copy)*
