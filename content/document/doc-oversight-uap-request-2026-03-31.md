The letter in which Rep. Anna Paulina Luna, chair of the House Oversight Committee's Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets, asked the Secretary of War on 31 March 2026 for 46 UAP video items that whistleblowers had told the Task Force AARO held, with a deadline of 14 April 2026. The items are listed by their uploader titles, call signs and dates: drone and aircraft clips from the Persian Gulf, Iran, Syria, Afghanistan, the East China Sea, the U.S. East Coast, Lake Huron and elsewhere.

The Department of War released them in its second PURSUE release on 22 May 2026, in the letter's own order. AARO's descriptions of that release date the request to 6 March 2026 and attribute it to eight members of the House, which does not match the published letter. Our copy is the PDF published by the Oversight Committee.

*Sources: House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform (2026)*
