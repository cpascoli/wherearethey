The Brazilian Aerospace Defence Command's confidential report on the night of 19–20 May 1986, when lights and radar contacts over São Paulo, São José dos Campos and Goiás led the Air Force to scramble interceptors from the Santa Cruz and Anápolis air bases. The episode became known as the "Noite Oficial dos OVNIs" after the Minister of Aeronautics, Brig. Octávio Júlio Moreira Lima, held a press conference on 23 May. The report is held by Brazil's National Archives (Arquivo Nacional); the OCR text of the letter-spaced typescript is imperfect.

*Sources: Comando de Defesa Aérea, report of 2 June 1986 (Arquivo Nacional, Brazil)*
