The journalist Michael Shellenberger's 212-page written testimony for the House Oversight hearing of 13 November 2024, with appendices. Its argument is about oversight: the law requires that special access programmes and intelligence compartments be reported to Congress, including the "Gang of Eight", but he argues that for UAP this is not happening, partly through a White House loophole for waived programmes that are not covert action.

The appendices reproduce the "Immaculate Constellation" whistleblower report, which alleges an unacknowledged programme collecting and controlling UAP imagery, and a long chronology of UAP-related government documents and events. The chronology mixes primary documents, such as the Twining and Bolender memos, with disputed material derived from MJ-12, so individual items should be traced to their originals.

The Department of Defense said it has no record of a programme called Immaculate Constellation.
