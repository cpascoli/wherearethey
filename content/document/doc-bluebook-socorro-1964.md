The U.S. Air Force's Project Blue Book file on the sighting by Socorro police officer Lonnie Zamora on 24 April 1964, one of the few landing cases Blue Book left officially unidentified. The 168-page file includes the record card, investigation summaries, FTD correspondence, statements and press clippings. Released on Blue Book microfilm at the National Archives; our copy was assembled from NARA's page images and the OCR text is imperfect.

*Sources: National Archives, Project Blue Book (NARA ID 302532129)*
