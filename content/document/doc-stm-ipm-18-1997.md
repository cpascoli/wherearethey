The Brazilian Army's own investigation of the Varginha case: a military police inquiry (Inquérito Policial Militar) opened in 1997 by the commander of the Escola de Sargentos das Armas in Três Corações, the military school named in the stories of a captured creature. Its purpose was to establish whether a book alleging that the school's soldiers had captured and transported an extraterrestrial had committed a crime; it was not a scientific investigation of the sighting.

It concluded that the creature was most likely a local man mistaken for one, that the military movements cited were routine, and that the book was fiction; the case was closed. Its sceptics note that it was an investigation by the institution accused, of a book rather than of the witnesses, and that it never heard the soldiers who spoke to the ufologists anonymously. The scanned volumes are in Portuguese; our copies carry OCR text, which is searchable here.

*Sources: Superior Tribunal Militar, IPM nº 18/1997 (Autos Findos 908), vols. 1–2*
