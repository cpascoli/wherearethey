The Project Blue Book case file on the B-29 sightings of 29 January 1952 over North Korea, held by the National Archives in microfilm publication T1206 (Project "Bluebook", 1947–1969, roll 9) and digitized by Fold3. Our copy is the 30 page images from the NARA catalog, OCR'd for search.

It contains the Project 10073 record card, ATIC's analysis (Appendix III), Far East Air Forces bomber command messages and intelligence officers' comments, the Air Force intelligence memo prepared for a reply to Senator Russell, and later notes on a possible balloon explanation. The record card lists the source as two B-29 crews, the shape as a disk about three feet across and the colour as "fiery-like sun", and marks the conclusion as possibly balloon or possibly aircraft. No final evaluation was reached.

*Source: U.S. National Archives, Project Blue Book (local copy)*
