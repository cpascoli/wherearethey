*Les OVNI et la Défense : à quoi doit-on se préparer ?* is a report by COMETA, a **private** association of former auditors of France's Institute for Advanced National Defence Studies (IHEDN) and outside experts, chaired by the retired Air Force General Denis Letty, with a preface by General Bernard Norlain, a former director of IHEDN. It was not commissioned by the French government. It first appeared in a special issue of the magazine VSD in July 1999; the archive's copy is the English translation found in NASA's files and released in PURSUE release 01.

The report reviews French cases (Air France AF 3532, Trans-en-Provence) and foreign ones (Lakenheath–Bentwaters, the RB-47, Tehran, San Carlos de Bariloche), the work of GEPAN/SEPRA at CNES, and the defence questions it thinks the phenomenon raises. It concludes that unknown flying objects almost certainly exist physically and that the extraterrestrial hypothesis is the best one, though not proven, and recommends informing officials and pilots and pursuing study.

These are the conclusions of a private group of retired officers and engineers, not an official finding. On AF 3532 its account differs from the official one: GEIPAN's case file judges the radar track inconsistent with the object the crew described.

*Sources: COMETA, UFOs and Defense: What Should We Prepare For? (VSD, July 1999), English translation released as PURSUE record 255_413270 · GEIPAN case file on AF 3532*
