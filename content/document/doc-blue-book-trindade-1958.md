A Project Blue Book file on the photographs taken from the Brazilian Navy ship Almirante Saldanha off Trindade Island on 16 January 1958. It holds the Project 10073 record card (conclusion: hoax), the U.S. Naval Attaché's Office of Naval Intelligence Information Report 39-58 of 11 March 1958, which carries the Brazilian Navy Ministry's official statement and the attaché's comments, the 1960 correspondence prompted by a citizen's protest against the hoax label (with a reply from the Air Force and a letter from the CIA's legislative counsel to Rep. Gordon Scherer), an internal memo on whether to release the ONI report to NICAP, a galley proof of the Trindade chapter of Menzel and Boyd's *The World of Flying Saucers*, and the APRO Bulletins of March and May 1960 with Olavo Fontes's case for the photographs.

Our copy is the one published by The Black Vault in its “From the Desks of Project Blue Book” collection (pages stamped with Rob Mercer's name), not the National Archives microfilm. The scans are poor: pages 2, 26, 28–30, 34–36, 44 and 47–52 of our text were re-OCR'd at 300 dpi; the rest is the PDF's own text layer.

*Sources: Project Blue Book file, Trindade Island, 16 January 1958, via The Black Vault; ONI Information Report 39-58 (U.S. Naval Attaché, Rio de Janeiro, 11 March 1958)*
