A Ministry of Defence file of the UFO desk's Parliamentary questions and correspondence from September 1995 to October 1996, opened at The National Archives in August 2010. The year was busy: a former desk officer's book was about to appear, and Martin Redmond MP tabled a run of questions on UFOs and on Rendlesham Forest. The file holds the desk's drafts and background notes, which restate the Ministry's position on the Halt memo, with the memo itself attached more than once.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 24/1983/1; page numbers are those of the Archives' PDF.*
