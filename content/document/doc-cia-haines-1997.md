A history of the CIA's involvement with UFOs from the late 1940s to 1990, written by Gerald K. Haines, then the National Reconnaissance Office historian, and published in the CIA's journal *Studies in Intelligence* in 1997. It was prompted by DCI R. James Woolsey's 1993 order to review all Agency files on UFOs.

It is the clearest official acknowledgement that the U.S. government knowingly misled the public about UFO sightings. Citing CIA officials from the U-2 and OXCART programs, it says secret reconnaissance flights accounted for over half of UFO reports from the late 1950s through the 1960s, and that the Air Force made "misleading and deceptive statements" to protect them, a deception that "added fuel" to later cover-up theories. It also concludes that the CIA itself paid only peripheral attention to UFOs after the early 1950s, which believers in a deeper cover-up dispute.

*Sources: CIA, Studies in Intelligence (1997), our copy of the CIA PDF*
