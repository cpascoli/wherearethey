AARO's annual report to Congress for May 2023 to June 2024, released on 14 November 2024 with a press briefing by its director, Jon Kosloski. It counts 757 new reports, resolves every closed case to ordinary objects, states that AARO has found no evidence of extraterrestrial beings, activity or technology, and notes 21 cases that merit further analysis. A section headed “AARO Possesses No Data to Indicate the Capture or Exploitation of UAP” says AARO is formalising a process should UAP material ever be captured.

*Source: Department of Defense, AARO, November 2024 (also posted by ODNI)*
