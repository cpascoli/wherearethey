The Cabinet's written answer (内閣参質一六八第八四号) to a question submitted under Article 74 of the Diet Act by Ryuji Yamane, a Democratic Party member of the House of Councillors. The answer denies that the government has confirmed any UFO “from outside the Earth”, says the Air Self-Defense Force knows of no scramble that found one, and states that the government had done no study of the subject.

The answer concerns objects from beyond Earth, the wording of the question; it does not address unidentified objects in general, which the Defense Ministry began recording in 2020. Our copy is a PDF capture of the question and the answer as published on the House of Councillors website.
