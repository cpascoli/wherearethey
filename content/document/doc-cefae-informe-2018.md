The Argentine Air Force's CEFAE annual report for 2018, published on argentina.gob.ar. Besides the year's 21 resolved cases, it contains a detailed re-investigation of the 31 July 1995 Bariloche case. The report reconstructs both aircraft's flight paths and draws on the tower recordings, Gendarmería commander Cipuzak's report and drawing, and new interviews with the 727's copilot and with the man who operated a discotheque searchlight that night.

*Sources: Fuerza Aérea Argentina, CEFAE (2018)*
