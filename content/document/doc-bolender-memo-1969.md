facts:
  - text: An internal Air Force memo, “Unidentified Flying Objects (UFO)”, dated October 20, 1969, recommending the end of Project Blue Book. It states that continuing the project “cannot be justified, either on the ground of national security or in the interest of science,” citing the Condon (University of Colorado) study and the National Academy of Sciences review.
    status: documented
    source: library/docs/doc-bolender-memo-1969.pdf
  - text: The key passage says that “reports of unidentified flying objects which could affect national security are made in accordance with JANAP 146 or Air Force Manual 55-11, and are not part of the Blue Book system,” meaning that security-relevant reports went through separate channels that did not end with Blue Book.
    status: documented
    source: library/docs/doc-bolender-memo-1969.pdf
  - text: Released under FOIA to researcher Robert Todd in January 1979. NICAP notes that similar statements appear in Blue Book memos going back to 1958–59.
