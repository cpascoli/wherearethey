The slides AARO director Jon Kosloski used at the open Senate Armed Services subcommittee hearing of 19 November 2024: reporting trends and resolution outcomes since 1996, and three resolved cases, the 2013 Puerto Rico (Aguadilla) object, the Navy's GoFast video and the 2018 Mount Etna video. They are the written basis for what he said at the hearing, including the Etna distance he spoke of as 170 metres but which the slide gives as 170 kilometres.

*Source: AARO, November 2024 (aaro.mil)*
