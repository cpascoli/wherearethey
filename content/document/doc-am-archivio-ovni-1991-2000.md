The Italian Air Force's published table of the OVNI reports it holds from 1991 to 2000, the sequel to the 1972–1990 table, one page per report (some 110). Each gives the place, date, time, shape, colour, direction, speed, motion, altitude, weather and who made the report, then the finding ("riscontri"). Most entries up to 1997 carry the same finding as the earlier table: on the basis of the data in the archive, the event was catalogued as O.V.N.I. Most later ones say that the data gathered from the Air Force's offices showed no link with known activity or phenomena, so the event is catalogued as O.V.N.I.

The table names no witnesses. As in the earlier table, "O.V.N.I." records that no explanation was found, not extraordinary performance or origin; some entries name the likely cause (a firework, a planetary conjunction) and still catalogue the event as O.V.N.I.

*Sources: Aeronautica Militare, Archivio OVNI periodo 1991–2000 (uploaded May 2023)*
