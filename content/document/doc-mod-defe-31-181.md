A file of DI55, the Defence Intelligence Staff branch that looked at UFO reports for anything of defence interest, covering February 1992 to April 1993 and opened at The National Archives in March 2009. It is mostly copies of what the UFO desk and RAF West Drayton sent the branch: signals, report forms, letters from the public and press cuttings, with the names of witnesses redacted. The branch's own comments are rare.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 31/181/1; page numbers are those of the Archives' PDF (the March 2009 highlights guide's numbers run one higher).*
