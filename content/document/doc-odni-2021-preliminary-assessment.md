The first unclassified U.S. intelligence-community assessment of UAP, written in response to the FY2021 Intelligence Authorization Act. Its plain admission that almost all cases were *unexplained*, and that some showed apparent advanced technology, marked the shift from ridicule to official acknowledgement.
