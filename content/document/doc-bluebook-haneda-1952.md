The Project Blue Book case file on the sighting of 5–6 August 1952 at Haneda Air Force Base, Tokyo, held by the National Archives in its sanitized version of the Blue Book case files. Our copy is the 15 page images from the NARA catalog, OCR'd for search.

It contains the record card, Far East Air Forces intelligence report IR-35-52 with the statement of control tower operator A/3C Lloyd West and his drawing of the object, the statements of the Shiroi GCI controllers (1st Lt. Henry E. Albert, 1st Lt. Melvin E. Rawlins) and of the F-94 crew (1st Lt. Wesley R. Holder and 1st Lt. Aaron Jones), the 35th Fighter-Interceptor Wing's report, and Blue Book's later analysis notes.

*Source: U.S. National Archives, Project Blue Book (local copy)*
