The transcript of a presentation that Capt. Edward J. Ruppelt, project engineer of the Air Force's reorganised UFO investigation at the Air Technical Intelligence Center, gave on 26 March 1952, released by the Department of War in its sixth PURSUE release (September 2026) together with the audio recording. Ruppelt went on to lead Project Blue Book.

He reviews the history since Kenneth Arnold's sighting, the specialists of the early studies and their conclusions, the reopening of the project in October 1951, the Lubbock lights photographs, radar cases and the Mantell crash, and the plans for diffraction-grating cameras and radar-scope photography. The transcript is the Department's own; our copy is its PDF.

*Sources: Department of War, PURSUE release 06 (2026)*
