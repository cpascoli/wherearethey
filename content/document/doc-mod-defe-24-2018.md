A Ministry of Defence registered file of the UFO desk, Secretariat (Air Staff), covering Parliamentary questions and correspondence from December 1997 to October 1998, opened at The National Archives on 3 March 2011 in the release of 35 files. Names of members of the public are redacted.

Among its papers is the MoD's handling of the Howden Moor incident of 24 March 1997, a year after the event: the questions tabled by Helen Jackson MP after David Clarke's enquiry, the answer and the note drafted for the minister, and the RAF search-and-rescue log that the answer was built on.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 24/2018 (our copy is from a public mirror of the March 2011 release; page numbers are those of the PDF)*
