The two-page memorandum from the Deputy Secretary of Defense, dated 15 July 2022 and published by the Department on 20 July, that turned the Airborne Object Identification and Management Synchronization Group (AOIMSG) into the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office. It directs the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security to stand up AARO, renames the AOIMSG's executive council, and orders the Navy to wind down the UAP Task Force and hand its data to the new office.
