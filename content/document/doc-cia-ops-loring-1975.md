A one-page extract from the CIA Operations Center's log for 28 October 1975, approved for release in 1977 (document C00020552 in the CIA reading room). It shows the National Military Command Center passing word of the Loring AFB intrusion to the CIA the morning after the first reported night. Everything else on the page is blanked.
