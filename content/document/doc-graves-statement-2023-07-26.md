Former Navy F/A-18 pilot Ryan Graves's written testimony for the House Oversight hearing of 26 July 2023 opens with his conclusion: "advanced UAP are a national security and an aviation safety problem." He makes three points: UAP are routine but grossly under-reported, the stigma silences pilots, and the government knows more than it shares, noting that since 2021 all UAP videos have been classified secret or above.

He describes how, after a radar upgrade in 2014, his squadron VFA-11 began detecting unknown objects off Virginia Beach, confirmed them with infrared sensors, and saw them so often they became part of daily briefs. In the key incident, two jets entering training area W-72 had to take evasive action around "a dark gray cube inside of a clear sphere — motionless against the wind", and the squadron's safety report drew no response. The objects, he writes, could reach Mach 1, hold position against hurricane-force winds and stay aloft all day, with no visible lift, control surfaces or propulsion.

He relays Rear Adm. Tim Gallaudet's account of a 2015 "Urgent safety of flight issue" email with the GoFast video that vanished from recipients' systems, and describes his Americans for Safe Aerospace programme, through which more than 30 witnesses, mostly commercial pilots, have come forward.
