*The UFO Evidence* is the report the civilian group NICAP published in May 1964, edited by its staff member Richard H. Hall: a survey of the sightings NICAP judged unexplained, in sections by who saw them (Air Force; Army, Navy and Marine Corps; pilots; scientists and engineers; officials and citizens), with sections on special evidence, the Air Force investigation, foreign reports and Congress. Its front matter lists NICAP's director, Maj. Donald Keyhoe, and a Board of Governors that included Dewey Fournet, a former Air Force headquarters monitor of Project Blue Book, and Rear Adm. H. B. Knowles.

It is an advocacy document, not an official one. The board's statement argues that the residual unexplained cases are probably artificial objects under intelligent control, and that the Air Force had played the evidence down; one board member, the astronomer Charles Olivier, took no position on what UFOs were and backed NICAP only to press for open scientific study. The Air Force's own position is in its Project Blue Book file; the University of Colorado study it commissioned in 1966 concluded that “nothing has come from the study of UFOs in the past 21 years that has added to scientific knowledge”.

Our copy is the CIA's: a scan of the printed report released through the CIA's FOIA reading room in 2001 (record CIA-RDP81R00560R000100010001-0), OCR'd for search.
