Volume 1 of the Royal New Zealand Air Force's file on the Kaikōura sightings of December 1978, declassified and released by the New Zealand Defence Force in December 2010. It contains the RNZAF's witness interviews, a public statement on its conclusions, DSIR technical reports (including an image analysis of TV1 film), and independent studies, among them the NZ UFO Studies Centre's report. Our copy is the scan preserved on the Internet Archive; the OCR text is imperfect.

*Sources: RNZAF file AIR 1080/6/897 (released by NZDF, 2010)*
