Two telegrams from the U.S. Embassy in Kuwait to the State Department, both dated 29 January 1979 and signed by Ambassador Frank Maestrone, as preserved in NARA's Central Foreign Policy Files (Record Group 59, Electronic Telegrams 1979) and served by its Access to Archival Databases; both carry the State Department's declassification marking of 20 March 2014. KUWAIT 0486 summarises the Kuwaiti government's scientific committee and its report on the sightings of November and December 1978 and relays an oil company official's account of the pumping equipment; section 1 of KUWAIT 0508 explains why Kuwait's air defence radars did not see them.

The KISR committee's report itself, and the Kuwaiti press coverage the cable mentions, are not on file here.

*Sources: National Archives, RG 59 Central Foreign Policy Files, Electronic Telegrams 1979 (AAD), KUWAIT 0486 and KUWAIT 0508*
