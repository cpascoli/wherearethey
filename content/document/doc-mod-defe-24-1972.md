A Ministry of Defence file opened at The National Archives in August 2009, on the stories that circulated from 1992 about the loss of an RAF Lightning and its American pilot, Capt. William Schaffner, off Flamborough Head on 8 September 1970. It holds the Grimsby Evening Telegraph's series on the “Riddle of Foxtrot 94”, letters from UFO researchers, the UFO desk's research into the Board of Inquiry papers, a summary of the Aircraft Accident Report and a 1994 RAF flight-safety article, “Don't believe everything you read!”.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 24/1972 (our copy is The Black Vault's mirror)*
