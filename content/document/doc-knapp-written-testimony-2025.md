George Knapp's seven-page written statement for the 9 September 2025 House hearing on UAP. It covers his reporting on Bob Lazar, AAWSAP and the Russian UFO files he brought back in the early 1990s, including the October 1982 Ukrainian missile-base case.

His account of that case goes further than the 1982 witness statements and the 2000 paper by the Soviet programme's coordinators. Those describe lights in the sky and a warning indicator that lit for some tens of seconds, which the coordinators judged a coincidence.
