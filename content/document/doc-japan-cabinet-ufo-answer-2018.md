The Cabinet's written answer (内閣衆質一九六第八四号) to a question from Seiji Osaka, a Constitutional Democratic Party member of the House of Representatives, prompted by the December 2017 reports of the Pentagon's AATIP program. It repeats the 2007 position that the government has never confirmed a UFO from outside the Earth, and declines to comment on the U.S. program or on any sharing of information with Washington.

Two years later, in September 2020, Defense Minister Taro Kono ordered the Self-Defense Forces to record and report unidentifiable objects in the air. Our copy is a PDF capture of the question and the answer as published on the House of Representatives website.

*Sources: House of Representatives of Japan (2018)*
