The Indian government's written answer in Parliament on the object seen over Imphal airport on 19 November 2023. Asked by Manish Tewari MP for the details, whether the airport was closed and whether the object was a danger to aircraft, the Minister of State for Civil Aviation set out the timeline from the air traffic control record, the flights diverted and held, and the NOTAM, and said that any unidentified object near an airport is a danger because its movements are unpredictable.

It describes only a white object that hovered near the control tower and was visible until shortly before sunset. It does not say what the object was.

*Sources: Lok Sabha, Unstarred Question No. 2024, answered 14 December 2023 (Ministry of Civil Aviation)*
