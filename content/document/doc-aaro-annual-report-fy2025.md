AARO's annual report to Congress for June 2024 to May 2025, issued under the Department of War name and published on 20 July 2026, months after the statutory deadline. Every resolved case is attributed to ordinary objects, and for the first time the report states that no evidence suggests any government or private entity has ever captured or exploited UAP-derived material. It also records reports from sensitive sites that, if validated, could be "a currently unmitigated threat vector", and a Navy report of about 100 airborne objects off Virginia still under investigation.

*Source: Department of War, AARO, July 2026; our copy is the original PDF, also mirrored by The Black Vault*
