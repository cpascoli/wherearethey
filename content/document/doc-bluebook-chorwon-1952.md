The Project Blue Book case files on the sightings of 31 May 1952 just south of Chorwon (Cheorwon), Korea, held by the National Archives in its sanitized version of the Blue Book case files: “Chorwon, Korea, May 1952” (20 pages) and “Korea, May 1952” (3 pages). Our copies are the page images from the NARA catalog, OCR'd for search.

They contain the record cards, the reports of Detachment 1, 6004th Air Intelligence Service Squadron (52-93 and 52-94, two airmen on guard duty at a radar site; 52-106, the F-94 crew of the 319th Fighter-Interceptor Squadron), the airmen's sketches and statements, Far East Air Forces comments, and a FEAF compilation of earlier Korean sightings, including F-94 crews chasing and shooting down lighted balloons in May 1952.

*Source: U.S. National Archives, Project Blue Book (local copy)*
