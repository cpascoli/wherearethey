A static-looking light southeast of Valencia, flashing green, red and white, was later found to follow the movement of the sky quite faithfully."

The Spanish Air Force's file on the Manises incident of 11 November 1979, together with the related events of 17 November (an Albacete Mirage F1 scramble) and 28 November (lights and radar returns over Madrid). It was declassified in 1994 and published by the Ministry of Defence in the Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa under a CC BY 4.0 licence ("Fuente: Biblioteca Central del Ejército del Aire y del Espacio / Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa"). Witness names are blanked in the released copy. The OCR text is imperfect; quotations here were checked against the page images.

*Sources: Ejército del Aire, Mando Operativo Aéreo, expediente 791111/791117/791128 (Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa)*
