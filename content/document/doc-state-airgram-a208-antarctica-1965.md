A one-page confidential State Department airgram from the U.S. Embassy in Buenos Aires, signed for the ambassador by Albert Mayio, counsellor for commercial and economic affairs, dated 4 September 1965. It was released by the National Security Agency in its UFO document collection; our copy is the NSA's PDF as archived by the Wayback Machine. The scan is poor, and the text was read by OCR and checked against the image.

It shows that the U.S. government followed the 1965 Deception Island sightings through its naval attaché, and it records the La Plata Observatory's remark that Echo II was passing over the Antarctic at the time.

*Sources: NSA UFO release, airgram A-208 (1965)*
