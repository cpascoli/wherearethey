The second joint annual report to Congress on UAP by the Department of Defense and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, drafted by AARO, covering reports received from 31 August 2022 to 30 April 2023. It counts 291 new reports (801 in all), finds no confirmed health effects and no attribution to foreign actors, and attributes the unresolved remainder mainly to gaps in sensor data.

*Source: ODNI and DoD, October 2023 (aaro.mil)*
