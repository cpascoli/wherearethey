Two files of the Ministry of Defence's UFO desk, Secretariat (Air Staff) 2a, mostly letters from the public and researchers, largely of 1988–89 with some older reports, and the MoD's replies, opened at The National Archives in the first release of MoD UFO files on 20 October 2008. Names of members of the public and witnesses are redacted.

They hold the papers on the 1957 Manston intercept: Milton Torres's narrative, his wingman's differing account, the solicitor's questions about an electronic-warfare experiment, an MoD memo on a newspaper enquiry, and the report that the U.S. Air Force had no record of the event.

*Source: The National Archives (UK), DEFE 24/1931 and DEFE 24/1942, released October 2008; our copies are from The Black Vault's mirror of that release, and the page numbers are those of the PDFs.*
