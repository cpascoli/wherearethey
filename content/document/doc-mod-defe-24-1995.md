A Ministry of Defence file of the UFO desk's correspondence for the second half of 1997, opened at The National Archives in August 2010. Among the letters from the public is a sustained challenge over Rendlesham Forest: a correspondent who sent the desk the first detailed accounts by Jim Penniston and Charles Halt, published that year, and asked the Ministry to show what it had actually investigated in 1981. The reply restated the air-defence remit and the absence of corroboration for Halt's memo. The accounts themselves, abridged from a magazine article, are thus on the MoD's file in the witnesses' own words.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 24/1995/1; page numbers are those of the Archives' PDF.*
