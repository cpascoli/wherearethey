*Warp Drive, Dark Energy, and the Manipulation of Extra Dimensions* (April 2010) is one of the 38 Defense Intelligence Reference Documents commissioned under the DIA's AAWSAP programme.

According to AARO's official summary in PURSUE release 06, it asks whether warp propulsion could be grounded in known theoretical physics by linking general-relativistic warp metrics with dark energy, Casimir effects and higher-dimensional models from string theory and brane cosmology. It hypothesises that if dark energy arises from vacuum effects originating in extra dimensions, a future technology able to manipulate those dimensions might alter local spacetime expansion to create a warp bubble, reducing the enormous energy demands of earlier warp models.

AARO notes that the concept "relies entirely on unverified assumptions" about the reality and controllability of extra dimensions, and that the paper "offers no viable engineering pathway toward a functioning propulsion system."
