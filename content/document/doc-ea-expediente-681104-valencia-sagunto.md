  - text: "The file summarises that at 18:23Z on 4 November 1968 Iberia flight IB-249, a Caravelle from Barcelona to Alicante, told Barcelona Control it had in sight a very large light with two small side lights, which descended, went out, rose and lit again, and finally went out and was lost towards the Mediterranean. Barcelona had no radar contact, as the estimated position was beyond its range."

The Spanish Air Force file on the lights reported by the crew of Iberia flight IB-249 between Valencia and Sagunto on 4 November 1968, declassified in 1992. It contains the Air Operations Command intelligence summary and the transcript of the crew's exchange with Barcelona Control. Published in the Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa under CC BY 4.0 ("Fuente: Biblioteca Central del Ejército del Aire y del Espacio / Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa").
