David Grusch's written opening and closing statements for the House Oversight hearing of 26 July 2023. He introduces himself as an intelligence officer for 14 years, a USAF major and from 2021 to 2023 a GS-15 at the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, where he co-led UAP analysis, and as a member of the UAP Task Force from 2019 to 2021 for the National Reconnaissance Office, tasked with identifying the special access programmes relevant to its mission.

His central claim: "I was informed, in the course of my official duties, of a multi-decade UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering program to which I was denied access", which he reported "to my superiors and multiple Inspectors General", after which he says he suffered retaliation. He stresses that his testimony rests on information from individuals with long records of service, some of whom shared photographs, documents and classified oral testimony, and that he is "speaking to the facts as I have been told them."

The closing statement refers to "the Non-Human Reverse Engineering Programs I have reported". AARO's 2024 historical review found no evidence for such programmes.
