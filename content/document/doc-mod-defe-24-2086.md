The Ministry of Defence's file on the wave of sightings across south-west England, Wales and the Midlands on the night of 30–31 March 1993, opened at The National Archives in August 2009. It holds police and RAF Police reports, among them the RAF Police patrol at RAF Cosford, a Meteorological Office observer's report from RAF Shawbury, the UFO desk's radar checks, its minutes to the Assistant Chief of the Air Staff, the discovery that a Russian rocket had re-entered that night, and correspondence with civilian investigators.

*Source: The National Archives, DEFE 24/2086 (our copy is The Black Vault's mirror)*
