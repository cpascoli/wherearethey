GEPAN's Technical Note 17, dated Toulouse, 21 March 1983, is the official report on the "Amarante" case of 21 October 1982: a biologist's account of a small oval object hovering for twenty minutes over his garden in a suburb of a city in eastern France, followed by withered amaranth plants beside where it had been. It reproduces the gendarmerie statement and telex, and gives the reconstruction of the observation, a study of the witness, the sampling of the plants, weather, sky and air-traffic checks, military radar data, a physicist's note on the grass and the plant biochemistry.

The note keeps the witness anonymous ("Monsieur HENRI") and codes the place names. Its findings are mixed: the radar recordings show nothing notable, and the plant analyses could not tell the withered amaranths from the control, the analysts pointing to frost the day before and to poor sample keeping; on the other hand GEPAN found no reason to reject the testimony of a single, well-placed witness.
