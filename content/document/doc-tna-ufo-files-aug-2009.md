The National Archives' guide to the batch of Ministry of Defence UFO files (DEFE 24) it opened in August 2009, with the key stories bookmarked by the historian David Clarke. It is a finding aid, not the files themselves: it gives the file and page references where the official papers on each case can be read.

For the Belgian wave it points to two official records held in British files: the Belgian Air Force's statement sent to the MoD in November 1993, and General Wilfried De Brouwer's four-page account of the March 1990 F-16 intercepts, which confirms radar lock-ons the pilots could not explain.

*Source: The National Archives (UK), August 2009*
