The House Oversight Subcommittee on Cybersecurity, Information Technology, and Government Innovation, chaired by Rep. Nancy Mace. It is in the archive because it co-hosted, with the National Security subcommittee chaired by Rep. Glenn Grothman, the November 2024 hearing “Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena: Exposing the Truth”.

The witnesses were Rear Adm. Tim Gallaudet, who described a 2015 “URGENT SAFETY OF FLIGHT ISSUE” email that later vanished; Luis Elizondo, who testified that “UAP are real”; the journalist Michael Shellenberger, who delivered the “Immaculate Constellation” whistleblower report; and the former NASA official Michael Gold. The hearing, its recording and its transcript are filed separately.

*Sources: House Oversight hearing (13 November 2024)*
