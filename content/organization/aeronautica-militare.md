Italy's air force. Since the 1978 sighting wave it has collected and checked reports of unidentified flying objects (OVNI); today its Reparto Generale Sicurezza (RGS) does this work. Reports are checked against air traffic, air-defence radar and weather data. An event is catalogued "O.V.N.I." when no technical or natural explanation is found. The Air Force publishes summary tables of past reports.

*Sources: Aeronautica Militare OVNI page*
