The Senate Select Committee on Intelligence oversees the U.S. intelligence agencies. It is in the archive because whistleblowers say they took their UAP claims to it. According to David Grusch, the Inspector General's summary of his whistleblower complaint was sent to it and to its House counterpart in 2022, and he testified that he gave the locations of recovered material to the intelligence committees and the Inspector General.

Chris Mellon, a former minority staff director of the committee, later worked to bring the Navy UAP videos to public attention. Jake Barber says he went to the committee's staff, who “don't question it for one second”. Luis Elizondo says the members on the intelligence committees who should be briefed often are not. Remote viewer Joe McMoneagle says he gave a demonstration to a Senate intelligence subcommittee.

The committee has published nothing on file confirming these accounts; they are what the witnesses say.

*Sources: The Debrief (2023) · July 26, 2023 hearing · Jake Barber with Jesse Michels · Luis Elizondo with Jillian Michaels*
