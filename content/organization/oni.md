The Office of Naval Intelligence, the U.S. Navy's intelligence service, ran the UAP Task Force from 2020 with the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security, after Navy pilots' reports and videos drew attention to UAP. Scott Bray, then Deputy Director of Naval Intelligence, testified at the May 2022 House hearing.

*Sources: [U.S. Navy (2020)](https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/Press-Releases/display-pressreleases/Article/2314280/)*
