The Office of Naval Intelligence is the U.S. Navy's intelligence service. It is in the archive because it ran the UAP Task Force from 2020 with the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security, after Navy pilots' reports and videos drew attention to UAP.

At the May 2022 House hearing Scott Bray, then Deputy Director of Naval Intelligence, testified that in August 2020 Deputy Secretary of Defense Norquist directed the establishment of the task force within the Department of the Navy, building on the Navy's response to aviators' reports from training ranges. Jay Stratton, who also worked at ONI, was the task force's first director. Its work produced the ODNI's Preliminary Assessment of June 2021, and its duties passed to the AOIMSG in November 2021.

Nimitz pilot Alex Dietrich says Pentagon officials from ONI, AATIP and the task force have since assured her that her first suspicion, a missile fired on a live range, was eliminated.
