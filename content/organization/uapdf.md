The UAP Disclosure Fund, later the Disclosure Foundation, is an advocacy organisation pressing for public disclosure of UAP information. It is in the archive as the organiser of two events on Capitol Hill.

It hosted the congressional briefing “Understanding UAP: Science, National Security & Innovation” on 1 May 2025, sponsored by the House Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets: a briefing, not a formal hearing, with no sworn testimony. Speakers included Avi Loeb, Tim Gallaudet, Chris Mellon and Kirk McConnell. On 25 June 2026 it organised an all-day public forum in the Senate's Kennedy Caucus Room, whose national security panel it recorded and published. There Chris Mellon said the Foundation offers free legal advice to potential whistleblowers.

It is an advocacy group, not a source of evidence; what its speakers say is filed under each of them.
