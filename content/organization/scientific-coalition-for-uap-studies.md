The Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies is a U.S. non-profit of scientists, engineers and investigators who apply scientific methods to UAP cases and publish their analyses. Its best-known work is a long forensic report on the 2004 Nimitz encounter, distilled into the 2019 peer-reviewed *Entropy* paper by Kevin Knuth, Robert Powell and Peter Reali, and earlier reports on the 2008 Stephenville lights and the 2013 Aguadilla video, both co-authored by Powell. It holds an annual conference (Toronto, July 2026) and streams talks and panels.

Its members are mostly private researchers rather than witnesses, and its reports are its own analyses; where official findings exist they can differ. AARO attributed the Aguadilla object to balloons or sky lanterns.

*Sources: SCU website (about, team); the Entropy paper (2019); SCU panel on the Nimitz paper (2021); AARO's findings as reported by DefenseScoop (2024)*
