The U.S. Department of State handles American diplomacy; its cables and reports from embassies abroad sometimes record UAP sightings reported by foreign governments or by U.S. personnel overseas. It contributed 9 records to the PURSUE releases, linked from the Index, and a State Department series is held in the National Archives' Record Group 615.

Two cases on file rest on its cables. In January 1994 the U.S. Embassy in Dushanbe reported a Tajik Air 747 crew's sighting over Kazakhstan, adding: “We have no opinion and report the above for what it may be worth.” In November 1963, asked to verify a Brazilian radio report of a crashed sphere at Conde, Bahia, the U.S. Consul in Salvador replied that the story was “apparently fabricated in Rio”.

The cables relay what others reported; the department offers no assessment of its own.

*Sources: PURSUE records · State Department cable, Dushanbe (1994) · NASC files on the Conde, Bahia report (1963)*
