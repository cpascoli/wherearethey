The Intelligence Community Inspector General investigates complaints and whistleblower disclosures across the U.S. intelligence agencies. It is in the archive because the best-known UAP whistleblower complaints went to it.

In May 2022 David Grusch filed a PPD-19 urgent-concern complaint with the ICIG, alleging that information on UAP crash-retrieval programmes was being withheld from Congress. According to Grusch and *The Debrief*, the ICIG found it “credible and urgent” in July 2022. Under oath in July 2023 he said he had given the exact locations to the Inspector General, and in 2026 that he brought witnesses there who had shown him video and photos; he also says it was “back-channeled to me unofficially” that the White House told the office to stop its investigation. Dylan Borland says he filed with the ICIG and AARO in 2023, and that his complaint was found “credible, not urgent”.

All of this comes from the complainants. The ICIG has not published its determinations, and AARO later said it found no verifiable evidence of such programmes.

*Sources: The Debrief (2023) · House Oversight hearing (26 July 2023) · Dr. Phil with David Grusch (2026) · Dylan Borland with Jesse Michels*
