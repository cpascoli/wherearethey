The Intelligence Community Inspector General investigates complaints and whistleblower disclosures across the U.S. intelligence agencies. In May 2022 David Grusch filed his complaint about withheld UAP programmes with the ICIG, which, according to Grusch, found it "credible and urgent" in July 2022; the ICIG has not published its determination.

*Sources: The Debrief (2023) · House Oversight hearing (26 July 2023)*
