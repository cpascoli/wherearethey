The National Archives at Kew is the official archive of the UK government. It holds the Ministry of Defence's records of UFO reports, kept since the 1960s (earlier material was destroyed after five years), including the Rendlesham Forest correspondence (DEFE 24/1948/1). The MoD's UFO files were released there between 2008 and 2013, with the historian David Clarke as consultant. Some material remains closed, such as the witness details in the Calvine papers.

*Sources: [UK National Archives — UFO reports](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/)*
