The Central Intelligence Agency is the main U.S. foreign-intelligence agency. Its UAP history includes the Robertson Panel, the scientific panel it convened in January 1953 after the Washington sightings, which recommended debunking public interest in UFOs, and funding for the Stargate remote-viewing research.

The CIA contributed 23 records to the PURSUE releases, and its declassified reading room holds many earlier documents; those filed here are linked below.
