Japan's Ministry of Defense oversees the Self-Defense Forces. It is in the archive for the directive Defense Minister Taro Kono issued in September 2020, ordering the forces to record, photograph and analyse unidentified aerial objects, a step he said was prompted by the Pentagon's release of Navy UAP videos that April.

At his press conference of 15 September 2020, published by the ministry, Kono said the directive concerns objects that cannot be identified, not objects from space, and that he had discussed the issue with U.S. Defense Secretary Mark Esper in Guam. Two years earlier, in 2018, the Cabinet had answered in parliament that the government had never confirmed the existence of objects from outside the Earth. Luis Elizondo later cited Japan as reaching out to share UAP information with the U.S.

Kono also set limits. He said there was no record of the SDF ever photographing such an object, and called the directive a down-to-earth intelligence effort, with drones on his mind.
