The Office of the Director of National Intelligence oversees the U.S. intelligence community. It published the *Preliminary Assessment: Unidentified Aerial Phenomena* in June 2021, the first unclassified government report on UAP, co-published AARO's 2023 annual report, issued Intelligence Community Directive 906 on controlled access programmes, and supports the Department of War's PURSUE releases.

The 2021 assessment covered 144 reports from U.S. government sources and identified one, a large deflating balloon: “The others remain unexplained.” Its one PURSUE record, ODNI-UAP-D001, is a senior intelligence official's first-hand account of orbs at a western military facility in 2025. Tim Gallaudet says an ODNI memo now waives non-disclosure agreements for people reporting UAP information to PURSUE or AARO, and Rep. Anna Paulina Luna says ODNI briefings have opened up to her task force.

Its handling of whistleblowers is also contested. Dylan Borland says that, according to Jeremy Corbell, an ODNI official discussed his testimony with Corbell and attributed to him a drawing he says he never made.
