The Office of the Director of National Intelligence oversees the U.S. intelligence community. It published the *Preliminary Assessment: Unidentified Aerial Phenomena* in June 2021, the first unclassified government report on UAP, issued Intelligence Community Directive 906 on controlled access programmes, and supports the Department of War's PURSUE releases.

*Sources: ODNI Preliminary Assessment (2021) · [war.gov/UFO](https://www.war.gov/UFO/)*
