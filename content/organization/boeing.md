Boeing is one of the largest U.S. aerospace and defence manufacturers.

In this archive it is named in allegations, not confirmed by any official record, that major contractors hold retrieved UAP material as part of "legacy" programmes. AARO's 2024 historical review reported that executives of the companies named by interviewees met its director and denied this on the record. Separately, Boeing contractors were among the witnesses of the 2003 "Red Square" incursions at Vandenberg Air Force Base.

*Sources: AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024)*
