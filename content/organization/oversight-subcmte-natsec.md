The House Oversight Subcommittee on National Security, the Border, and Foreign Affairs, chaired by Rep. Glenn Grothman. It is in the archive because it held the July 2023 hearing that put UAP whistleblower testimony under oath, and co-hosted the November 2024 hearing with Rep. Nancy Mace's subcommittee.

At the July 2023 hearing, “UAP: Implications on National Security, Public Safety, and Government Transparency”, Cmdr. David Fravor described the 2004 Nimitz “Tic Tac” encounter, Ryan Graves described regular encounters by his F/A-18 squadron off Virginia Beach, and David Grusch testified that he had been told of “a multi-decade UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering program”. The November 2024 hearing heard Tim Gallaudet, Luis Elizondo, Michael Shellenberger and Michael Gold.

What the witnesses said is testimony. AARO's position, given to the Senate in 2023 and 2024, is that it has found no credible or verifiable evidence of extraterrestrial technology.
