The Federal Bureau of Investigation, the U.S. domestic law-enforcement and counter-intelligence agency, collected "flying disc" reports from the late 1940s. Its files include the 1950 Guy Hottel memo and an investigation of the "Majestic 12" documents, both filed here.

The FBI is the second-largest contributor to the PURSUE releases, with 104 records so far.

*Sources: FBI Vault · PURSUE records*
