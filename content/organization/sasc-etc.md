The Senate Armed Services Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and Capabilities, chaired by Senator Kirsten Gillibrand, with Senator Joni Ernst as ranking member in 2024. It has held the Senate's public hearings with AARO's leadership; Gillibrand led the FY2022 NDAA amendment that created the office.

On 19 April 2023 it heard AARO's founding director Sean Kirkpatrick, who said “AARO has found no credible evidence thus far of extraterrestrial activity, off world technology, or objects that defy the known laws of physics”, while some cases stay unresolved for lack of data. Gillibrand opened by citing “strong evidence of advanced technology” in objects seen by service members. On 19 November 2024 it heard his successor, Jon Kosloski, who said AARO held over 1,600 reports and had “not discovered any verifiable evidence of extraterrestrial beings, activity or technology”; Gillibrand asked AARO to share what it could on Nimitz and Gimbal.

David Grusch told the House in July 2023 that Kirkpatrick's statement was “not accurate”.
