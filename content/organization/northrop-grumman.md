Northrop Grumman is a major U.S. defence contractor that builds advanced, classified and unconventional-looking aircraft such as the B-21 Raider stealth bomber and the X-47B uncrewed demonstrator, which are sometimes mistaken for UAP.

It is also named in allegations, not confirmed by any official record, that major contractors hold retrieved UAP material as part of "legacy" programmes; AARO's 2024 historical review reported that executives of the companies named by interviewees denied this on the record.

*Sources: [Northrop Grumman](https://www.northropgrumman.com/what-we-do/air/b-21-raider) · AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024)*
