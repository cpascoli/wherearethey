NASA is the U.S. civilian space agency. It commissioned an independent study team on UAP that reported in 2023; people linked to it here include the Apollo 14 astronaut Edgar Mitchell and Michael Gold, a member of that study team.

NASA contributed 40 records to the PURSUE releases: documents, mission audio recordings and images.
