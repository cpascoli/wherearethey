The U.S. Department of Defense, styled the Department of War since Executive Order 14347 of September 2025 made that an authorised secondary title, oversees the armed forces and most U.S. government UAP work: the Air Force's Projects Sign and Blue Book, the DIA's AAWSAP, the Navy's UAP Task Force, and today's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO).

It runs PURSUE, the public release of declassified UAP files at war.gov/UFO, and is by far its largest contributor, with 284 of the 478 records released through release 07 (October 2026).
