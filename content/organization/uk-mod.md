The United Kingdom's Ministry of Defence, and before 1964 the Air Ministry, was the government department that received and assessed reports of unidentified flying objects for some sixty years. A Flying Saucer Working Party reported in 1951 that all sightings could be explained; afterwards reports were filed by the Air Ministry and MoD secretariats and copied to the Defence Intelligence Staff, whose branch DI55 produced the Project Condign study of “unidentified aerial phenomena” in 2000.

The MoD closed its UFO desk in 2009 and transferred its UFO files to The National Archives, which opened them in ten releases between 2008 and 2013.

*Sources: The National Archives' highlights guides (2011, 2013)*
