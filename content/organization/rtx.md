RTX, formerly Raytheon Technologies, is a major U.S. defence and aerospace contractor that builds radars, sensors and missiles, including the systems through which military aircrew detect many UAP.

It is named in allegations, not confirmed by any official record, that major contractors hold retrieved UAP material as part of "legacy" programmes; AARO's 2024 historical review reported that executives of the companies named by interviewees denied this on the record.

*Sources: AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024)*
