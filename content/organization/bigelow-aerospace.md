Bigelow Aerospace is the Las Vegas space-habitat company founded by the hotel entrepreneur Robert Bigelow in 1999. Its subsidiary BAASS (Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies) was the contractor for the Defense Intelligence Agency's AAWSAP program from 2008 to 2012: the DIA's solicitation and original order of September 2008, BAASS's Project Management Plan, its monthly reports, its Utah Ranch Investigation Report of 2010 and its self-assessment of November 2010 are all in the PURSUE releases. Hal Puthoff, whose EarthTech was a BAASS subcontractor, says the company won an open broad area announcement; George Knapp says it hired 50 investigators within weeks, built a database of more than 240,000 cases and made the 2004 Nimitz "Tic Tac" its first case. The biochemist Colm Kelleher worked on its UAP research.

AARO's 2024 historical record report, which leaves the contractor unnamed as "a private sector organization", says the $22 million contract covered research in 12 aerospace-technology areas and that UFO investigation "was not specifically outlined in the contract's statement of work"; the contractor nonetheless reviewed new and Blue Book cases, ran investigatory teams, proposed laboratories for recovered material and studied paranormal reports at the Utah ranch then owned by the head of the organization, with the support of the DIA program manager but without DIA seeking or authorising the work. DIA cancelled the program in 2012 "due to lack of merit and the utility of the deliverables".

The company is also at the centre of the recovered-material allegations. *The New York Times* reported in 2017 that it modified buildings in Las Vegas to store alloys said to come from UAP; Luis Elizondo testified in 2024 that an aerospace contractor's transfer of 1950s material to Bigelow Aerospace was blocked for want of a Secretary of the Air Force memo, and David Grusch and Knapp say Lockheed Martin's divestment of hull fragments was stopped by the CIA. AARO reports that the executives of the companies named to it denied on the record ever holding such material; no transfer or material has been documented.
