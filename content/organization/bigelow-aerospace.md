Bigelow Aerospace is the Las Vegas space-habitat company founded by the hotel entrepreneur Robert Bigelow in 1999. Its subsidiary BAASS (Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies) was the contractor that ran the Defense Intelligence Agency's AAWSAP research from 2008 to 2012, including investigations at Bigelow's Skinwalker Ranch.

People linked to it here include the biochemist Colm Kelleher, who worked on Bigelow's UAP research.

*Sources: AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bigelow_Aerospace)*
