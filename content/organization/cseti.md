CSETI, the Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence, is a civilian organisation founded by the physician Steven Greer in 1990 to pursue "CE-5": deliberate, human-initiated contact with extraterrestrial intelligence through meditation and field expeditions.

It is outside this archive's sourcing criteria as an evidence source; it is included for context on Greer's work and the CE-5 concept.
