CSETI, the Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence, is a civilian organisation founded by the physician Steven Greer in 1990 to pursue CE-5: deliberate, human-initiated contact with extraterrestrial intelligence through meditation and field expeditions. Greer's 1999 book *Extraterrestrial Contact* is about its work.

On Larry King's 1994 special, Greer said: “I personally have been within a few hundred feet of these craft as they have been maneuvering.” He said CSETI teams had signalled to objects and “vectored” them to within a few hundred feet. Greer later founded the Disclosure Project, which held the 2001 National Press Club witness event.

CSETI is outside this archive's sourcing criteria as an evidence source. Its contact claims rest on Greer's and its members' accounts, and no official record on file confirms them. It is included for context on Greer's work and the CE-5 concept.
