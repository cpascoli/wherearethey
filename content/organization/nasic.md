The National Air and Space Intelligence Center is the U.S. Air Force's centre for scientific and technical intelligence on foreign air, space and missile systems, headquartered at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base. It traces its heritage to 1917 and has been known as T-2 Intelligence, the Air Technical Intelligence Center (ATIC) and the Foreign Technology Division (FTD). After the Second World War it studied captured German aircraft and documents, partly under Col. Howard McCoy.

It is also the institutional home of the Air Force's UFO investigations: AARO and the CIA's 1952 records place Projects Sign, Grudge and Blue Book under ATIC. NASIC's official heritage, however, does not mention that work. David Grusch says NASIC analysed foreign intelligence about U.S. crash-retrieval programs. Claims that it plays a wider role in retrieving or exploiting non-human craft, made in some documentaries, rest on inference and anonymous accounts rather than records.

*Sources: NASIC heritage fact sheet (our copy) · AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024) · CIA memorandum on flying saucers (1952) · Capitol press conference (June 9, 2026)*
