The U.S. Department of Energy runs the country's nuclear-weapons complex and national laboratories, sites that feature in some reports of UAP near sensitive facilities. It contributed 5 records to the PURSUE releases, linked from the Index.

The main case on file is the 2015 incursion at the Pantex nuclear weapons plant in Texas, recorded in a DOE incident report. Its ground radar detected an unknown object, Protective Force officers followed it and tracked it on camera, the radar video went to Sandia National Laboratories and the evidence to the FBI. The report notes the object never appeared threatening or came close to sensitive assets.

Asked under oath in July 2023 whether the DOE is involved in UAP data collection, David Grusch said: “I cannot confirm or deny that in a public setting.” In 2024 Gen. Gregory Guillot named it among his partners on the drone-incursion problem.

*Sources: PURSUE records · Pantex Unidentified Object Incident Report (2015) · July 26, 2023 hearing transcript · SASC hearing transcript (14 March 2024)*
