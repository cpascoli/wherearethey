CEFAE (Comisión de Estudio de Fenómenos Aeroespaciales) is the Argentine Air Force's office for studying reports of aerial phenomena. It analyses sightings that come with a witness form and evidence, and publishes annual reports with its identifications. Most cases turn out to be ordinary objects misinterpreted in good faith.

Its 2018 report, directed by Commodore (ret.) Rubén Lianza, analyses 21 sightings received that year and finds a known cause for all of them. It also resolves two historical cases, including the 1995 Bariloche sighting by an Aerolíneas Argentinas crew. There CEFAE concludes that the lights were a discotheque's sky-tracer searchlight reflected on ice-crystal cloud during a city-wide blackout; the operator acknowledged in writing that he was testing it that night. CEFAE also interviewed the copilot for the first time, who said the lights looked to him from the start like reflected lights.

The report notes that only the captain, Jorge Polanco, described a solid body; the Gendarmería pilot saw a light with no structure.

*Sources: CEFAE annual report 2018*
