CEFAE (Comisión de Estudio de Fenómenos Aeroespaciales) is the Argentine Air Force's office for studying reports of aerial phenomena. It analyses sightings that come with a witness form and evidence, and publishes annual reports with its identifications. Most cases turn out to be ordinary objects misinterpreted in good faith.

*Sources: CEFAE annual report 2018*
