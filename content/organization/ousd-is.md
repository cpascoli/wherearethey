The Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security oversees the Pentagon's intelligence and security agencies. It ran the UAP Task Force with the Office of Naval Intelligence and then the AOIMSG; Under Secretary Ronald Moultrie testified at the May 2022 House hearing.

*Sources: [U.S. Navy (2020)](https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/Press-Releases/display-pressreleases/Article/2314280/) · HPSCI hearing (2022)*
