The Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security oversees the Pentagon's intelligence and security agencies. It is in the archive because it ran the Pentagon's UAP effort between the Navy's task force and AARO.

The UAP Task Force was formally established in August 2020 under the Under Secretary, with the Office of Naval Intelligence. In November 2021 its duties passed to the AOIMSG, which the office also ran, until AARO replaced it in July 2022. At the May 2022 House hearing Under Secretary Ronald Moultrie said the Secretary of Defense had assigned the work to his office “because I am responsible for looking at intelligence matters.” He defined UAP as “airborne objects that, when encountered, cannot be immediately identified”, and said he knew of no official DoD UAP study between Project Blue Book and AATIP.
