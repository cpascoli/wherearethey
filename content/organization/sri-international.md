SRI International, formerly the Stanford Research Institute, is a non-profit research institute in Menlo Park, California. It is in the archive because from 1972 its physicists Harold Puthoff and Russell Targ ran the government-funded remote-viewing experiments, with subjects such as Ingo Swann, that grew into the Stargate programme.

Puthoff says the CIA funded a first study of Swann at SRI, of $50–60K, because the Soviets were spending millions on ESP research. Targ and Puthoff described the work in their 1977 book *Mind-Reach*. Joe McMoneagle describes his first session at SRI's radio physics lab with Targ, and says he went straight from the Army unit into the labs, at SRI from about 1985 to 1988.

The results are disputed. A 1995 CIA-commissioned review divided its two experts, and the programme was ended that year as too vague to be useful.
