The UAP Science Advisory Council is a panel of scientists announced in June 2026 to bring open scientific analysis to UAP data. It is chaired by Harvard astrophysicist Avi Loeb. According to press reports, it was formed at the request of the White House in coordination with AARO, the ODNI and the FBI, and reports to an interagency UAP Governance Board.

Its members span believers and sceptics. They include Garry Nolan and Tim Gallaudet, the sceptic Michael Shermer, and data scientists from Loeb's Galileo Project, and the council works only with unclassified data. Gallaudet says it has asked for videos and data on cases such as Aguadilla. In October 2026 Loeb and fellow council members Richard Cloete, Omer Eldadi and Michael Shermer co-authored an analysis suggesting that about 8% of the Pentagon's PURSUE sensor videos may show birds. The Governance Board's own charter and membership have not been published.

*Sources: Skeptic (15 June 2026) · DefenseScoop (17 June 2026) · American Alchemy interview (Gallaudet) · Loeb on Medium (October 2026)*
