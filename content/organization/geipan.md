GEIPAN is the French space agency CNES's office for unidentified aerospace phenomena. It began in 1977 as GEPAN, was renamed SEPRA in the late 1980s, and became GEIPAN in 2005. It collects reports from the public, the gendarmerie, the Air Force and civil aviation, investigates them, and publishes its case files and classifications online.
