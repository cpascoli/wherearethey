The Defense Intelligence Agency is the U.S. military's main intelligence agency. It ran AAWSAP, the $22 million UAP study (2008–2012) contracted to Bigelow's BAASS, released its technical papers (the DIRDs) under FOIA, and circulated the U.S. defence attaché report on the 1976 Tehran incident. It also co-funded the Stargate remote-viewing research.

People linked to it here include AAWSAP's initiator James Lacatski, the former director Vice Adm. Thomas R. Wilson and the former UAP Task Force director Jay Stratton.

*Sources: AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024) · DIA FOIA release (2018)*
