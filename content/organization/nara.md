The National Archives and Records Administration preserves the U.S. government's permanent records. Sections 1841–1843 of the FY2024 National Defense Authorization Act (Public Law 118-31) required federal agencies to transfer digital copies of their UAP records to NARA, which holds them as Record Group 615, the Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Records Collection, with a presumption of disclosure. The provisions were what survived of the Schumer–Rounds UAP Disclosure Act after the House removed its independent review board and its eminent-domain power, so the collection depends on what each agency identifies and transfers as publicly releasable.

NARA released the first records on 24 April 2025, from ODNI, the Office of the Secretary of Defense, the FAA and the Nuclear Regulatory Commission. By September 2026 the collection had nine series, the NSA, the State Department, the FBI, the Air Force and Customs and Border Protection having followed, and NARA says it will keep adding records as agencies transfer them. Everything online can be downloaded in bulk as zip files with JSON metadata, updated at least three times a year; the first four series together came to under 200 MB. The same pages gather NARA's much larger older UFO holdings: the Project Blue Book case files, the Air Force OSI investigation records of 1948–1968, the "Roswell Reports" source files, and UFO files from the Carter, Ford and Clinton presidential libraries.

Record Group 615 is distinct from the Department of War's PURSUE releases on war.gov, which this archive mirrors record by record; several of the same agencies appear in both.
