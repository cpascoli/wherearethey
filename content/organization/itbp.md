The Indo-Tibetan Border Police is the Indian paramilitary force that guards the border with China. It is in the archive because its personnel reported luminous objects over the Ladakh border region in 2012, filed here as the Ladakh sightings.

According to press reports, an ITBP unit at Thakung, near Pangong Tso, logged more than 100 sightings of “Unidentified Luminous Objects” between August and October 2012, and its reports went to ITBP headquarters and the Prime Minister's Office. The Army then brought in radar and astronomers.

None of the ITBP's own reports has been released, and the explanation is disputed: India's defence minister said there was “no conclusive proof” of the sightings, and astronomers reportedly identified the lights as Jupiter and Venus.

*Sources: Ladakh incident record · UPI (2013)*
