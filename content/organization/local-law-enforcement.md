This entry groups records from local police and sheriff's departments in the PURSUE releases: 8 records so far, all in Release 06 of September 2026. Police officers are often the first official witnesses of sightings near their communities.

The eight records come in pairs from Colorado, in October 2023 and January 2024: an officer's phone video of flashing lights, and a transcript of it. AARO removed the audio and redacted the transcripts to protect the reporters' identity. In one video an officer describes the light as “silent” with “red, blue, and green flashing lights”.

PURSUE files them as unresolved reports. They record what the officers saw, not what the lights were.

*Sources: PURSUE records · LLE-UAP-PR001 and PR004*
