CEFAA (Comité de Estudios de Fenómenos Aéreos Anómalos) is Chile's official committee for studying anomalous aerial phenomena, created in 1997 within the country's civil aviation authority, the DGAC. It collects and analyses reports from pilots, air-traffic controllers and the public, and is one of the longest-running government UAP offices in the world.

*Sources: [DGAC / SEFAA](https://sefaa.dgac.gob.cl/historia/)*
