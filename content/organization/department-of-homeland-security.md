The U.S. Department of Homeland Security appears in this archive through KONA BLUE, a special access programme proposed to DHS around 2011 by people involved in the DIA's AAWSAP/AATIP work. Hal Puthoff describes it as the group's next step after AAWSAP and AATIP. Interviewees told AARO it was a sensitive DHS compartment created to hide the retrieval and exploitation of “non-human biologics”.

AARO's 2024 historical review found it was only a proposal. It “was never approved by DHS and its supporters never provided empirical evidence to support their claims”, and no data or material was transferred to DHS. In 2024 Gen. Gregory Guillot also named DHS among his partners on drone incursions over U.S. bases.

*Sources: AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024) · Joe Rogan Experience #2314: Hal Puthoff · SASC hearing transcript (14 March 2024)*
