The House Intelligence Committee's Subcommittee on Counterterrorism, Counterintelligence, and Counterproliferation, chaired at the time by Rep. André Carson, held the open hearing on UAP on 17 May 2022, the first public congressional hearing on the subject in over fifty years, with Under Secretary Ronald Moultrie and Deputy Director of Naval Intelligence Scott Bray.
