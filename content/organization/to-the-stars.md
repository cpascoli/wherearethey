To The Stars Academy of Arts & Science, now To The Stars Inc., is a San Diego company founded in 2017 by the musician Tom DeLonge, the physicist Harold Puthoff and the former CIA officer Jim Semivan. It employed Luis Elizondo and Chris Mellon and helped bring the Navy UAP videos and the AATIP story to public attention in December 2017.

Puthoff says it later arranged, through a U.S. Army contract and AARO, for a bismuth–magnesium metal sample to be analysed at Oak Ridge National Laboratory.
