The Executive Office of the President. It is in the archive because on 19 February 2026 President Trump directed the Secretary of War and other agencies to identify and release government files on UAP, UFOs and extraterrestrial life, the directive behind the PURSUE releases.

Two PURSUE records so far come from the Executive Office, both from the 1963 files of the National Aeronautics and Space Council: its inquiry into a Brazilian radio report of a crashed sphere at Conde, Bahia, which the U.S. consul reported was “apparently fabricated in Rio”, and a memorandum on recovering re-entering space debris on foreign soil. According to Skeptic, the UAP Science Advisory Council chaired by Avi Loeb was formed in 2026 at the White House's request.

David Grusch says it was “back-channeled to me unofficially” that the White House told the Inspector General to stop investigating White House special access programs; no record on file confirms it.
