The Executive Office of the President. On 19 February 2026 President Trump directed the Secretary of War and other agencies to identify and release government files on UAP, UFOs and extraterrestrial life, the directive behind the PURSUE releases. One PURSUE record so far comes from the Executive Office.

*Sources: [war.gov/UFO](https://www.war.gov/UFO/) · PURSUE records*
