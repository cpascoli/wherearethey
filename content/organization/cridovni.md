CRIDOVNI (Comisión Receptora e Investigadora de Denuncias de Objetos Voladores No Identificados) is the Uruguayan Air Force's commission for receiving and investigating reports of unidentified flying objects. The Air Force dates it to 7 August 1979, which makes it one of the oldest official UFO offices still working. The Air Force's organisational decree places it among the bodies that report directly to the Commander-in-Chief. Its public office was in central Montevideo.

Most of what it publishes is explanations. Its communiqués have identified a UFO on a live broadcast as an Austral airliner (2018) and lights over the country as Starlink satellites (2020, 2023). It also declared viral 2021 videos of a UFO and U.S. aircraft in Maldonado unreal. Its members have said publicly that a few per cent of cases stay unexplained, among them two in which Air Force aircraft tried and failed to intercept luminous spheres. Those are their own statements; no case file has been published.

Col. (ret.) Ariel Sánchez, its president in 2023, described it at the 2013 Citizen Hearing. In 2026 the Air Force announced workshops with the U.S. AARO through CRIDOVNI and said the commission was being reorganised.
