Tom Cooper is an aviation historian and author on Middle Eastern air forces, including Iran's F-14 units. Through interviews with Iranian aircrew he has reported a 2004 Iranian air-defence task force set up to protect nuclear sites against what Iran believed were CIA spy drones, and encounters with "luminous objects" near the Arak heavy-water plant; no official Iranian documents are available.

*Sources: [The Aviationist (2013)](https://theaviationist.com/2013/11/10/f-14-vs-ufo/)*
