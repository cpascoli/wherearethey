Knapp says Gaffney was angry that Lockheed still held material it was supposed to have surrendered. Knapp named him to Congress in September 2025 as a gatekeeper of the legacy program, and Dylan Borland has echoed it. Michael Shellenberger says he has not been able to verify the claim.

The allegation rests on second-hand accounts. No document supporting it has been made public, and AARO's 2024 report found no evidence of any such program. The report adds that the former CIA official its interviewees named, whom it does not identify, signed a memorandum stating he had no knowledge of the allegation and denied stopping any transfer.
