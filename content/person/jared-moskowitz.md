Jared Moskowitz is a Democratic U.S. Representative from Florida and a member of the House Oversight Committee. At the July 2023 UAP hearing, given time by ranking member Robert Garcia for an opening statement, he called on military leaders to let Congress visit Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, the Dugway Proving Ground or Groom Lake (Area 51) "if there's nothing to conceal". In his opening statement at the November 2024 hearing he cited the stealth helicopter used in the 2011 Osama bin Laden raid, which he said came out of Area 51, as an example of advanced technology kept secret.

*Sources: House Oversight hearings (26 July 2023, 13 November 2024)*
