Investigative journalist at KLAS-TV in Las Vegas and a Peabody Award winner. He broke the Bob Lazar story in 1989, co-wrote *Hunt for the Skinwalker* and *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon*, and testified at the September 9, 2025 hearing. He is a reporter rather than a primary witness.
