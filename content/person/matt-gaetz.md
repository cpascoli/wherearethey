U.S. Representative for Florida's 1st district (R) from 2017 to 2024, whose district includes Eglin Air Force Base, and a member of the Armed Services and Judiciary committees.

At the July 2023 House Oversight hearing, waived on to the subcommittee, he described a briefing he, Tim Burchett and Anna Paulina Luna obtained on an Eglin UAP report after initially being refused access to the images and the flight crew. He urged a classified session with David Grusch and a subpoena for the Eglin radar data and image if necessary. AARO later published a resolution of an apparently matching Eglin case.
