Raja Krishnamoorthi is a Democratic U.S. Representative from Illinois and a member of the House Committee on Oversight. He is in the archive as one of the Democratic members of its Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets, set up in February 2025 under Rep. Anna Paulina Luna, whose remit includes UAP alongside the JFK, RFK and MLK records.

The printed record of the task force's UAP hearing of 9 September 2025 lists him on the Democratic side, with Jasmine Crockett as ranking member, Summer Lee and Dave Min. He is not among the members recorded as present that day, and the archive holds no statement by him on UAP.

*Sources: House Oversight Committee · Hearing transcript, 9 September 2025*
