S. C. Pierman was a Capital Airlines captain and one of the airline pilots who reported lights during the Washington, D.C. sightings of July 1952. He is in the archive as a pilot witness to that case.

On 19 July 1952, according to the incident record, controller Edward Nugent at Washington National Airport picked up seven unidentified targets south-southwest of the city, and Pierman, in the air nearby, saw fast-moving white lights over about 14 minutes. His account is known here from a secondary source (Wikipedia), not from a statement of his own on file.

On 29 July Maj. Gen. John Samford told a Pentagon press conference that the radar returns were due to temperature inversions and the visual sightings to stars and meteors. The episode led the CIA to convene the Robertson Panel in January 1953.

*Sources: Washington 1952 incident record*
