Alexandro Wiggins is a U.S. Navy senior chief petty officer who testified at the House hearing "Restoring Public Trust Through UAP Transparency and Whistleblower Protection" on 9 September 2025, describing UAP encounters observed by Navy personnel. His testimony is filed with the hearing.
