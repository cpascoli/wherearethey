Alexandro Wiggins is a U.S. Navy senior chief petty officer who testified at the House hearing "Restoring Public Trust Through UAP Transparency and Whistleblower Protection" on 9 September 2025, describing the encounter he witnessed aboard USS Jackson off southern California on 15 February 2023, when four tic-tac-shaped objects were tracked on radar and infrared before departing together at high speed. Rep. Eric Burlison called him the first currently serving witness to come forward.

*Sources: House Oversight hearing (9 September 2025)*
