Brandon Fugal is a Utah commercial real-estate executive who acquired Skinwalker Ranch in 2016 through Adamantium Real Estate, revealing his ownership in 2020. He has since opened the ranch to scientific-style investigation filmed for the History Channel series *The Secret of Skinwalker Ranch*.
