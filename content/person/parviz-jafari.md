Parviz Jafari was an Imperial Iranian Air Force major and F-4 Phantom squadron commander who later became a general. In the early hours of 19 September 1976, after Tehran residents reported a brilliant object in the sky, he flew the second of two F-4s scrambled from Shahrokhi Air Base to intercept it. He is one of the very few fighter pilots ordered to fire on a UAP who has described it publicly.

At the 2007 National Press Club panel he said he locked the object on radar at 25 miles, with a return as big as a 707 tanker, and watched it flash red, green, orange and blue. When a smaller object detached and came towards him, he tried to fire a heat-seeking missile, but his weapons panel and radio failed. Another object appeared to land, lighting up the ground. The first F-4 had also lost its instruments and communications as it closed in.

The event is unusually well documented. A U.S. defense attaché report went to the DIA and was distributed to the Secretary of State, the CIA, the NSA and the White House, and a DIA evaluation form attached to it calls it a classic for the study of the phenomenon; the cable itself names no aircrew. Sceptic Philip Klass attributed the light to Jupiter and the failures to faulty equipment.
