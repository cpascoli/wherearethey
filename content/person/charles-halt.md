U.S. Air Force lieutenant colonel and deputy base commander at RAF Bentwaters/Woodbridge, later a colonel. His January 13, 1981 memo to the UK Ministry of Defence, “Unexplained Lights”, is the key official document of the Rendlesham Forest incident. He has since said that what he saw was not of this world and that it was covered up.
