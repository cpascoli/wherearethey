Clifford Stone was a retired U.S. Army sergeant first class who spent years filing Freedom of Information Act requests for military UFO records. His book *UFOs Are Real* (1997) presents government documents he obtained in this way, with his commentary.

At the Disclosure Project's press conference at the National Press Club on 9 May 2001 he claimed he had taken part in recoveries of crashed craft and debris, some involving bodies and some live occupants, while the public was told there was nothing to it. He repeated the claim in *Eyes Only* (2011), his account of his Army career.

The claims are his alone. No official record, document or other named witness in the archive corroborates them, and AARO's 2024 historical review found no evidence of crash-retrieval programmes.

*Sources: Disclosure Project press conference (2001) · UFOs Are Real (1997) · Eyes Only (2011) · AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024)*
