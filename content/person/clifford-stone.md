Clifford Stone was a retired U.S. Army sergeant first class. At the Disclosure Project's 2001 National Press Club event he claimed he had taken part in recoveries of crashed craft and debris, some involving bodies and some live occupants, while the public was told there was nothing to it. No official record supports his account.
