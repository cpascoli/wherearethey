bio: "Brig. Gen. Carroll H. Bolender was the U.S. Air Force's Deputy Director of Development in 1969, when he wrote the internal memo of 20 October 1969 recommending the end of Project Blue Book. The memo is most often cited for its statement that UFO reports which could affect national security “are not part of the Blue Book system.”"
---

Drawing on the University of Colorado (Condon) study and the National Academy of Sciences review, the memo concludes that continuing the project “cannot be justified, either on the ground of national security or in the interest of science.”

Its most cited sentence reads in full: “reports of unidentified flying objects which could affect national security are made in accordance with JANAP 146 or Air Force Manual 55-11, and are not part of the Blue Book system.” Researchers, Stanton Friedman among them at the 2013 Citizen Hearing, cite it as evidence that Blue Book was never the whole story; sceptics read it as routine procedure.

The memo was released under FOIA to the researcher Robert Todd in 1979. The archive holds nothing else by Bolender.
