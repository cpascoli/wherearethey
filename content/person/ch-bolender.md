Brig. Gen. Carroll H. Bolender was the U.S. Air Force's Deputy Director of Development in 1969, when he wrote the memorandum recommending the end of Project Blue Book. The Bolender memo is often cited because it notes that reports of UFOs that could affect national security would continue to be handled through standard Air Force procedures outside Blue Book.
