Anna Brady-Estevez is associated with American Deep Tech and the U.S. National Science Foundation. She spoke on the science and innovation panel, with Michael Gold, at the congressional briefing "Understanding UAP: Science, National Security & Innovation" on 1 May 2025. Hosted by the UAP Disclosure Fund and moderated by Luis Elizondo, it was a briefing for members of Congress, not a formal hearing, and there was no sworn testimony.

She said that while at the National Science Foundation she funded companies working on what entrepreneurs called “UAP adjacent or UAP inspired technologies”. The archive holds no other statement by her.
