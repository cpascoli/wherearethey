Retired DIA intelligence officer and rocket scientist who conceived and managed AAWSAP (2008–2012), the $22 million program contracted to Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies (BAASS).

With Colm Kelleher and George Knapp he co-wrote *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon* (2021) and *Inside the US Government Covert UFO Program* (2023). In the second book he states that the U.S. had a craft and gained access to its interior. The Pentagon's DOPSR cleared that manuscript in April 2023 (case 22-SB-0151, with no endorsement of its accuracy); *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon* also went through DOPSR review. The AAWSAP contract documents are now public through PURSUE.
