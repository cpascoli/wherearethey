Justice's Lockheed career comes from To The Stars Academy's 2020 filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission: Director for Advanced Programs at Lockheed Martin Aeronautics from 2012 to 2016, then Director for Integrated Systems at the Skunk Works until he retired and joined To The Stars in September 2017. Hal Puthoff named him among the company's principals in 2018, and David Fravor's 2023 written statement to Congress names him among the people Luis Elizondo joined there.

He is the most direct link on record between Lockheed's Skunk Works and the private UAP effort that followed AATIP. The allegations that Lockheed held recovered material (see Lockheed Martin and AAWSAP) come from others; no document or statement on file has him speak to them.

*Sources: To The Stars Academy SEC filing (preliminary offering circular, 1 September 2020) · Hal Puthoff's 2018 presentation · David Fravor's written statement, July 2023*
