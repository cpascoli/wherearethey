Donald Schmitt is an American Roswell researcher from Wisconsin, formerly with the J. Allen Hynek Center for UFO Studies, who has co-written several books on the 1947 case, first with Kevin Randle (*UFO Crash at Roswell*, 1991) and later with Tom Carey (*Witness to Roswell*). He says they have interviewed more than 600 people connected with the case, and he has led archaeological digs at the debris field.

His credibility was damaged in 1995, when a reporter found he had falsely claimed a law-enforcement background and doctoral studies; Schmitt admitted making false statements, and Randle ended their partnership. At the 2013 Citizen Hearing he testified for the Roswell panels on witness accounts, the Air Force's changing explanations and the retired Gen. Arthur Exon. His accounts relay what others told him, mostly decades later.
