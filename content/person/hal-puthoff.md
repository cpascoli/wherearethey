American physicist (PhD, Stanford) known for laser physics and theoretical work on zero-point (vacuum) energy. In the 1970s he and Russell Targ began the remote-viewing research at SRI International that grew into the government’s Stargate program.

He later directed EarthTech International and the Institute for Advanced Studies at Austin, was a senior advisor connected to the DIA’s AAWSAP research, and co-founded To The Stars Academy in 2017.
