Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence in the Clinton and George W. Bush administrations, and earlier Minority Staff Director of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence.

He helped bring the Navy UAP videos to The New York Times in 2017 and has since written and lobbied on UAP transparency and airspace-awareness gaps. At the May 2025 briefing he described drone incursions over sensitive U.S. sites.
