Russell Yokum was a sergeant with the St. Helens Police Department in Oregon. Early on 17 March 1981 he reported a bright light over the Columbia River, which he and two Oregon State Police officers watched for about 40 minutes while the officers taped their radio exchange with a CB operator across the river. The recording was later analysed by the Center for UFO Studies.

Some later accounts spell his name Yoakum.

*Sources: Greg Long, “Columbia River UFO 3/17/1981” (Pacific Harbor Publishing, 1996), via [sacred-texts.com](https://www.sacred-texts.com/ufo/columbia.htm)*
