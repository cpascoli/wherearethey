Russell ("Rusty") Yokum was a sergeant with the St. Helens Police Department in Oregon. Early on 17 March 1981 he reported a bright light over the Columbia River, which he and two Oregon State Police officers watched while the officers taped their radio exchange with a CB operator across the river. The recording was analysed by the Center for UFO Studies; in Greg Long's account, investigators who interviewed him recorded that he described the light as light orange. In 1981 he gave the story to the National Enquirer.

Some later accounts spell his name Yoakum.

*Sources: National Enquirer (Joe Mullins), 1981 · Greg Long, "Tales from the Secret Study" (Pacific Harbor Publishing, 1996) · [the tape, on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ELD-qcSdsg4)*
