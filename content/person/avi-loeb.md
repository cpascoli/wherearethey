Harvard astrophysicist who chaired Harvard’s astronomy department (2011–2020) and in 2021 founded the Galileo Project, which builds open scientific instruments to search for technological objects in the sky. In 2023 he led an expedition that recovered spherules from the IM1 interstellar meteor site off Papua New Guinea.
