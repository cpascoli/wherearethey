Summer Lee is a Democratic U.S. Representative from Pennsylvania and one of the Democratic members of the House Oversight Committee's Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets, chaired by Rep. Anna Paulina Luna, whose remit includes UAP.

She took part in the task force's UAP hearing of 9 September 2025. She urged members not to "get distracted by sensational stories" and to focus on protecting whistleblowers, "not only the ones who are reporting on UAP." She used her time to question the minority witness, Joe Spielberger of the Project on Government Oversight, about retaliation against whistleblowers and the independence of inspectors general, and entered into the record a New York Times article, “DOGE put critical Social Security data at risk, whistleblower says.” In her five minutes she put no questions to the UAP witnesses.

*Sources: House Oversight Committee · Hearing transcript, 9 September 2025*
