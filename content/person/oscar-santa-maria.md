Oscar Santa María Huertas is a retired commander in the Peruvian Air Force. He says that on 11 April 1980, flying a Sukhoi Su-22, he was ordered to intercept a balloon-like object over La Joya Air Base, fired cannon rounds at it with no visible effect, and watched it outmanoeuvre his jet at high altitude. He told the story at the National Press Club in 2007 and the Citizen Hearing in 2013.

*Sources: National Press Club (2007) · Citizen Hearing (2013)*
