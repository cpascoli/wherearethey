Apollo 14 lunar module pilot (1971) and the sixth person to walk on the Moon. He founded the Institute of Noetic Sciences in 1973. Having grown up near Roswell, he said for decades that the 1947 crash was real and that Earth has been visited. The Wilson memo was reportedly found among his papers after his death in 2016.
