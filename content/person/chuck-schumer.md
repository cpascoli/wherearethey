Chuck Schumer, a Democrat, has represented New York in the U.S. Senate since 1999 and was Senate Majority Leader from 2021 to 2025. In 2023 he and Republican Senator Mike Rounds introduced the UAP Disclosure Act, modelled on the JFK records law; a scaled-back version became part of the FY2024 NDAA, creating the UAP Records Collection at the National Archives.
