Jerome Nelson was a U.S. Air Force Atlas F missile crew officer with the 579th Strategic Missile Squadron at Walker Air Force Base, New Mexico, from 1962 to 1965. At the 2010 National Press Club press conference he described guards' reports of a lit object hovering over his silo. Confined to the launch control center, he did not see it himself.
