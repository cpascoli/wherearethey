Opening that hearing, she asked whether UAP are tied to foreign adversaries such as China or Russia “leveraging advanced technologies beyond our current capabilities”, or are unknown scientific phenomena, and called for all agencies to work together to aggregate data. In questioning she asked whether AARO worked with universities; Kosloski said its university partnerships were “lacking” because data must first be declassified. She also asked how AARO balances public demand for transparency against the risk of revealing classified information.

Her statements in the archive are questions, not claims about what UAP are.

*Sources: Senate hearing record (19 November 2024)*
