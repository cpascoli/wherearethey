Larry Warren was a U.S. Air Force security specialist at RAF Bentwaters in 1980. He is in the archive as one of the Rendlesham witnesses, and the one whose account is most disputed.

At the Disclosure Project press conference of 9 May 2001 he said he guarded nuclear weapons at Bentwaters/Woodbridge and took part in part of a three-night UFO event in December 1980, in which objects were seen on the ground and another life form was seen. In *Left at East Gate* (1997), written with the investigator Peter Robbins, he says he was taken by truck into the forest to join other personnel at a landed object, and that the witnesses were afterwards warned to tell no one.

His account differs from those of other base personnel, and some of them dispute it. Lt. Col. Charles Halt denies Warren was with him in the forest, and Robbins later disowned the account.

*Sources: his own testimony at the Disclosure Project press conference, May 9, 2001 · Left at East Gate (1997) · [David Clarke](https://drclarke.substack.com/p/dropped-object)*
