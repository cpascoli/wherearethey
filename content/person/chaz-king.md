Chaz King served in U.S. Air Force Security Forces at Vandenberg Air Force Base, California. He was on duty during the October 2003 incursions over the base's missile-defence sites, known as the Vandenberg "Red Square" incident, and described them in the 2025 documentary *The Age of Disclosure*, alongside Jeffrey Nuccetelli, who testified about the same events before Congress.
