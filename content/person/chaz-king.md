Chaz King served in U.S. Air Force Security Forces at Vandenberg Air Force Base, California. With Jeffrey Nuccetelli and Boeing contractors he reported the 2003 incursions of a huge glowing geometric craft over missile-defence sites, known as the Vandenberg "Red Square" incident.

*Sources: House Oversight hearing (9 September 2025)*
