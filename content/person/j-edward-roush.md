J. Edward Roush, a Democratic U.S. Representative from Indiana, chaired the House Committee on Science and Astronautics' symposium on UFOs on 29 July 1968, which heard from scientists including J. Allen Hynek, James E. McDonald and Carl Sagan.

*Sources: 1968 House symposium record*
