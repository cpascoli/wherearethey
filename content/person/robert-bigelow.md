Real-estate entrepreneur (Budget Suites of America) who founded Bigelow Aerospace in 1999, which built the BEAM expandable module attached to the ISS in 2016. He funded the National Institute for Discovery Science (1995), owned Skinwalker Ranch (1996–2016), and his BAASS subsidiary was the AAWSAP contractor.
