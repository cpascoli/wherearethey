Chad Underwood is a former U.S. Navy F/A-18 pilot with Strike Fighter Squadron 41 (VFA-41). During the November 2004 USS Nimitz encounter he recorded the infrared "FLIR1" video of the "Tic Tac" object, a term he coined, which was published in 2017 and officially released by the Pentagon in 2020.
