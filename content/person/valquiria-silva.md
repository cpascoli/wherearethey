On 20 January 1996, aged 14, she was one of the three young women who said they saw a creature on a vacant lot in Jardim Andere, Varginha, with her sister Liliane and their friend Kátia Xavier.

She is a civilian witness, filed outside the archive's criteria; her account is the core of the case, and the Brazilian Army's 1997 inquiry judged that what the three women saw was most likely a local man crouched in the rain.

*Sources: Fantástico (Globo, 4 February 1996) · National Press Club press conference (NewsNation, 20 January 2026)*
