Igor Maltsev was a colonel general of aviation and, in 1990, chief of the Main Staff of the Soviet Air Defence Forces. After air-defence radars and an interceptor were alerted to lights near Pereslavl-Zalessky on 21 March 1990, he gave the unit reports to the newspaper *Rabochaya Tribuna*. This was an unusually senior public acknowledgment of a UFO alert by the Soviet military.

His published assessment described a large, silent, highly manoeuvrable disc, but he presented it as his own reading of the eyewitness data rather than a conclusion.
