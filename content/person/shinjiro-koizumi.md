bio: "Shinjiro Koizumi is a member of Japan's House of Representatives and a former Minister of the Environment. He became secretary-general of the cross-party Diet league for UAP clarification when it was founded on 6 June 2024, according to the South China Morning Post; the league is chaired by the former Defense Minister Yasukazu Hamada."
---

Shigeru Ishiba was an adviser to the league, which has grown to about 100 members. Its founding statement calls UAP a potential national-security threat, possibly unknown-technology weapons or spy drones, and urges the government to create an office like the Pentagon's AARO and share data with the United States. The archive holds no statement by Koizumi himself on the subject.
