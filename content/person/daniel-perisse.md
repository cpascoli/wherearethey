Daniel Alberto Perissé was an Argentine Navy officer, a lieutenant (teniente de fragata) of the marines, who commanded the naval detachment on Deception Island, Antarctica, during the sightings of June and July 1965. His radio reports were the basis of the Argentine Navy's official communiqués of 6 and 7 July 1965, and he was quoted worldwide saying the object was neither a balloon, a star nor an aircraft.

He had an interest in UFOs before the events and the Naval Intelligence Service's UFO questionnaires on the island's sightings appear to be in his handwriting. He later rose to capitán de fragata and kept writing about the cases: a synopsis in 1979, a table for a 1983 congress in Posadas, a paper for the 1987 MUFON symposium in Washington, and a posthumous book chapter. A 2012 re-investigation that used his records concluded that the lights of 3 July were most probably a meteor, the Echo II satellite and Mars or bright stars.

*Sources: Argentine Navy bulletin no. 172 (1965) and Perissé's later papers, as transcribed in FOTOCAT Report no. 3 (2012)*
