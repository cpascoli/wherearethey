Koos de Klerk was a South African Police constable. On patrol with Constable John Locken on the Pretoria–Bronkhorstspruit highway just after midnight on 16 September 1965, he and Locken said their van's headlights lit a domed, copper-coloured disc standing on the road, which lifted off within seconds, trailing flames.

The account is known from the 1965 wire reports, as summarised by NICAP; a British paper reported the next day that police and scientists were investigating. No police record of the inquiry or its outcome has been found.

*Sources: South Wales Echo, 17 September 1965 · NICAP, UFOs: A New Look (1969), citing Associated Press, 17 September 1965*
