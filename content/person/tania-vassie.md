Tania Vassie was a student at Westall High School in Melbourne in April 1966, when more than 200 students and teachers say they saw a silver object descend near the school grounds. She is in the archive because others place her closest to where it came down. As a private individual her account is outside this archive's core sourcing criteria, and no statement of her own is on file.

In the documentary *Westall '66*, a former student says she and another girl went over the fence behind Vassie, "Tania was in the lead", and found "a great big round patch" of flattened grass where the object had been. Asked about Vassie, she says she was "given to believe" Tania saw it on the ground, that Tania came back "all to pieces", was taken away in an ambulance and never came back to school.

That is a classmate's recollection, not confirmed by any record. The researcher Keith Basterfield has proposed a stray HIBAL balloon as the object; the match is unconfirmed.
