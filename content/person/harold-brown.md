Harold Brown (1927–2019) was a physicist who served as Secretary of the Air Force from 1965 to 1969 and later as Secretary of Defense (1977–1981). He testified at the House Armed Services Committee hearing on UFOs on 5 April 1966, the first open congressional hearing on the subject, with Blue Book chief Maj. Hector Quintanilla and the consultant J. Allen Hynek.

He told the committee that 18 years of investigation had not identified any threat to national security, or any evidence of extraterrestrial vehicles, and that the Air Force would continue to investigate with an open mind. Asked afterwards what the Air Force was hiding, he said: “We have not been hiding anything… The hearing this morning was public for just that reason. In those cases where, for lack of data or lack of a convincing hypothesis, the sighting has been kept in the unidentified category, we've been perfectly willing to say that, too.” CBS Reports broadcast that statement in May 1966.
