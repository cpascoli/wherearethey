Harold Brown (1927–2019) was a physicist who served as Secretary of the Air Force from 1965 to 1969 and later as Secretary of Defense (1977–1981). He testified at the House Armed Services Committee hearing on UFOs on 5 April 1966.

*Sources: House Armed Services hearing (1966)*
