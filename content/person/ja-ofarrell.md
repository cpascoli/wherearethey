Lt J. A. O'Farrell was a Royal Australian Navy pilot flying Hawker Sea Furies from the RAN air station at Nowra, New South Wales. He is in the archive as the witness of the 1954 Nowra Sea Fury incident, which the National Archives of Australia describes from its own records.

On 31 August 1954, returning to Nowra on a night flight, he noticed first one, then a second, bright light flying near his aircraft. He described each as “a vague shape with the white light situated centrally on top”. As they kept pace with him he radioed Nowra, which confirmed that the objects were registering on radar; they then left to the north-east at tremendous speed.

According to the archive, a shaken O'Farrell made a detailed report on returning to base, which survives with the radar operator's confirmation. Its 2018 article says the nature of the objects “still remains a mystery”; no identification is on file.

*Sources: National Archives of Australia (2018)*
