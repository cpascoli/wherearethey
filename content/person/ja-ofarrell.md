Lt J. A. O'Farrell was a Royal Australian Navy pilot at the naval air station at Nowra, New South Wales. On 31 August 1954 he reported two lights that paced his Sea Fury and were confirmed on Nowra's radar, one of the best-known Australian military sightings. His report and the radar operator's confirmation survive in the national archives.

*Sources: National Archives of Australia (2018)*
