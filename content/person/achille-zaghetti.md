Achille Zaghetti, from Grosseto in Tuscany, was the Alitalia captain who on 21 April 1991, commanding an MD-80 from Milan to Heathrow, reported a light-brown, missile-like object passing close to his airliner over Kent; his co-pilot saw it too, and London control reported an unknown radar target behind the aircraft. His handwritten report, the CAA's inquiry and the MoD's papers are in the files opened at The National Archives in 2008.

He was named in the press at the time and spoke about the encounter again in 2008, comparing the object to a fighter's long-range fuel tank. No recorded interview with him has been found.

*Sources: MoD file DEFE 24/1953 (his report; The Independent, 6 May 1991) · The Telegraph via ANI, 22 October 2008*
