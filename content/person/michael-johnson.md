Michael Johnson was a U.S. Air Force security policeman at Ellsworth Air Force Base who, with Mario Woods, answered an alarm at the N-5 Minuteman silo in November 1977. Both men describe seeing a huge craft over the site and hours of missing time; the Air Force then separated the two men and one was discharged. They reunited for interviews and a joint hypnotic regression in 2025–2026.

*Sources: Interviews with Jesse Michels (2025–2026)*
