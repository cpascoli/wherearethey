John Kilburn was a Royal Air Force flight lieutenant with a Shackleton crew at RAF Topcliffe in North Yorkshire in 1952. He is the named witness of the Topcliffe sighting during NATO's Exercise Mainbrace, the alliance's first big naval exercise in the North Atlantic.

On 19 September 1952 he and other RAF personnel at Topcliffe said they saw a silvery disc follow a Meteor jet on its approach, swaying like a pendulum, then hover and speed away; personnel at nearby RAF Dishforth reported something similar. The account is known through David Clarke's history of the incident.

According to Edward Ruppelt of Project Blue Book, the Mainbrace sightings led the RAF to take UFOs seriously, and the UK Air Ministry began investigating reports on a permanent basis in 1953. No explanation was published.
