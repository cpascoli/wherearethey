John Kilburn was a Royal Air Force flight lieutenant with a Shackleton crew at RAF Topcliffe in 1952. On 19 September 1952, during NATO's Exercise Mainbrace, he and other RAF personnel saw a silvery disc follow a Meteor jet on its approach, swaying like a pendulum before hovering and speeding away.

*Sources: Mainbrace incident record*
