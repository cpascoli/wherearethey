Boris Aleksandrovich Sokolov is a retired Soviet colonel and candidate of technical sciences. From 1978 to 1989 he coordinated research on anomalous phenomena for the Ministry of Defence and the USSR Academy of Sciences, the programme known as Setka. He investigated the October 1982 incident at a Strategic Rocket Forces division in Ukraine.

He gave his account to George Knapp in the early 1990s. Knapp relays it as UFOs triggering the missile launch system. In his own 2000 paper with Yuli Platov, however, Sokolov explained the lights as flares and called the panel fault a chance coincidence.

*Sources: Platov & Sokolov, Vestnik RAN 70(6), 2000; George Knapp, written testimony, 9 September 2025*
