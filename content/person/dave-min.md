Dave Min is a Democratic U.S. Representative from California. He is listed as a Democratic member of the House Oversight Committee's Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets in the record of its 9 September 2025 UAP hearing, alongside Raja Krishnamoorthi and Summer Lee, with Jasmine Crockett as the Democratic counterpart.

The task force was set up on 11 February 2025, with Rep. Anna Paulina Luna as chair; its declassification mandate covers UAP among other subjects. The archive holds no statement by Min on UAP.
