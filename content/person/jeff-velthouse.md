Jeff Velthouse was the Holland, Michigan, police officer (badge 73) who responded to UFO calls on the night of 8 March 1994, after 911 dispatch had received a number of calls. He wrote the police report that is the main record of the sightings.

Through a resident's binoculars he saw an object high to the southwest with red, green and white lights, but could not make out a shape. He wrote that the lighting “appeared to be different than a normal aircraft” and was “oscillating, rather than blinking”, and that he saw two objects moving southwest. He asked dispatch to call the National Weather Service in Muskegon, drove to Tulip City airport but could not relocate the objects, and later that shift spoke with the meteorologist Jack Bushong about the radar returns he had tracked.

The report records what the witnesses said, not what the lights were. It was closed on 9 March 1994 with no further information.
