Jeff Velthouse was the Holland, Michigan, police officer who responded to the wave of UFO calls on 8 March 1994. His incident report records his own observation of coloured lights and his conversation with the National Weather Service meteorologist who was tracking radar returns that night.

*Sources: Holland Police Department report 940308035*
