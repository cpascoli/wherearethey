Nick Pope is a former UK Ministry of Defence civil servant who ran the ministry's UFO desk in the Secretariat (Air Staff) from 1991 to 1994, investigating public and military reports for the British government. He has since become the best-known British official voice on the subject, through books, broadcasts, the 2007 National Press Club panel and the Rendlesham Forest panel of the 2013 Citizen Hearing.

Rendlesham is his central case. He says the MoD's Defence Intelligence Staff judged the radiation readings at the landing site "significantly higher than the average background", that the U.S. commander in Europe took material from the investigation to Ramstein without telling the British, and that his cold-case review found misidentification, hoax and delusion did not fit the facts. He quotes Lord Hill-Norton's challenge that the witnesses were either telling the truth or "hallucinating or lying". He is also measured. Some intelligence files from the period were destroyed, but he says he has seen no evidence of a deliberate cover-up.

He also championed the 1990 Calvine photograph, a print of which hung in his office until a superior removed it. The MoD's UFO project, which he says was modelled on Project Blue Book, closed in the 2009 defence cuts. Sceptics attribute Rendlesham to a fireball and the Orford Ness lighthouse, and Calvine possibly to a small model close to the camera.

*Sources: Citizen Hearing on Disclosure (2013) · National Press Club (2007) · Calvine photograph record*
