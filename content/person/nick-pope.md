Nick Pope is a former UK Ministry of Defence civil servant who ran the ministry's UFO desk from 1991 to 1994. He has since written and spoken widely on UFOs, including at the National Press Club in 2007 and on the Rendlesham Forest panel of the 2013 Citizen Hearing.

*Sources: National Press Club (2007) · Citizen Hearing (2013)*
