Francisco Javier Lerdo de Tejada was the captain of Transportes Aéreos Españoles flight 297, a Supercaravelle carrying 109 passengers from Salzburg to the Canary Islands, on 11 November 1979. He is in the archive as the pilot at the centre of the Manises incident.

At about 23:00 the crew saw red lights that seemed to approach the aircraft at collision speed and then follow it. Radar stations in Madrid and Barcelona could offer no explanation, and he diverted to Valencia-Manises, an event often described as the first emergency landing of a commercial flight because of a UFO. A Spanish Air Force Mirage F1 was scrambled from Los Llanos.

The Spanish Air Force file, declassified in 1994, found no confirmed radar contact correlated with the lights. Its investigating general found “no proof to admit the presence of UFOs” and suggested a bright planet, a weather balloon or reflected lights. Later researchers proposed bright stars and refinery flare stacks; the witnesses reject these explanations.
