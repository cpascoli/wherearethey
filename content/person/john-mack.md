John Mack (1929–2004) was a professor of psychiatry at Harvard Medical School, known in his later career for his work with people who reported abduction experiences. In November 1994 he travelled to Zimbabwe to interview the Ariel School children about the sighting two months earlier; footage of those interviews is central to the documentary *Ariel Phenomenon* (2022).
