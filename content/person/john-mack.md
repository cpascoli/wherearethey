John Mack (1929–2004) was a professor of psychiatry at Harvard Medical School, known in his later career for his work with people who reported abduction experiences. His book *Abduction* (1994) presents his work with more than 100 of them, most without obvious signs of mental illness; *Passport to the Cosmos* (1999) followed. A Harvard faculty committee reviewed his clinical care and research with his subjects and took no action after a 15-month review.

In November 1994 he travelled to Zimbabwe to interview the Ariel School children about the sighting two months earlier, after the ufologist Cynthia Hind. In the opening of *Ariel Phenomenon* (2022) he says “we came away convinced it's an extraordinary event occurred here”.

Others cite him second-hand. Linda Moulton Howe says he told her “i think we are dealing with extraterrestrial biological entities in some cases”, and the lawyer Daniel Sheehan, his counsel, says material he learned of while representing Mack described beings recovered at Roswell. These are their accounts, not his.
