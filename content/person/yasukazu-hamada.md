Yasukazu Hamada is a member of Japan's House of Representatives who served twice as Minister of Defense (2008–2009 and 2022–2023). He is in the archive as chair of the cross-party Diet league for UAP clarification, founded on 6 June 2024, according to the South China Morning Post.

The league has Shinjiro Koizumi as secretary-general and Shigeru Ishiba as an adviser, and has grown to about 100 members. Its founding statement calls UAP a potential national-security threat, possibly unknown-technology weapons or spy drones, and urges Japan to create an office like the Pentagon's AARO and share data with the United States. At its launch two members, Kei Endo and Yoshiharu Asakawa, said they had personally seen UFOs. The archive holds no statement by Hamada himself on the subject.
