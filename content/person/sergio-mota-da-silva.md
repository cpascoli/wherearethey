Sérgio Mota da Silva was the Brazilian Air Force sergeant on duty as controller in the São José dos Campos tower on 19 May 1986, and gave the first alert of the "Official UFO Night". He later became a civil air traffic controller, and gave his first television interview, without showing his face, to TV Globo in 2016.

*Sources: TV Globo, Fantástico (2016) · [Government of Brazil (2022)](https://www.gov.br/en/government-of-brazil/latest-news/2022/official-ufo-night-in-brazil)*
