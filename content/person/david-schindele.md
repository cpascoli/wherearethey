David Schindele is a retired U.S. Air Force captain and former ICBM launch-control officer at Minot Air Force Base, North Dakota. At the 2013 Citizen Hearing on Disclosure, on the "Nuclear Tampering" panel, he testified that in 1966 his crew found all ten missiles of November Flight "off alert and unlaunchable" after an object had hovered over the launch control facility overnight.

*Sources: Citizen Hearing (2013)*
