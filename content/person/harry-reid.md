U.S. Senator from Nevada (1987–2017) and Senate Majority Leader (2007–2015). With Senators Daniel Inouye and Ted Stevens he secured classified funding for AAWSAP in 2008. He later publicly backed the 2017 disclosures and called for UAP transparency. He died in 2021.
