Harry Reid was a Democratic U.S. Senator from Nevada (1987–2017) and Senate Majority Leader (2007–2015), and the politician most responsible for the Pentagon's modern UAP programs.

In 2007–2008, with Senators Daniel Inouye and Ted Stevens, he directed $22 million in defense appropriations to the DIA. The money funded AAWSAP, which was contracted to Bigelow's BAASS and later carried on informally as AATIP. In 2009 he asked for the effort to be made a special access program, and the Pentagon refused on James Clapper's advice. After DIA cancelled the program in 2012, he and Senator Joseph Lieberman backed the failed KONA BLUE proposal at DHS. AARO's 2024 report documents these steps, and describes the contractor's work at Skinwalker Ranch as UAP and paranormal research outside the program's official purpose.

After The New York Times revealed the program in December 2017, he spoke publicly for transparency. In *The Phenomenon* (2020) he said that "most of it hasn't seen the light of day". David Grusch says he briefed Reid in 2021 and was told the U.S. held UFO material Reid had been denied access to for decades. Jay Stratton says Reid was his first congressional briefing, in 2007. Reid died in December 2021. His former chief of staff could not confirm Stratton's account of what he was briefed.
