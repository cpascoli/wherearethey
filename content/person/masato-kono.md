Masato Kono was one of two schoolboys in Kōfu, Japan, aged eight, who on 23 February 1975 reported a craft coming down in a vineyard near their housing estate and a small being that approached them; the case is filed as the Kōfu incident. As a private individual his account is outside this archive's core sourcing criteria.

*Sources: Kōfu incident record*
