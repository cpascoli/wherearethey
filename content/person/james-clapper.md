The film's first trailer introduces him as “the fourth Director of National Intelligence”. In a clip from it he says he spent 32 years in the Air Force, his last job “chief air force intelligence” before he became director of the DIA. During his Air Force service, he says, there was an active program to track anomalous activities that could not otherwise be explained, many of them connected with ranges out west, notably Area 51.

Christopher Mellon says that on a documentary set Clapper described UAP over one of the most sensitive test ranges when he led Air Force intelligence, alarming the people testing stealth aircraft. Mellon presents this as contradicting AARO's explanation that witnesses were seeing U.S. stealth craft. Both are recollections.
