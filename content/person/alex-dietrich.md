Alex Dietrich is a retired U.S. Navy lieutenant commander and F/A-18 pilot. On 14 November 2004 she flew the second Super Hornet in David Fravor's flight during the USS Nimitz "Tic Tac" encounter. While Fravor spiralled down towards the object, she stayed high as cover, giving a second, overhead view of the white, wingless object over a patch of disturbed water before it shot away.

She stayed in the Navy after the encounter and, by Fravor's account, was invited to the Pentagon several times to discuss it and view other UAP videos. Around 2016 she contacted Fravor, which led to Luis Elizondo reaching out to him. She went public in 2021, appearing with Fravor on CBS's *60 Minutes* ahead of the ODNI's preliminary UAP assessment, and she appears in *The Age of Disclosure* (2025).

She is notably measured. She says the object seemed to lack any flight surfaces or propulsion and "appeared to respond" to Fravor's jet. She also says it could have been a natural phenomenon or human activity, that she is not "a UFO person" or a whistleblower, and that she talks about it mainly to make it normal for pilots to report what they see. She and Fravor reject AARO's suggestion that the Tic Tac was American technology. Senate staffer Kirk McConnell says their account is what convinced him.
