Lothar Neethling was a colonel and head of the South African Police forensic laboratory in Pretoria. He is in the archive as the official who examined the evidence from the Rosmead tennis-court “landing” of 12 November 1972.

Residents of Rosmead, in the Eastern Cape, saw a bright light zig-zag across the sky and seem to descend on a school tennis court; afterwards scorch marks and holes were found in the tar, and nearby trees died. Samples went to his laboratory. Neethling said analysis found nothing unusual and suggested vandals with pick-axes had made the holes.

His finding is the only official assessment of the case on file, and it is known from a secondary source.

*Sources: Rosmead incident record*
