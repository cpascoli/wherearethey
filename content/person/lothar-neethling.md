Lothar Neethling was a colonel and head of the South African Police forensic laboratory in Pretoria. After the November 1972 Rosmead tennis-court "landing", he said analysis of the samples found nothing unusual and suggested vandals with pick-axes had made the holes.

*Sources: Rosmead incident record*
