André Carson, a Democrat from Indiana, chaired the House Intelligence subcommittee that held the open hearing on UAP on 17 May 2022, the first public congressional hearing on the subject in over fifty years. He appears in the documentary *The Age of Disclosure* (2025).

*Sources: HPSCI hearing record (2022) · The Age of Disclosure*
