U.S. Senator from New York (D) since 2009. As chair of the Senate Armed Services Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and Capabilities she led the bipartisan effort that created the Pentagon’s UAP office (AARO) and chaired its open hearings in 2023 and 2024.
