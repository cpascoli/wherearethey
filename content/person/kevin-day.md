Kevin Day was a senior chief operations specialist aboard the cruiser USS Princeton in 2004, in charge of the radar picture: "my job was to man the radars and ID everything that flew in the skies." He says the Princeton tracked anomalous objects for about two weeks before the 14 November "Tic Tac" encounter, and he directed the fighters towards them.
