Anthony Choy is a founding member of OIFAA, the Peruvian Air Force's office for investigating anomalous aerial phenomena. He is in the archive as a panellist at the National Press Club event on UFOs in November 2007 and a witness at the Citizen Hearing on Disclosure in 2013.

At the National Press Club he described the Chulucanas case in northern Peru: on 13 October 2001, he said, eight red-orange spheres were filmed before hundreds of people and stayed suspended in silence for over five hours, and the Air Force had him investigate. At the 2013 Citizen Hearing he presented the Chulucanas sightings again and said the videos went to “Julio Chamorro” Flores, head of the Air Force's UFO investigation office.

He also relayed witness accounts that in the 1980s an object fell in Peru and its remains were taken away by helicopters “definitively not part of the peruvian armed forces”; asked whose they were, he said “apparently they were american”. That account is second-hand, and no record in the archive confirms it.

*Sources: National Press Club (2007) · Citizen Hearing (2013)*
