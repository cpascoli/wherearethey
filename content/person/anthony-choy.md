Anthony Choy is a founding member of OIFAA, the Peruvian Air Force's office for investigating anomalous aerial phenomena. He spoke at the National Press Club event on UFOs in November 2007 and testified at the Citizen Hearing on Disclosure in 2013.

*Sources: National Press Club (2007) · Citizen Hearing (2013)*
