Mike Rounds, a Republican U.S. Senator from South Dakota, co-sponsored the UAP Disclosure Act with Chuck Schumer in 2023; a scaled-back version became part of the FY2024 NDAA, creating the UAP Records Collection at the National Archives. He appears in *The Age of Disclosure* (2025).
