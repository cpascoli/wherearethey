Mike Rounds is a Republican U.S. Senator from South Dakota. With Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer he introduced the UAP Disclosure Act in July 2023, as an amendment to the FY2024 National Defense Authorization Act.

Modelled on the JFK Records Act, the bill would have created a presidentially appointed UAP Records Review Board and used eminent domain to recover any "technologies of unknown origin and biological evidence of non-human intelligence" held privately. The House stripped the review board and the eminent-domain provisions. The enacted FY2024 NDAA kept a narrower requirement for agencies to collect their UAP records at the National Archives. The two senators reintroduced the full text in July 2024.

Rounds is among the on-record interviewees of the documentary *The Age of Disclosure* (2025). Hal Puthoff, on Joe Rogan's podcast, pointed to the bill's repeated use of the phrase "non-human intelligence".
