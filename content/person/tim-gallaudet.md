Retired U.S. Navy rear admiral and PhD oceanographer who commanded the Navy Meteorology and Oceanography Command, then served as Assistant Secretary of Commerce and acting NOAA Administrator (2017–2021).

He testified in November 2024 that an urgent flight-safety email about UAP during a 2015 Navy exercise, with the “GoFast” video attached, vanished from recipients’ inboxes the next day. He advocates research into “transmedium” UAP in the oceans.
