Former U.S. Navy F/A-18F pilot with Strike Fighter Squadron 11 (“Red Rippers”). He reports that his squadron saw UAP almost daily off the U.S. East Coast in 2014–2015, including a near-collision with an object described as a dark gray or black cube inside a clear sphere.

He founded Americans for Safe Aerospace to support pilot reporting and testified under oath on July 26, 2023.
