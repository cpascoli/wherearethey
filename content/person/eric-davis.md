Astrophysicist specializing in advanced propulsion and “breakthrough physics”. He worked with Hal Puthoff at EarthTech International and contributed research to AAWSAP, including the DIRD “Traversable Wormholes, Stargates, and Negative Energy”.

He is the reported author of the notes known as the Admiral Wilson memo. He has said publicly that he briefed Defense Department and congressional staff on recovered “off-world vehicles not made on this earth”, and at the May 2025 briefing he described recovered craft as “not of this Earth”.

*Sources: May 1, 2025 briefing (see video moments) · Jesse Michels interview · The New York Times (July 2020)*
