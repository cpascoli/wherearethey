Jim Lovell (1928–2025) was a U.S. Navy test pilot and NASA astronaut who flew on Gemini 7, Gemini 12 and Apollo 8, and commanded Apollo 13.

On Gemini 7 in December 1965 he described the spent booster as a brilliant body in the sun beside Frank Borman's "bogey"; the audio was released by NASA in PURSUE release 01.
