João Baptista Macuco Janini is a pathologist who says he has performed more than 50,000 autopsies over sixty years and taught at three federal universities. He examined tissue samples from the autopsy of Marco Eli Chereze, the military police officer whose 1996 death is linked by ufologists to the Varginha case.

His statement presents an extraterrestrial origin of the bacterium as a hypothesis awaiting molecular tests. Press reports of the official necropsy describe septicaemia caused by a common bacterium.
