Former U.S. Air Force captain and Minuteman missile launch officer at Malmstrom AFB, Montana. He says that on March 24, 1967, all ten missiles under his control went offline while security guards reported a glowing red object over the facility. He co-wrote *Faded Giant* (2005) and spoke at the 2010 National Press Club event on UFOs and nuclear weapons.
