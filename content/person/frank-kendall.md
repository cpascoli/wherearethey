Frank Kendall served as Secretary of the Air Force. He appears in this archive in connection with the December 2023 drone incursions over Langley Air Force Base. When Rep. Rob Wittman raised them at a House Armed Services hearing in April 2024, Kendall answered with threats to bases overseas and did not address Langley.

*Sources: House Armed Services hearing record (17 April 2024, local copy)*
