A. K. Antony was India's Defence Minister from 2006 to 2014. He is in the archive for his answer to Parliament on the luminous objects reported over the India–China border in Ladakh in 2012.

According to UPI, an Indo-Tibetan Border Police unit at Thakung, near Pangong Tso, logged more than 100 sightings of “Unidentified Luminous Objects” between August and October 2012. Asked in the Lok Sabha in November 2012 whether the Army's Leh-based 14 Corps had reported UFOs over the border, Antony said there was “no conclusive proof” of such sightings.

Press accounts of what the lights were differ. UPI reported that astronomers from the Hanle observatory called them “non celestial”. The Register reported in July 2013 that two astronomers from the Indian Institute of Astrophysics, after theodolite measurements at Thakung, concluded they were the planets Jupiter and Venus.
