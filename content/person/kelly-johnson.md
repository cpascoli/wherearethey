bio: "Clarence “Kelly” Johnson (1910–1990) was Lockheed's leading aircraft designer: chief engineer of its California Division and founder of the Skunk Works, which built the U-2 and the SR-71. In December 1953 he and members of his flight-test staff reported to Air Force intelligence an object they saw at the same time from the ground and from the air off Point Mugu, California."
---

Johnson's report, with signed accounts by Lockheed's engineering test pilot Roy Wimmer, chief flight test engineer Rudy Thoren, flight test engineer Joe Ware and chief aerodynamicist P. A. Colman, went to the Air Technical Intelligence Center in January 1954. It was released in PURSUE release 08 (November 2026), with the Air Force's record card, which concludes “Lenticular Cloud.” The forwarding officer's cover letter says Johnson had been reluctant to write the report and to send it on, believing those who claim to have seen flying saucers are not considered “logical and practical hard-headed engineers”.

Johnson wrote that he had believed in the possibility of flying saucers for at least five years, and that about two years earlier he and his wife had seen a silent, light-blue “flame or emanation” travelling fast near Brents Junction, California. A 1949 memo in the same release shows him pressing the Air Force to interview a Lockheed employee about a sighting over Glendale.
