Irving Newton was the only weather forecaster on duty at Fort Worth Army Air Field on 8 July 1947 when General Roger Ramey called him to his office to look at the debris flown in from Roswell. He identified it on the spot as the remains of a balloon and a RAWIN radar target, an identification the Fort Worth Star-Telegram reported the next day.

In a sworn statement for the Air Force's 1994 investigation he repeated that account, adding that Maj. Jesse Marcel, introduced as the man who found the material, tried to convince him that markings on the sticks were alien writing. He said he had been interviewed and misquoted by many authors over the years, but that the facts remained as he stated them.

*Sources: Fort Worth Star-Telegram, 9 July 1947 · The Roswell Report (U.S. Air Force, 1995), attachment 30*
