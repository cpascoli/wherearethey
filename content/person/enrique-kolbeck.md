Enrique Kolbeck was a radar air-traffic controller at Mexico City International Airport. He is in the archive as one of the witnesses at the Disclosure Project's press conference at the National Press Club on 9 May 2001.

There he said that on 4 March 1992 “we detect 15 objects” west of Toluca airport. On 28 July 1994, he said, a domestic flight had “almost a collision” with an unknown object, and a week later another flight reported a near-collision while the object briefly appeared on radar; pilots then reported bright lights for weeks. On 15 September 1994, he said, the radar held one apparently motionless object for about five hours; radar technicians confirmed the system was working, and the next day people southeast of the airport reported a large disc.

These are his accounts; no Mexican aviation records of the contacts are on file.
