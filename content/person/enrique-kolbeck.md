Enrique Kolbeck was a radar air-traffic controller at Mexico City International Airport. At the Disclosure Project's 2001 National Press Club event he said Mexico City radar detected 15 objects near Toluca airport on 4 March 1992, and that airliners reported near-collisions with unknown objects in July 1994.

*Sources: Disclosure Project press conference (2001)*
