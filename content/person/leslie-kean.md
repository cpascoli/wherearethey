Leslie Kean is an investigative journalist who, with Helene Cooper and Ralph Blumenthal, wrote the *New York Times* article of 16 December 2017 that revealed the Pentagon's $22 million AATIP programme. She is the author of *UFOs: Generals, Pilots, and Government Officials Go on the Record* (2010), built on first-hand accounts from officials, and features in the documentary *The Phenomenon* (2020). She is a reporter rather than a witness under this archive's criteria.
