Jeffrey Nuccetelli is a former U.S. Air Force Security Forces member at Vandenberg Air Force Base, California. Testifying under oath to the House Oversight task force on 9 September 2025, he described five UAP incidents at Vandenberg between 2003 and 2005, one of which he witnessed himself and the others he investigated after six fellow service members came to him, including the October 2003 "Red Square" incursions over missile-defence sites.

*Sources: House Oversight hearing (9 September 2025)*
