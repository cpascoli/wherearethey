Jack Bushong was the National Weather Service meteorologist on duty in Muskegon, Michigan, on the night of 8 March 1994. While talking with emergency dispatch about the flood of UFO calls, he watched three unusually large returns on his weather radar and described them to police.

*Sources: Holland Police Department report 940308035*
