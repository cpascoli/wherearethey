Jack Bushong was the National Weather Service meteorologist on duty in Muskegon, Michigan, on the night of 8 March 1994, when residents of Holland, on the Lake Michigan shore, made a number of 911 calls about moving lights. His radar account is recorded in the Holland police report on the sightings.

According to Officer Jeff Velthouse's report, Bushong was on the phone with central dispatch while watching three “bleeps” on his radar, larger than the pinpoint returns of normal aircraft. He said they held a triangular shape, that one separated and returned and another separated, and that they were at 15,000–25,000 ft. One, he said, moved about 20 miles from the South Haven area over Lake Michigan in roughly 10 seconds, and the Muskegon tower had also seen them on radar.

The report gives his account second-hand, in the officer's words. Bushong said he was “unable to determine what it was”, and the report was closed the next day with no further information.
