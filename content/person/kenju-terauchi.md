Kenju Terauchi was the captain of Japan Air Lines cargo flight 1628, a Boeing 747, with about 29 years' flying experience. On 17 November 1986 over Alaska, he told FAA investigators through interpreters, he saw three unidentified objects: two that came close in front of the cockpit with bright lights and a much larger "mothership" he put at "two times carrier" in size. He said he had seen similar lights before.

After he spoke to Kyodo News in December 1986, Japan Air Lines grounded him and moved him to a desk job.

*Sources: FAA record of interview (1987)*
