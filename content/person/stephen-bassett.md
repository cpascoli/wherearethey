Stephen Bassett is the founder of Paradigm Research Group and a registered lobbyist for UFO disclosure. He is in the archive as the organiser of the 2013 Citizen Hearing on Disclosure. As an advocate rather than a witness, he is outside this archive's sourcing criteria.

Through his group he produced the Citizen Hearing, a privately organised mock congressional hearing held at the National Press Club from 29 April to 3 May 2013. According to his organisation, about forty researchers and military, agency and political witnesses testified over some thirty hours before six former members of Congress, among them Sen. Mike Gravel and Rep. Roscoe Bartlett. The organisation says the full recording was delivered to every member of Congress in November 2014.

It was not an official proceeding of Congress, and statements there were not given under oath. A press report said the former members were paid $20,000 each plus expenses; one of them answered that the fee was for five days of hearings.
