Guy Hottel was Special Agent in Charge of the FBI's Washington Field Office in 1950, when he sent a memo relaying third-hand an informant's claim that three "flying saucers" with small humanoid occupants had been recovered in New Mexico. It is the most-viewed file in the FBI Vault; the FBI notes it was never investigated further and does not mention Roswell.
