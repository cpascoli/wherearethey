Tom DeLonge is a musician, best known with the band Blink-182, who in 2017 co-founded To The Stars Academy of Arts & Science with the physicist Harold Puthoff and the former CIA officer Jim Semivan. The company helped bring the Navy UAP videos and the AATIP story to public attention in December 2017. As an entertainer and entrepreneur rather than a witness, he is outside this archive's sourcing criteria.
