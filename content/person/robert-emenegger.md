Robert Emenegger is a writer, composer and filmmaker. In the early 1970s, by his account while a vice president at Grey Advertising, he and the producer Allan Sandler developed a documentary on UFOs with the cooperation of officials at Norton Air Force Base, California; sources disagree on who commissioned it. The result, *UFOs: Past, Present, and Future* (1974), which he wrote and scored, was narrated by Rod Serling and featured former Project Blue Book officers.

Emenegger says he was promised Air Force footage of a craft landing at Holloman Air Force Base as the film's finale, and that it was withdrawn at the last minute; the film shows a dramatised version instead. He told the story with Paul Shartle on live television in 1988. The film is based on his book of the same name.
