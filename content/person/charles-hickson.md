Charles Hickson was a shipyard worker who, with the young Calvin Parker, reported being taken aboard a craft by three beings while fishing on the Pascagoula River in Mississippi on 11 October 1973. His account was recorded the same night: the Jackson County sheriff's tape of his questioning is filed here, with a 1974 CTV interview.

On the tape he describes a circling blue light hovering a few feet up, about 25–30 yards from the pier; an opening from which three beings came out; two of them floating him inside without force (“He didn't hurt me.”); and an object “like a big eye” moving over him for what he estimated was 20 or 30 minutes. He told the officers he did not want publicity. He later wrote *UFO Contact at Pascagoula* (1983) with William Mendez.

According to the CTV report, a polygraph examiner ran three tests and concluded he was telling the truth; sceptics point out that the examiner was not fully qualified and that Hickson's later accounts grew.

*Sources: Pascagoula incident record · Jackson County sheriff's tape (1973) · CTV W5 (1974)*
