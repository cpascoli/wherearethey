Howard P. Robertson (1903–1961) was a mathematical physicist at the California Institute of Technology. In January 1953 he chaired the CIA's Scientific Advisory Panel on UFOs, the Robertson Panel, which concluded that UFOs posed no direct threat to national security and recommended a public "debunking" and education effort, a recommendation critics see as the origin of the stigma around reporting.

*Sources: Robertson Panel report (1953)*
