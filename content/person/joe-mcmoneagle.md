U.S. Army chief warrant officer recruited in 1978 as “Remote Viewer #001” for the Army’s Grill Flame program, later Stargate. He received the Legion of Merit for his intelligence work before retiring in 1984, and later continued remote-viewing research with SRI and the Cognitive Sciences Laboratory.
