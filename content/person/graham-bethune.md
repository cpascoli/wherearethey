Graham Bethune was a retired U.S. Navy commander and pilot who held a top-secret clearance. On the night of 10 February 1951, flying a Navy aircraft from Keflavik, Iceland, to Argentia, Newfoundland, he says his crew saw a huge circle of white lights on the sea about 300 miles out. When the lights went out, a glowing object rose from the sea, climbed towards their altitude, and flew alongside the aircraft, showing a dome.

At the Disclosure Project's 2001 press conference he said crew members ducking in alarm collided and were injured, the cockpit compass and direction finders failed, and the object was tracked on radar at more than 1,800 miles per hour. He also said 31 passengers saw it, that the Air Force interrogated the crew at Argentia and Navy intelligence took individual reports, and that he holds an 18-page Navy and Air Force report from the National Archives. He said he would testify to it under oath.

That report is not in this archive, so his account rests on his own testimony.

*Sources: Disclosure Project press conference (2001)*
