Graham Bethune was a retired U.S. Navy commander and pilot with a top-secret clearance. At the Disclosure Project's 2001 event he said that on the night of 10 February 1951, flying from Keflavik, Iceland, to Argentia, Newfoundland, his crew saw a circle of lights on the sea and then a glowing object that climbed to their altitude and flew alongside the aircraft.

*Sources: Disclosure Project press conference (2001)*
