Former U.S. Air Force intelligence officer and Afghanistan combat veteran who later worked at the National Reconnaissance Office and the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, where he co-led UAP analysis and represented the NRO on the UAP Task Force.

In 2022 he filed a whistleblower complaint with the Intelligence Community Inspector General alleging that information about UAP crash-retrieval programs was being withheld from Congress. He went public in June 2023 (The Debrief, NewsNation) and testified under oath on July 26, 2023 that the U.S. runs a multi-decade crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering program, to which he was denied access.
