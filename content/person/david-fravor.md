Retired U.S. Navy commander and F/A-18F pilot who commanded Strike Fighter Squadron 41, the “Black Aces”.

On November 14, 2004, flying from USS Nimitz, he was vectored by USS Princeton to radar returns off Baja California and saw a white, wingless “Tic Tac” object about 40 feet long that, he says, mirrored his aircraft and then accelerated out of sight. He has told the story to The New York Times (2017), on the Lex Fridman podcast (2020) and under oath to Congress (2023).
