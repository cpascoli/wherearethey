Ross Coulthart is an Australian investigative journalist (born 1962) and NewsNation's senior special correspondent, who interviewed David Grusch for NewsNation in June 2023. He is the author of *In Plain Sight* (2021) and openly advocates for UFO disclosure. He is a reporter rather than a witness under this archive's criteria.
