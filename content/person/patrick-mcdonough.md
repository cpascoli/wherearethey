Patrick McDonough was a U.S. Air Force geodetic surveyor who fixed precise coordinates for missile and aircraft guidance systems. At the 2010 National Press Club press conference he described a large silent object hovering over a Minuteman silo near Conrad, Montana, in September 1966.

*Sources: National Press Club press conference (2010)*
