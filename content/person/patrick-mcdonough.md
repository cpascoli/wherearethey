Patrick McDonough is a former U.S. Air Force geodetic surveyor with the 1381st Geodetic Survey Squadron (1962–1966). He says the squadron's work was to fix precise coordinates for the inertial guidance systems of missiles and aircraft. He told his story at the 2010 "UFOs and Nukes" press conference at the National Press Club.

He says that in September 1966, while his three-man team surveyed a new Minuteman site southeast of Conrad, Montana, a round object about 50 ft across came from the north and stopped about 300 ft above the open silo, shining a white light into it, for perhaps 20 to 30 seconds, then shot off to the east. Driving away, the team overturned their truck. He says the highway patrolman who came told them his dispatch had received over 20 UFO reports that night.

His account is his own testimony, given 44 years after the event. He says he never heard anything from the Air Force about the incident.
