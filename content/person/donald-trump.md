He says he is "not too much of a believer", but in interviews in October and November 2026 he described meetings with Air Force pilots, "really good, solid people", who told him of a round object passing their F-22s at several times their speed: four times on Newsmax, three times on *60 Minutes*. He has not named the pilots or said when or where the encounters happened.

On *60 Minutes* (8 November 2026) Ross Douthat asked him about whistleblowers' claims of a secret program that even presidents don't know about. He said he hadn't heard of one, and that if there is one he would find out "right after this interview". Jay Stratton, who advises the White House on the releases, said in October that he did not think the President had seen the real evidence.
