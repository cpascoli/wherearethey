Jon T. Kosloski is a physicist and engineer from the U.S. intelligence community who was named director of the Pentagon's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) on 26 August 2024, succeeding Sean Kirkpatrick and acting director Tim Phillips. He has testified to the Senate Armed Services subcommittee on emerging threats, and has said AARO is analysing several "true anomalies" it does not understand.
