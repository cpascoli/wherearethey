Lee Seung-bae is a retired Republic of Korea Air Force colonel, a graduate of the 13th class of the Air Force Academy, with 24 years of flying and about 3,500 flying hours, some 1,800 of them on the F-4D/E Phantom. As a lieutenant colonel he led the two-Phantom formation that watched an unidentified object over North Gyeongsang province on the night of 31 March 1980.

He began speaking about it after retiring in 1989; Monthly Chosun published an interview with him in February 1992, and in 2016 he wrote a detailed report for the Korean UFO research centre KUFOS.
