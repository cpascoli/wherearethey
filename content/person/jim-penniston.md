Jim Penniston was a staff sergeant in the U.S. Air Force Security Police at RAF Woodbridge in December 1980. He was among the patrolmen who went into Rendlesham Forest on 26 December and says he approached and touched a small triangular craft with symbols on its surface; his account is not independently corroborated. He has spoken at the National Press Club (2007) and the Citizen Hearing (2013).

*Sources: Rendlesham incident record · Citizen Hearing (2013)*
