Adam Schiff, a Democrat from California, chaired the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence when its counterterrorism subcommittee, chaired by André Carson, held the open hearing on UAP on 17 May 2022. It was the first public congressional hearing on the subject in more than fifty years. He later moved from the House to the U.S. Senate.

Schiff spoke at the hearing as full committee chair. Among the most fascinating questions, he said, were objects measured by multiple instruments, “and yet it seems to move in directions that are inconsistent with what we know of physics or science more broadly.” The witnesses were Under Secretary of Defense Ronald Moultrie and Deputy Director of Naval Intelligence Scott Bray.

His part in the archive is limited to that hearing; no other statement by him on UAP is filed here.
