Joe Spielberger is senior policy counsel at the Project On Government Oversight (POGO), a nonpartisan watchdog, working on whistleblower protection and government secrecy. He was the minority (Democratic) witness at the House task force hearing of 9 September 2025, testifying on overclassification and whistleblower protections rather than on UAP sightings.

In his statement he called whistleblowers “the first line of defense” against waste, fraud, abuse of power and corruption, said POGO advises members of both parties “to focus on the evidence, not the individual”, and urged Congress to update and expand whistleblower laws. In questioning he said national security whistleblowers are “forced to rely on protection from the same agencies and people who they are alleging retaliated against them”, and named abuse of the security clearance process as one form of retaliation.

He made no statements about UAP themselves.
