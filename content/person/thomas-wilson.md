Retired U.S. Navy vice admiral who directed the Defense Intelligence Agency from 1999 to 2002, after serving as the Joint Staff’s director of intelligence (J-2). He is the subject of the leaked Wilson memo, which claims he was refused access to a contractor-held UAP reverse-engineering program. The memo’s authenticity has not been officially confirmed.
