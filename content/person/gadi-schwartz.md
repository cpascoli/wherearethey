Gadi Schwartz is an NBC News correspondent. He is in the archive as the moderator of the panel “Security & Defense: National Security, UAP, and Emerging Threats” at Disclosure Forum 2026, an all-day public forum on UAP transparency held on 25 June 2026 in the Senate's Kennedy Caucus Room and organised by the Disclosure Foundation.

His panel brought together Rear Adm. Tim Gallaudet, Christopher Mellon, Kirk McConnell and Marik von Rennenkampff. It discussed the Langley drone incursions, UAP around ships and test ranges, special access program oversight, whistleblower protections and the PURSUE releases; the Foundation published the recording on 28 August 2026. He is a reporter, not a witness.
