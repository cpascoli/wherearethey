L. Mendel Rivers, a Democratic U.S. Representative from South Carolina, chaired the House Armed Services Committee from 1965 to 1970, including its hearing on UFOs on 5 April 1966, called after Gerald Ford's request for an inquiry.

*Sources: House Armed Services hearing (1966)*
