Lonnie Zamora was a police officer in Socorro, New Mexico. On the afternoon of 24 April 1964, while chasing a speeding car, he reported seeing an egg-shaped craft and two small figures in coveralls in a gully south of town, and the craft lifting off with a roar and flame. His sighting became one of the best-documented landing cases in Project Blue Book, which listed it as unidentified.

*Sources: Project Blue Book case file, Socorro 1964 (NARA)*
