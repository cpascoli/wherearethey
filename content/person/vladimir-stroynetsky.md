Vladimir Ivanovich Stroynetsky was a major serving in a military unit outside Moscow during the spring 1990 wave of sightings along the Yaroslavl Highway. He reported the lights to the newspaper *Rabochaya Tribuna* and then described them on camera to Soviet state television's *Vremya*.

His account came weeks after the 21 March 1990 air-defence alert in the same region.

*Sources: CIA/FBIS FB PN 90-123; Vremya, 30 April 1990 (Gosteleradiofond)*
