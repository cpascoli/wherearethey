Isaac Ben-Israel is a professor at Tel Aviv University, a retired major general of the Israeli Air Force and chairman of the Israel Space Agency. He is in the archive as the official who answered Haim Eshed's claims of December 2020.

Eshed, the retired general who had led Israel's space-security programme, told *Yediot Aharonot* that the U.S. government had for years been in contact with extraterrestrials from a “Galactic Federation”. Ben-Israel told *The Times of Israel* that Eshed had gone too far with his claims and that human–alien encounters were unlikely, while defending Eshed's standing in his field.

His response is filed as a dispute of both Eshed's interview and the book it was given to promote, Hagar Yanai's *The Universe Beyond the Horizon* (2020). NASA, asked by NBC News, said it had yet to find signs of extraterrestrial life.
