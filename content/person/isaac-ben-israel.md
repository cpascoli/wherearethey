Isaac Ben-Israel is a professor at Tel Aviv University, a retired Israeli Air Force major general and chairman of the Israel Space Agency. When the former space-programme head Haim Eshed claimed in December 2020 that the U.S. was in contact with a "Galactic Federation", Ben-Israel said Eshed had gone "too far" and that human–alien encounters were unlikely.
