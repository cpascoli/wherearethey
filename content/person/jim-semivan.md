bio: "Jim Semivan is a retired CIA operations officer who spent 25 years in the Agency's Directorate of Operations, overseas and at home, and retired in 2007 as a member of its Senior Intelligence Service. In 2017 he co-founded To The Stars Academy of Arts & Science with Tom DeLonge and Hal Puthoff, and served as its Vice President Operations and a director."
---

Semivan's career comes from To The Stars Academy's own filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission (2019 and 2020), which also describe his consulting for the Intelligence Community after he left the CIA. Hal Puthoff named him among the company's principals when presenting it in 2018, alongside the former Skunk Works director Steve Justice.

To The Stars helped bring the Navy UAP videos and the AATIP story to public attention in December 2017, and employed Luis Elizondo and Chris Mellon.

*Sources: To The Stars Academy SEC filings (offering supplement, 15 July 2019; preliminary offering circular, 1 September 2020) · Hal Puthoff's 2018 presentation*
