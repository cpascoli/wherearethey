Mona Nemer is a Canadian scientist who was first appointed Chief Science Advisor of Canada in 2017 and reappointed in 2024. Before that she was Professor and Vice-President of Research at the University of Ottawa and directed its Molecular Genetics and Cardiac Regeneration Laboratory; her research in molecular cardiology concerned heart failure and the genetics of cardiac birth defects. She holds a PhD in chemistry from McGill University.

As Chief Science Advisor she launched the Sky Canada Project in the fall of 2022, a review of how reports of unidentified aerial phenomena from the public are handled in Canada. Her office published its final report in June 2025, with fourteen recommendations. In her foreword she stresses that the project was about reporting services, not about what UAPs are.
