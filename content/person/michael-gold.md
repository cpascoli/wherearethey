Michael Gold is a former NASA associate administrator and a member of the independent study team NASA convened on UAP, which reported in 2023. He testified at the House Oversight hearing of 13 November 2024, arguing that science is the best tool for understanding UAP and that stigma, including threats against members of NASA's study team, stands in the way, and spoke at the congressional briefing of 1 May 2025. He appears in *The Age of Disclosure* (2025).
