Meng Zhaoguo was a forestry worker at the Red Flag Forest Farm in Heilongjiang, China, who said that in June 1994 he saw an object crash on a mountainside, was struck by a beam of light when he approached, and later had repeated encounters with beings. Chinese UFO researchers investigated the case. As a private individual his account is outside this archive's core sourcing criteria.
