Delbert C. Newhouse was a U.S. Navy photographer who rose to Chief Warrant Officer 4. On 2 July 1952, driving near Tremonton, Utah, he saw a group of bright objects and filmed them. The Tremonton film became one of the most analysed UFO films of the 1950s, reviewed by the Navy's Photographic Interpretation Center, Project Blue Book and the Robertson Panel.

Blue Book assessed him as honest and reliable. He described the sighting on camera in the 1956 documentary *Unidentified Flying Objects*.

*Sources: PURSUE records DOW-UAP-D102, D104 (Department of War/AARO, 2026)*
