Colm Kelleher is a biochemist who worked on UAP research for Robert Bigelow at BAASS, the contractor for the Pentagon's AAWSAP, alongside the DIA's James Lacatski.

With Lacatski and the journalist George Knapp he co-wrote *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon* (2021) and *Inside the US Government Covert UFO Program: Initial Revelations* (2023), whose manuscript the Pentagon cleared for publication in April 2023. Asked in October 2023 about the craft Lacatski described, he said there was not enough data for a firm assessment.
