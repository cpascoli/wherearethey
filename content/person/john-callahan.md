John Callahan was Division Chief of Accidents and Investigations at the Federal Aviation Administration in the 1980s. He says he briefed the radar data from the November 1986 Japan Air Lines flight 1628 sighting to officials including CIA personnel, who told those present that the meeting "never happened". He spoke at the Disclosure Project event in 2001 and the Citizen Hearing in 2013.

*Sources: Disclosure Project press conference (2001) · Citizen Hearing (2013)*
