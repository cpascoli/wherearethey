Sandra Larson of Fargo, North Dakota, said that in August 1975, while being driven to Bismarck with her daughter and her daughter's boyfriend, the three saw glowing orange spheres over Interstate 94 and lost about an hour; under later hypnosis she described being examined aboard a craft. As a private individual her account is outside this archive's core sourcing criteria.
