Michael Shellenberger is an American journalist and author who publishes the newsletter Public. He is known for books and reporting on energy, the environment and government censorship. He came to UAP as a reporter on government secrecy rather than as a believer, and calls himself agnostic about what the phenomenon is.

His first UAP story covered the 1996 Varginha creature case in Brazil, where he had lived. He returned to the subject after David Grusch's 2023 testimony and set out to verify Grusch's claims. In November 2024 a source gave him a 12-page whistleblower report describing "Immaculate Constellation", said to be an unacknowledged special access program that pools UAP imagery and signature intelligence from military platforms. Testifying on November 13, 2024 alongside Tim Gallaudet, Luis Elizondo and Michael Gold, he had the report entered into the record. He also relayed a second source's account of a video of orbs rising from the sea off Kuwait.

He told Congress the report did not look like circular reporting and that other sources confirmed the program's name. He also conceded he had not verified every part of it and did not know where the full version is held, and he said he does not know what UAP are. Grusch later referred to Immaculate Constellation as a National Security Council program but would not discuss it. The Defense Department has not publicly acknowledged the program. Shellenberger calls for a Church Committee-style reform of intelligence secrecy.
