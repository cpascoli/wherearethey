Lt. Gen. Otto P. Weyland commanded the U.S. Far East Air Forces during the Korean War. After B-29 crews over Wonsan and Sunchon reported orange lights pacing their bombers in January 1952, he told the press: "We can't say there's nothing to it, but unless we can get more data, I don't think we can expect a conclusive answer."

*Sources: Wonsan–Sunchon incident record*
