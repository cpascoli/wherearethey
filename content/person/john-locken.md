John Locken (spelled Lockem in the American reports of the time) was a South African Police constable. On patrol with Constable Koos de Klerk on the Pretoria–Bronkhorstspruit highway just after midnight on 16 September 1965, he said the headlights of their van lit a domed, copper-coloured disc standing on the road, which lifted off within seconds, emitting flames: "Its lift-off was quicker than anything I have ever seen."

His account is known from the 1965 wire reports, as summarised by NICAP; a British paper reported the next day that police and scientists were investigating. No police record of the inquiry or its outcome has been found.

*Sources: South Wales Echo, 17 September 1965 · NICAP, UFOs: A New Look (1969), citing Associated Press, 17 September 1965*
