A 54-minute interview from NewsNation's Reality Check series, recorded for the January 2025 special in which Jake Barber went public. Ross Coulthart questions Don Paul Bales, a military and law-enforcement veteran who says he has known Barber since 1996, on Barber's background and character, Bales's own work on Barber's team, the Toughbook recovery attempts and the psionic sessions. Coulthart interviewed Barber's teammates separately; John Blitch says he had not seen the others' interviews before his own.

Bales vouches for Barber's account of a covert career behind his aircraft-mechanic record and says he has seen credentials and orders Barber was issued, but declines to describe the classified work. His own first-hand sighting claims are limited; much of the interview is his opinion on secrecy, disclosure and faith. NewsNation re-aired the interview in September 2026 as "Military veteran backs claims about a secret UAP program".
