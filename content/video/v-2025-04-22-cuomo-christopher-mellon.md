A one-hour interview on Chris Cuomo's podcast with Christopher Mellon, former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Senate Intelligence Committee staff director. Mellon argues that the government cannot identify the drones over its own bases, that a classification guide has kept unclassified UAP video from the public in breach of a Navy pledge to Congress, that secret programs can stay hidden for decades, and that Congress should ask about NORAD's strip-alert launches and commission an oral history of the subject.

Transcribed locally with Whisper (YouTube's captions were unavailable); some names are misheard ("Sturek" for Sturrock, "Kozlowski" for Kosloski, "Aero" for AARO).
