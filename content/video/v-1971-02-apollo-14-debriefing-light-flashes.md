The first part of the Apollo 14 post-flight debriefing at the Manned Spacecraft Center in February 1971, released by NASA in PURSUE release 04: a medical debriefing of Alan Shepard, Stuart Roosa and Edgar Mitchell, which ends, at 1:31:19, with questions about the light flashes they counted during the flight. The light-flash discussion continues, with some overlap, in NASA-UAP-D027. AARO describes the flashes as cosmic rays passing through the eye and striking the retina.

*Source: NASA, released by the Department of War in PURSUE (NASA-UAP-D026), via DVIDS. Whisper transcript.*
