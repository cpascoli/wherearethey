The second set of excerpts, about ten minutes, from Linda Moulton Howe's 1998 interview with the anonymous "Agent Kewper": his account of being taken past recovered craft, German and alien, of drawings he says he saw in the Roswell file, and of a being in an interview room. In Dolan's 2013 interview for *The Anonymous Interview* he tells the same visit more briefly.

He is anonymous and unconfirmed, and these are his claims alone; the descriptions of the Roswell file echo, and Howe herself compares them with, those in Philip Corso's 1997 book. The audio is an unofficial archive copy kept by researchers on GitHub; the archive streams its own copy.
