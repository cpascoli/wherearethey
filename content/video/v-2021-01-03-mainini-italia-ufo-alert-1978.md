An interview with retired General Giulio Mainini, recorded on 16 April 2019 at Cameri air base by Alberto Negri and Sabrina Pieragostini, for Spazio Tesla and Extremamente; it was published on the Spazio Tesla Channel on 3 January 2021. Mainini tells how he came to fly, then his September 1978 sighting from an F-104, the UFO office at the Air Force's intelligence department, and his views on the 2017 Pentagon videos and on air defence. In Italian. Transcribed locally with Whisper.

His 1978 account in La Stampa does not include the chase: there he said he kept to his course and the light vanished after three minutes (see the incident file).
