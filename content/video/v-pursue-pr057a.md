An infrared video from a U.S. military platform over the Yellow Sea in January 2023, uploaded to a classified network in June 2024 under two titles, “Spherical UAP in clouds” and “[Platform] Observes UAP in East China Sea 05 JAN 2023 INDOPACOM”; released as PR057a and PR057b in PURSUE release 02. It matches item 22 of the House Oversight Task Force letter, the MQ-9 video Kevin Campbell says is “99%” likely one of his; the release does not identify the platform or the operators.
