The regular morning press conference of Japan's Chief Cabinet Secretary, Minoru Kihara, on 11 May 2026, three days after the Pentagon's first release of UAP files (PURSUE). Asked about a U.S. infrared video taken near Japan in 2023, Kihara said he had seen the published footage, that Japan gathers and analyses information on unidentifiable aerial objects with the United States, and that any Japanese disclosure would be judged case by case.

Several foreign outlets reported that he confirmed for the first time that Japan holds UAP footage of its own. He does not say so in this recording: he says only that disclosure would be weighed case by case, including whether it would reveal Japan's intelligence-gathering capabilities.

*Source: Government of Japan (Kantei press conference video, via Gov-Online). The Kantei publishes no transcript of the questions and answers; Japanese transcript by Whisper.*
