Rep. Eric Burlison talks to Chrissy Newton on her podcast *Rebelliously Curious* about the UAP disclosure effort in Congress, international cooperation, and David Grusch's departure from his staff in September 2026. He says his office is still trying to verify Grusch's claims, including footage of so-called Nordic beings that Grusch has described, and that he will make no claim until he has proof; no such footage has been made public.
