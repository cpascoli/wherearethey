Interviews recorded in Roswell on 11–12 July 1990, during a reunion of 509th Bomb Group veterans, and now held by the National Archives among the records of the Air Force's declassification team (RG 341, local identifier 341-ROSWELL-19, "Roswell Reunion: Number 2"). The questioners are civilian researchers; one is addressed as Kevin.

Jesse Marcel Jr. gives the fullest early account of the night in July 1947 when his father, Maj. Jesse Marcel, brought debris home: foil-like material, brittle black pieces and small I-beams with violet symbols. He is careful about what he cannot remember, including whether his father used the words "flying saucer". Vern Maltais relays Barney Barnett's second-hand story of bodies, and the B-29 flight engineer Robert Porter describes loading light, paper-wrapped packages for Fort Worth, saying himself that his doubt about the balloon comes from what others told him. The Air Force's 1994 report quotes the 1991 affidavits of Marcel Jr. and Porter alongside its MOGUL explanation.

*Source: National Archives, RG 341 (via the Internet Archive, public domain). Whisper transcript.*
