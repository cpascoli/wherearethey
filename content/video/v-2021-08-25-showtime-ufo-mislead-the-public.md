An official clip from episode 3 of *UFO*, the 2021 Showtime documentary series, in which former AFOSI agent Richard Doty is confronted about the disinformation fed to Albuquerque researcher Paul Bennewitz. He says he acted on his supervisors' orders, to stop Bennewitz revealing a secret laser at Kirtland Air Force Base by encouraging his belief that he had seen UFOs.
