A segment of Antena 3's *Cita con la Vida* broadcast on 10 April 1996, after the Spanish Air Force declassified its Canary Islands files. The first part covers the June 1976 sightings on Gran Canaria with civilian witnesses. Then retired Lt. Gen. Carlos Dolz de Espejo, who commanded the Canary Islands Air Zone in 1976, describes in the studio the phenomenon he saw from the cockpit on 19 November 1976, alongside researcher José Guijarro. Transcribed locally with Whisper from a Dailymotion upload of the broadcast; the archive links the upload, not the broadcaster's master.
