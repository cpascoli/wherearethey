The *60 Minutes* segment in which Bill Whitaker interviewed retired Navy pilots David Fravor and Alex Dietrich about the November 2004 Nimitz "Tic Tac" encounter, broadcast on 16 May 2021, a month before the ODNI's preliminary UAP assessment. It was Dietrich's first major television appearance. Both describe the white, wingless object over disturbed water, its erratic, "ping pong ball" movement and its sudden disappearance. Dietrich stresses that they could not classify it and should not jump to conclusions. Chris Mellon adds the radar operators' accounts of extreme acceleration. This upload is the official 6-minute excerpt published by *60 Minutes*.
