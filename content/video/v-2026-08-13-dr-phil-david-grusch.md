David Grusch interviewed by Phil McGraw on the Dr. Phil Podcast, recorded around 11 August 2026. Grusch repeats and extends his account of a decades-long program to recover and exploit non-human craft: he describes video and photos of craft and bodies shown to him by program insiders, puts the number of recovered craft at between 25 and 100, says some occupants were taken alive, and says he first learned of U.S. retrieval efforts from a foreign adversary's intelligence reporting. He also describes the retaliation he says he faced, his work for Rep. Eric Burlison, and AARO's failure to follow up his leads. All of it is his own, unverified account; he says he was not hands-on and did not see the material in person. Transcribed with Whisper.
