Part two of Ryan Graves's interview with retired Air Force test pilot Dan Isbell on the Safe Aerospace Podcast. Isbell describes three later sightings, none from an aircraft: a roughly 300-foot black triangle over Edwards Air Force Base in 1990, and two encounters with lights and a sphere at his Florida homes in 2014 and 2016. He also discusses meeting George Knapp and Jeremy Corbell.
