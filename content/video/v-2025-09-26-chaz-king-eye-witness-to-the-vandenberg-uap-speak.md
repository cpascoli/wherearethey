A 76-minute episode of Jeff Nuccetelli's podcast, with his co-host Tom, in which Chaz King, who served under Nuccetelli in Vandenberg's Security Forces in the early 2000s, gives his first-hand account of the night of the October 2003 incursions. As area supervisor at Space Launch Complex 4 he says he and four others watched a light over the Pacific turn into a dark square object the size of a football field, which hovered silently near the coast for a minute or so and then shot away north.

Nuccetelli adds what happened next as he tells it: the statements and police reports, the lack of any rules of engagement, OSI's hostile debriefing of a later witness, and his own reports to Congress, AARO and the FBI. King says he spoke only briefly with Ryan Graves and was never contacted by AARO.

Both men speak twenty years after the events. King's description of a square craft differs from Nuccetelli's sworn description of a triangle, and he counts five witnesses including himself where *The Age of Disclosure* quotes him as "myself and five". The first half of the episode is about their police work on the base and is not UAP-related.
