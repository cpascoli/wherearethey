Ross Coulthart interviews physicist Randy Bostick, former Science Advisor to AARO, at the Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies conference in Toronto (24–26 July 2026); NewsNation published the episode on 3 November 2026. Bostick explains what AARO does and does not hold, walks through his work on the GoFast case resolution, describes AARO's Gremlin sensor system, and argues that the main obstacle is military sensor data that is never saved. He speaks for himself, not for AARO.
