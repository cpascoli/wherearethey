George Knapp interviews his co-authors James Lacatski, the DIA's AAWSAP program manager, and Colm Kelleher, BAASS's program manager in Las Vegas, as *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon* came out in October 2021. They describe AAWSAP as wholly UFO-focused, the difference between AAWSAP and AATIP, BAASS's staffing and databases, and the medical cases at the heart of the book. Transcribed with Whisper; the automatic transcript wrongly labelled every line with Lacatski's name, so speakers here are identified from the questions.
