Gary Voorhis, a former AEGIS and CEC technician on the USS Princeton during the 2004 Nimitz encounters and a co-founder of UAPx, interviewed by the Alternative Science Network at the Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies conference in Huntsville in 2024. He describes the tracks he saw before and after Fravor's intercept, relays a second-hand story about the sonar team, says the ship never treated the objects as a threat, and recounts UAPx's meeting with AARO's Sean Kirkpatrick.
