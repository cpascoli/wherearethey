An 81-minute episode of the Safe Aerospace Podcast in which Ryan Graves interviews Benjamin Read, a former Marine F/A-18 Hornet pilot of about 18 years and now an airline captain. The first half covers his career, from enlisted helicopter avionics technician to Hornet pilot, his spinal injury, Task Force Personnel Recovery in Iraq and his time as a general's aide; the second, the May 2021 encounter after departing Houston, in which the controller called unidentified traffic and Read saw a mercury-like object the size of a 737 hover and then leave.

Read hedges his own account throughout: expectation bias, time compression, a duration of "four seconds, maybe less", a photo that showed nothing, and his concession that the object was probably never at risk of being hit.
