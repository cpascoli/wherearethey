Jimmy Akin and Dom Bettinelli discuss Kenneth Arnold's sighting of 24 June 1947 and the explanations offered for it. Its value for the archive is the archival recording it plays in full, from 10:20 to 16:20: Arnold's radio interview of the day after the sighting, in which he describes, in his own words and before the term “flying saucer” took hold, the chain of objects he timed between Mount Rainier and Mount Adams.

The rest of the episode is the hosts' commentary, citing among others the optical physicist Bruce Maccabee.

*Source: Jimmy Akin's Mysterious World (SQPN), YouTube, 2019. Whisper transcript.*
