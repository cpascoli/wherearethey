The UFO segment (37:36–40:33) of Ross Douthat's White House interview with President Trump for CBS's *60 Minutes*, broadcast on 8 November 2026 after the midterm elections; the network posted the full 52-minute extended interview on YouTube the next day, and a shorter version of the exchange is in the broadcast (from 22:50). Trump says he is "not too much of a believer", but that Air Force pilots he met in the Oval Office described a round object passing their F-22s at three times their speed. Asked whether whistleblowers are right that a secret program exists that even presidents don't know about, he says he hasn't heard of one, and that if there is one he will find out "right after this interview".

He doesn't name the pilots or say when or where the encounters happened. In October Jay Stratton, who advises the White House on the file releases, said he did not think the President had seen the real evidence: "he's still being shielded from the truth".
