A short excerpt of Milton Torres's talk at the X-Conference in 2009, describing the night of 20 May 1957 when, flying an F-86D from RAF Manston, he locked on to a radar target over East Anglia, was ordered to fire, and saw it leave his radar in seconds. Here he says the debriefer came from the embassy; in his 1988 written account in the MoD file he describes a civilian who appeared the next day.
