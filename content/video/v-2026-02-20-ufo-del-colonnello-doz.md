Interview by journalist Sabrina Pieragostini for her blog Extremamente, recorded together with Antonio Virzì's podcast *Something is Up* and cut from the same conversation (the long version is on file). Retired Air Force colonel Roberto Doz recounts his career and his sighting of 13 December 1985 at the San Cesareo motorway toll booth, south-east of Rome, and what speaking about it meant for a serving pilot.
