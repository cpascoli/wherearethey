Ross Coulthart interviews David Burkett, a cybersecurity researcher and the author of *Lies Above: How the Pentagon Misled Congress and Cost America Its Skies* (2026), on NewsNation's *Reality Check*. Burkett presents the government's handling of UAP as a scandal whatever the objects are, citing the Condon Committee, the 1967 Malmstrom missile shutdown and the 2025 report that it was an EMP test, the New Jersey and Langley drone incursions, and the missing Nimitz sensor data. Most of what he says is relayed from others or his own reading of the record.
