Excerpts, about eleven minutes, of the journalist Linda Moulton Howe's interview with an anonymous man who said he had worked for the CIA from the late 1950s under a cover name, aired under the pseudonym "Agent Kewper" on Dreamland and Coast to Coast AM in 1998. In this part he describes a meeting in the spring of 1959 at which he and his CIA superior showed President Eisenhower and Vice President Nixon photographs of discs, some of which he took for German craft from Argentina.

Fifteen years later the same man repeated much of this, in less detail, to Richard Dolan for Jeremy Corbell's *The Anonymous Interview* (2013). He is anonymous: his identity and service are unconfirmed, and nothing he describes is backed by a record. The audio is an unofficial archive copy, kept by researchers on GitHub; the archive streams its own copy.

*Source: richgel999/uap_resources on GitHub ("Audio 01"). Whisper transcript; Howe's questions and his answers are not always separated.*
