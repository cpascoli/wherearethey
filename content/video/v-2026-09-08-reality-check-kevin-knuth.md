Ross Coulthart interviews the physicist Kevin Knuth, recorded at the Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies' 2026 conference in Toronto, about his 2019 peer-reviewed paper estimating the accelerations of the Nimitz Tic Tac and other craft, the Malmstrom missile shutdowns, Sean Kirkpatrick's 2023 Senate testimony, UAPx's sensor work and the limits placed on the UAP Science Advisory Council. Knuth's figures for the Nimitz descent rest on Kevin Day's account of the radar, not on the radar data. Coulthart's summary that Kirkpatrick denied any peer-reviewed work existed goes further than the hearing record: Kirkpatrick told those with alternative views to submit their research to peer-reviewed journals.

The opening and middle of the interview also cover Knuth's own childhood premonition, an apparition he and his son say they saw in their Albany home, and the 1988 Montana cattle mutilations; these are personal accounts outside the archive's criteria and are not curated as moments.
