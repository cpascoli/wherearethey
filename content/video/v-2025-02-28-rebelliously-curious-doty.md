A two-hour interview with former AFOSI agent Richard Doty by Chrissy Newton for her podcast *Rebelliously Curious* (The Debrief), published in February 2025. It is his most detailed on-record account of the deception operation against Paul Bennewitz: how the case began, how he and his colleagues encouraged Bennewitz's belief that the classified aircraft he saw were UFOs, where the reporting went, and the document he was ordered to show the journalist Linda Moulton Howe in 1983. Doty also repeats his claims that the phenomenon is real. Transcribed with Whisper; names in the auto-transcript are often misspelled.
