A longer excerpt from Gordon Cooper's Mercury-Atlas 9 flight: sunrise over the haze layer, brilliant white particles drifting from the capsule, the release of a flashing xenon beacon he later finds in the dark, and the “fireflies” that come off whenever he fires a thruster, which ties them to the spacecraft itself.
