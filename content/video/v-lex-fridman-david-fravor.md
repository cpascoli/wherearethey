Lex Fridman's long-form interview with Cmdr. David Fravor (U.S. Navy, ret.), recorded in 2020. After an hour on his flying career, Fravor gives a detailed first-hand account of the November 2004 Tic Tac encounter off southern California, answers skeptic Mick West's explanations, and discusses the Gimbal and GoFast cases, secrecy and SpaceX.
