Jesse Michels interviews former Air Force geospatial-intelligence specialist Dylan Borland, who testified to Congress in September 2025. He gives a longer account of his 2012 Langley triangle sighting, describes learning of a program while at BAE Systems, and details his dealings with AARO, the IC Inspector General and the ODNI. Most of his program claims cannot be checked; he frames many answers as hypothetical or declines to name people.
