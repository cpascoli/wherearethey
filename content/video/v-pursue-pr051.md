A five-minute video from CENTCOM's area over Syria in 2021, edited by someone before its upload to a classified network in June 2024: an excerpt, enhanced replays, then the original. Item 2 of the House Oversight Task Force letter, released as PR051 in PURSUE release 02. When a tracking sensor stops panning, a distant object can appear to dart out of frame; the record makes no assessment.
