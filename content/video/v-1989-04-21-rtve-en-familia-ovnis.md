An episode of TVE's talk programme *En familia*, broadcast on 21 April 1989, ten years after the Manises case was classified. Its guests are retired Lt. Gen. Carlos Dolz de Espejo on the 1976 Canary Islands phenomenon, Iberia captain Juan Ignacio Lorenzo on his 1968 encounter near Sagunto, pilot and journalist José Antonio Silva, and Barajas controller Pablo. Lt. Col. Trinidad of the Air Staff answers for the Air Force on why UFO reports were classified, and a scientist, Dr. Bautista, also takes part. The last part features contactee claims, which the guests receive sceptically. Transcribed locally with Whisper from RTVE's archive copy; the archive links RTVE Play.
