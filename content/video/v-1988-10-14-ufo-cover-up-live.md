*UFO Cover-Up?: Live!* was a two-hour syndicated television special broadcast live from Washington, D.C., on 14 October 1988, hosted by Mike Farrell. It mixed credible witnesses, such as former Air Force officers and an airline captain, with the Roswell story, the Gulf Breeze photographs, regression hypnosis and two anonymous government informants, "Falcon" and "Condor", shown in shadow, who described MJ-12, a captured alien kept by the government and aliens' fondness for ice cream.

The informants are now generally identified as the AFOSI agent Richard Doty (Falcon) and Robert Collins (Condor). Doty has since described his role in deception operations aimed at UFO researchers, and the MJ-12 papers promoted on the show are widely regarded as forgeries. The broadcast also carries Paul Shartle's only well-known on-camera account of the Holloman landing film. Uploaded by Alejandro Rojas; auto-captions.
