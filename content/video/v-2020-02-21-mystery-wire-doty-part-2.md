Part 2 of George Knapp's 2019 interview with former AFOSI agent Richard Doty (Mystery Wire, February 2020). Doty says the FBI investigated and cleared him over the MJ-12 documents, that the released documents were not genuine though in his view based on real ones, and that a program existed to tell people who saw secret U.S. aircraft that they had seen UFOs.
