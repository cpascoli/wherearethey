A 98-minute episode of Jeff Nuccetelli's podcast, with co-host Tom Vernon, in which Grant Best, a Security Forces patrolman at Vandenberg in 2003, describes the night in October 2003 when he called in an unauthorized aircraft by the flight line: a silent, lit, layered object close to the size of a football field that came down near the flight line for about 15 seconds and left towards the coast. He also describes the next day's questioning by men who said they were OSI, the calls from local police and the sheriff about objects over the coast, and a "tic-tac type" object he and others watched over the ocean.

Best says the object did not land, dates it to about 15 or 16 October 2003, describes the OSI questioning as routine, and says he had forgotten the event for about twenty years; these points differ from Nuccetelli's accounts of the same sighting.
