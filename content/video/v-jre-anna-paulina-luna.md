Rep. Anna Paulina Luna, who chairs the House Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets, talks with Joe Rogan in August 2025. The first half hour and a later stretch cover UAP: the Eglin AFB visit that led to the task force, what she says she has seen in classified settings, AARO, and the obstacles to disclosure; much of the rest covers the JFK files, religion and other topics.
