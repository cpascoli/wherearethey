NASA's air-to-ground and public affairs audio from Gemini 7, released in PURSUE release 01: four hours into the flight, Frank Borman reports a “bogey” at ten o'clock high, which he distinguishes from the spent booster, then a stream of small particles a few miles off; Jim Lovell describes the booster tumbling in the sunlight. The NASA commentator calls the bogey an unidentified object. Neither the crew nor NASA identifies it on the tape.

*Source: NASA, released by the Department of War in PURSUE (NASA-UAP-D003A), via DVIDS. Whisper transcript.*
