NewsNation's 42-minute special, presented by Ross Coulthart, in which Jake Barber first went public. He describes the night he says he flew a white "egg" about 20 feet long out of a desert test range, a second object with eight sections, the illness he says struck his team after an escort mission, the Toughbook recovery missions and his fear of being set up, his confrontation with his organization's director of security, and Skywatcher's attempt to summon a craft in August 2024. Don Paul Bales, John Blitch and Garry Nolan speak in support, and NewsNation shows video it says comes from the retrieval program.

NewsNation says it checked Barber's records and confirmed he was an Air Force airman and aerospace mechanic in the late 1990s, with documents showing his recruitment into combat control, and that he met Senator Rubio's staff in fall 2024. The program, the retrievals and the source of the video are not confirmed, and the special carries no response from the Pentagon, AARO or an outside expert. Barber's later interview with Jesse Michels gives fuller and in places different detail.
