A 2025 report by Peru's state broadcaster TVPerú on the 11 April 1980 La Joya incident, with a new interview with former Peruvian Air Force pilot Oscar Santa María Huertas and a second eyewitness from the base, Julio Chamorro. Santa María repeats his account of being ordered to destroy a balloon-like, luminous object over the base, firing 64 cannon rounds at it without effect, and chasing it to about 63,000 feet. He shows what he says is a U.S. Defense Department report on the case dated 3 June 1980. Chamorro describes the object changing from a shapeless "deflating balloon" into a sphere and climbing vertically when the jet took off. The report's own introduction gives the year as 1981, but Santa María says 1980. Spanish; transcribed locally with Whisper, moments summarised in English.
