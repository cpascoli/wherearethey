A digitization of the 16mm Kodachrome film that U.S. Navy Chief Warrant Officer Delbert C. Newhouse shot near Tremonton, Utah, on 2 July 1952 and sent to the Air Force; one of Project Blue Book's visual cases. Released as PR159 in PURSUE release 06; the records submitted with it (D102) say it was originally paired with Idaho mountain scenery, not preserved here.
