An informal, unedited conversation between retired Navy pilot Alex Dietrich, the second pilot in the 2004 Nimitz "Tic Tac" encounter, and the sceptical UFO analyst Mick West, recorded in June 2021 and published on West's channel. They go through the leaked "event summary" document line by line. Dietrich cannot vouch for its provenance, but recognises much of its detail. She explains why her own account lasts about 10 seconds against Fravor's several minutes, and gives the background to her very limited vantage point as a junior wingman.

It is valuable counter-balance in the archive. Dietrich says she has no idea what the object was, does not rule out a natural phenomenon or human activity, and calls West's parallax theory as plausible as any other. She says there is no conspiracy, and she speaks mainly to make it easier for others to report. The conversation was transcribed from YouTube's automatic captions.
