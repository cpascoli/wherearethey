A 15-minute interview on the Hebrew-language channel Anomaly with Dror Gorlik, a former Israeli Air Force F-15 pilot and squadron commander, who tells on camera and under his full name, for the first time, of an object he saw while returning to land at Tel Nof Air Base in 1998. He is interviewed by his son, who writes and presents the channel. It opens with a montage of officials' statements on UAP from the U.S. and Brazil.

The interview is in Hebrew. The transcript is the archive's English translation of the channel's own Hebrew subtitles, line by line with the same timings (the Hebrew is kept beside it), and the moments quote that translation, not his exact words.
