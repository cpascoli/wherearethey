A 26-minute interview with retired Lt. Cmdr. Alex Dietrich published by the American Veterans Center in March 2025. She covers her path into naval aviation, beginning with a first training flight on 11 September 2001, her two combat deployments with VFA-41, and her account of the November 2004 Nimitz encounter as the junior wingman. She describes a 30–40-foot white object with no flight surfaces or propulsion that broke every rule of the flight envelope, and the Princeton's radar "glitches" that led to the intercept. She explains why she keeps to the word "unidentified": she does not know what it was, says the chain of command set it aside, backs AARO and better reporting channels, and stays open to a non-prosaic explanation.
