The first part of the Apollo 17 post-flight debriefing, released by NASA in PURSUE release 04: a long technical review of rendezvous, docking, lunar dust, cameras and the cabin. Two passages bear on sightings: a faint flash during rendezvous traced to the lunar module's own tracking light, and, at 48:33, the light-flash experiment, during which the blindfolded crew saw no flashes although they saw them before and after. (“Blinded” in the transcript is presumably “blindfolded”.)

*Source: NASA, released by the Department of War in PURSUE (NASA-UAP-D028), via DVIDS. Whisper transcript.*
