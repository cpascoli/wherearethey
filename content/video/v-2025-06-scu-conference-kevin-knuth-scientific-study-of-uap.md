Kevin Knuth's talk at the Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies' 2025 conference in Huntsville, Alabama, presenting the review of scientific UAP studies he led for a special issue of *Progress in Aerospace Sciences*. It opens with what he considers good scientific practice (sharing data with trusted colleagues, peer review, no public claims before the work is understood, and the harm done by non-disclosure agreements), then summarises the history the review covers: the 1947 wave, the green fireballs, Hynek's early debunking, physical-evidence cases and unidentified submerged objects.

Its judgments on Roswell and on AARO's historical report are his opinions, given without new evidence. In this talk he also says he is not convinced that much is known about UAP by anybody.
