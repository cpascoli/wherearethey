An interview with Dante Golinelli, a former fighter pilot of the Italian Air Force's 50° Stormo, recorded at his home near Rome on 29 June 2018 by the Piacenza association Spazio Tesla (Alberto Negri) and published on its YouTube channel in October 2018. Golinelli describes the object he chased in an F-84 over the San Damiano airfield in October 1966, and what followed on the ground.

The transcript is a Whisper transcription of the Italian audio, checked against the recording only where quoted; the opening minute is music.
