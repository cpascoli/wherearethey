The raw audio of Betty Hill's regression with Boston psychiatrist Benjamin Simon, which began on January 4, 1964. Under hypnosis, Barney's and Betty's accounts were consistent with each other. Simon nonetheless concluded, in an article for the journal *Psychiatric Opinion*, that the case was “a singular psychological aberration” ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barney_and_Betty_Hill_incident)). Barney had agreed to the sessions partly to put to rest what he called “the ‘nonsense’ about her dreams”.
