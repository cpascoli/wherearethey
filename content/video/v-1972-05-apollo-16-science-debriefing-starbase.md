Another part of the Apollo 16 scientific debriefing, released by NASA in PURSUE release 03. AARO's description singles out one off-hand remark at 32:41 (in the passage from 31:59 in our transcript): discussing why a magnetic anomaly, a laser-altimeter hollow and a gamma-ray peak all fall on the crater Van de Graaff, the investigator says "It could be an alien star base or something" and moves on. In context it is a joke about an unexplained correlation, not a claim.
