Wally Schirra on Sigma 7, the Mercury-Atlas 8 flight: white particles drifting from the capsule, which he compares to Glenn's “lathe shavings”, and a burst of light in the window at sunset whose source he cannot identify; he relates it to the sun passing below the horizon.
