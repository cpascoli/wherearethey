A two-hour live television special hosted by Larry King, produced by CNN and broadcast on TNT in 1994 from the desert outside Area 51 near Rachel, Nevada. It mixes live panel discussion with prerecorded reports on the evidence for UFOs and alleged government secrecy. Its most cited segment is a prerecorded interview with former Senator Barry Goldwater, who says he believes the Air Force's UFO knowledge is kept at Wright-Patterson and recalls Gen. Curtis LeMay angrily refusing him access to a room there. The copy linked here is a third-party upload of the original broadcast. Transcribed with Whisper.
