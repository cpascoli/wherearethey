Pete Conrad, Dick Gordon and Alan Bean of Apollo 12 describe to NASA's medical team the flashes and streaks they saw in the dark, eyes open or closed. They treat it as an effect inside the eye, and NASA later established it as cosmic rays striking the retina; AARO includes it among the NASA records released in PURSUE.

*Source: NASA, released by the Department of War in PURSUE (NASA-UAP-D008), via DVIDS. Whisper transcript.*
