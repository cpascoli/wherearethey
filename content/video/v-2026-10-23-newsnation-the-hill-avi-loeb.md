Harvard astrophysicist Avi Loeb, chair of the UAP Science Advisory Council, interviewed in the studio by Blake Burman on NewsNation's *The Hill* on 23 October 2026, the day PURSUE release 07 came out, and posted by NewsNation the next day. He calls the released data insufficient to identify the triangle and the orbs, proposes putting better sensors where they were reported, and says neither he nor the officials he speaks with have seen evidence that companies hold alien craft.
