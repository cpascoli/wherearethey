Harvard astrophysicist Avi Loeb, chair of the UAP Science Advisory Council, interviewed in the studio by Blake Burman on NewsNation's *The Hill* on 23 October 2026, the day PURSUE release 07 came out. He calls the released data insufficient to identify the triangle and the orbs and proposes putting better sensors where they were reported.

The recording Loeb posted on his Harvard page breaks off after about a minute; the rest of the five-minute interview is known only from his own transcript on Medium. In that transcript he says he has seen no evidence, nor have the officials he speaks with, that companies such as Lockheed Martin hold alien craft. He compares the case with black projects like the B-2, which were once taken for UFOs. He also says he hopes for an answer "within a year or two". Transcribed with Whisper.
