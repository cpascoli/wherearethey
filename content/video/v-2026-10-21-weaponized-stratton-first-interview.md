WEAPONIZED episode in which George Knapp and Jeremy Corbell discuss Jay Stratton's book *Out of the Shadows* and replay the interview they recorded with him in Huntsville, Alabama, in the summer of 2022. It was his first on camera, made while he was still in government. The Stratton footage runs from about 18:00 to 37:00; the rest is the hosts' commentary, including the AAWSAP files DIA says it has found, the "Axelrod" identification and the Lockheed transfer story. The 2022 material is four years old; only its release is new. Transcribed locally with Whisper.
