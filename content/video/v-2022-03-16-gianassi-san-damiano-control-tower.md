Testimony of Paolo Gianassi, later a colonel in the Italian Air Force, who on 17 October 1966 was the air traffic controller on duty in the tower at San Damiano (Piacenza). He was interviewed by Alberto Negri of Spazio Tesla; the recording date is not given, and the video was published in March 2022. Gianassi, who says he was not a pilot, recalls the radio exchanges with a young pilot, Sottotenente Vittorino Bocca, who chased a bright object in an F-84.

The transcript is a Whisper transcription of the Italian audio.
