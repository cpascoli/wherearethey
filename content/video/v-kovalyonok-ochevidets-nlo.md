A Russian television package with an interview of cosmonaut Col. Gen. Vladimir Kovalyonok. It plays an excerpt of what it presents as his 15 August 1978 report from Salyut 6, then has him describe in his own words the object he watched from the station during his 1981 flight with Viktor Savinykh. The original broadcaster and date are not identified. It is posted by the Homocosmicus mirror channel, uploaded in 2015. The voice-over contains errors: it places Savinykh on the 1978 flight and mixes the two events. Transcribed locally with Whisper (Russian).
