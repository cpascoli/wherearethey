James Lacatski's third WEAPONIZED interview, with George Knapp and Jeremy Corbell, recorded after the release of his fourth and final AAWSAP book, *Future Visions*. He describes how the program began, argues that UFOs belong under a "paranormal umbrella", says the four books were intended as disclosure, and says what was released about KONA BLUE was correct but incomplete. Knapp and Lacatski discuss the book's material on Skinwalker Ranch and the program's counterintelligence scares, and Lacatski admits where the program failed. Transcribed with Whisper.
