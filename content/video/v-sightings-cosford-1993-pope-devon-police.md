A segment of the U.S. television series *Sightings*, presented by Tim White, on the wave of sightings over Britain on the night of 30–31 March 1993, the case that Nick Pope, then running the Ministry of Defence's UFO desk, says changed his mind. It carries on-camera accounts by two North Devon police officers who saw the lights from their patrol car near Lynton, Sgt. Barry Mitchell and Officer Gus Cottey, and by Doug Cooper of the Devon UFO Research Organisation, whom a Cornwall police sergeant called that night.

The MoD's file on the case (DEFE 24/2086) records the Lynton officers' sighting, without their names, in Cooper's reports, and the MoD's replies to him; it also records that a Russian rocket re-entry at about 1.10 am explained many of the high-level sightings, which the programme does not mention. On radar, the file records that replays showed only some slow-moving primary contacts over Devon and Cornwall, which RAF West Drayton's controllers did not think significant.
