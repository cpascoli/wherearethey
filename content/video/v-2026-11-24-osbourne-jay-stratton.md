Jay Stratton's interview on Jack Osbourne's podcast *Trying Not To Die*, published on 24 November 2026, a month after his memoir *Out of the Shadows*. It is his fullest account on video of his dealings with James Ryder of Lockheed Martin. He says Hal Puthoff introduced them in 2009, that Ryder told him Lockheed held twelve craft, and that Ryder wanted to move one to Stratton's program at the Defense Intelligence Agency. The transfer needed the consent of the CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology, which, he says, refused.

These are Stratton's claims: no record of the proposed transfer is public, Ryder has made no verified statement on the subject, and AARO reports that the companies named by its interviewees denied on the record ever holding such material. Stratton himself says he saw no craft, debris, pictures or videos. He also covers Skinwalker Ranch, the experiences at his home, the origin of AATIP as a working group, Stephenville, Gimbal, and his doubts about the "Hellfire" video.
