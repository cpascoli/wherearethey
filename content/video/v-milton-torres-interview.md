Milton Torres, a former U.S. Air Force F-86D pilot, tells the story of the night of 20 May 1957, when he was scrambled from RAF Manston in Kent and ordered to fire a full salvo of 24 rockets at a radar target over East Anglia, only for it to leave his radar before he could fire. The interview was recorded after the Ministry of Defence released his 1988 written account in October 2008; it repeats that account, with the order to fire, the lock-on to a blip “as big as an aircraft carrier” and the debriefing by a civilian who swore him to secrecy.

It is his testimony, not a record of the flight: neither the MoD nor the U.S. Air Force found any record of the incident, and his wingman's written account in the same file differs on several points (see the incident).
