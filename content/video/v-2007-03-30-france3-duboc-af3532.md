A segment from France 3's evening news of 30 March 2007 on two French cases championed by Jean-Jacques Velasco, then head of the CNES's UAP unit: the Trans-en-Provence landing trace and the Air France flight 3532 sighting of 28 January 1994. Captain Jean-Charles Duboc revisits the sighting over Coulommiers and describes a tilted disc 400–500 metres across that "dematerialised". The report then examines the radar evidence Velasco cited. The echo was more than 40 km from the object, of a size consistent with a light aircraft, and dismissed by the Taverny air defence centre as an erratic, unconfirmed return. By 2007 Duboc himself accepted that the radar trace was not his object. Uploaded by a third party; French, transcribed locally with Whisper, with moments summarised in English.
