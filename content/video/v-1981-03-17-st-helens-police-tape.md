A short excerpt (about three minutes) of the radio exchange the officers taped that morning, as uploaded to YouTube in 2022. It runs from the glow fading toward dawn, through the sound at about 4:29 a.m. and the officers' description of it, to the time check. The speakers are not identified on the tape. The transcript is a local Whisper transcription of poor audio, and the moments quote only words that are in it.

The case is told in the incident entry; the CUFOS analysis mentioned in the sound moment is as reported in Greg Long's 1996 column "Tales from the Secret Study".
