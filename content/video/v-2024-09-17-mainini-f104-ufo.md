The second episode of RONIN FILM PRODUCTION's interview with retired General Giulio Mainini (published 17 September 2024). He first talks about the F-104 and a high-speed test flight, then from 6:51 about the night in 1978 when he says he chased a light from Cameri, the press coverage that followed, and the office at the Air Force's intelligence department that handled UFO reports under him. In Italian. The transcript is the uploader's Italian subtitles. The video description carries his official biography.

His 1978 account in La Stampa does not include the chase: there he said he kept to his course and the light vanished after three minutes (see the incident file).
