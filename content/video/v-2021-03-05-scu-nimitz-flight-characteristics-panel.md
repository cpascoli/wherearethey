A livestreamed panel of the Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies with the three authors of the 2019 *Entropy* paper on the Nimitz encounter: the physicist Kevin Knuth, Robert Powell and the electrical engineer Peter Reali. They explain where the data behind each estimate came from (Kevin Day's account of the radar, Fravor's sighting and the ATFLIR video), how much the figures depend on the 0.78 seconds Day gave, and how the paper was reviewed. Powell recounts what Kevin Day and Gary Voorhis told him about the ship's records being taken.

The authors say plainly that they never had the radar data; the radar-based estimates rest on witness accounts.
