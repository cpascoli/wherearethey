P.J. Hughes, an aviation electronics technician with the USS Nimitz's E-2 Hawkeye squadron in 2004, interviewed by Simeon Hein at the UFO Megacon in Laughlin, Nevada, the day after a panel with other Nimitz witnesses. He did not see the object; he describes how, within half an hour of his locking away the Hawkeye's data recorders from the flight airborne during Fravor's intercept, his commanding officer came with two men in Air Force flight suits and took them, signed out to the commanding officer and never returned.

It is his testimony. No record of the recorders' removal has been released, and David Fravor rejects the idea that anyone came to the ships.

*Source: Opening Minds Files (Simeon Hein), YouTube, 2019. Whisper transcript.*
