Episode 18 of Simone Tommasini's podcast, a long conversation with retired Air Force colonel Roberto Doz. Most of it is about the UFO question in general (the Nimitz case, Blue Book, disclosure, the stigma pilots face); from about 1:21:00 Doz tells his own sighting of 13 December 1985 at the San Cesareo toll booth and the witnesses he gathered afterwards. His own site links this episode at that point.
