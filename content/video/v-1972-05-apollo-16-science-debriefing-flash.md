A debriefing between the Apollo 16 crew and the mission's principal investigators, held in 1972 to share early results before Apollo 17, released by NASA in PURSUE release 03. At 25:11 the seismic team asks about a flash a crew member saw from lunar orbit, below the horizon on the dark side of the Moon, which the investigator calls the first report of a transient event seen from orbit; he hoped to match its time against the seismometers. AARO's description points to this passage; the rest of the recording is science results.
