A 2019 YouTube upload by a third party that joins three television pieces on the O'Hare sighting of 7 November 2006. The first (to 2:59) is CNN correspondent Gary Tuchman's report from Chicago, in which a United Airlines mechanic, identified only as “Joe”, becomes the first witness to speak on camera; NARCAP's report identifies him as its witness B, the mechanic in the left seat of the jet being taxied to the United hangar, and dates the interview to CNN Headline News on 6 January 2007 (a transcript reposted online gives 5 January). A sketch artist draws the object from his description; United and the FAA tell CNN they are not investigating.

The second piece (3:03–11:50) is a local Chicago interview with Jon Hilkevitch, the Tribune reporter who broke the story on 1 January 2007, recorded in the first days of January with off-air talk before and after. The third (from 11:56) is a later interview, given about four years after the event when Leslie Kean's mention of the case on the Colbert Report revived interest. The upload does not name the station or date of either.

The witness is anonymous and Hilkevitch is a reporter relaying what witnesses and documents told him; the FAA's weather explanation and the absence of any controller sighting or photograph are given in the same pieces.

*Sources: the recording; NARCAP Technical Report 10 (Haines et al., May 2007), note 10 and its press list.*
