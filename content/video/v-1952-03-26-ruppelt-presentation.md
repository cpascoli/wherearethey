The audio recording of a presentation given on 26 March 1952 by Capt. Edward J. Ruppelt, project engineer of the Air Force's reorganised UFO investigation at the Air Technical Intelligence Center, soon to be named Project Blue Book. The Department of War released it, with a transcript, in its sixth PURSUE release in September 2026.

Ruppelt reviews the reports since 1947 and the conclusions of the early studies (Project Sign and its successor), explains why the project was reopened in October 1951, discusses the Lubbock lights photographs, radar cases and the Mantell crash, and describes plans for diffraction-grating cameras and radar-scope photography. It is one of the few recordings of Ruppelt's own voice.

*Source: Department of War, PURSUE release 06 (DVIDS, 2026). Whisper transcript; the official transcript is linked.*
