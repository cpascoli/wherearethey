Interviews recorded in Roswell on 11–12 July 1990, during a reunion of 509th Bomb Group veterans, and held by the National Archives among the records of the Air Force's declassification team (RG 341, local identifier 341-ROSWELL-18, "Roswell Reunion: Number 1"); the companion tape, Number 2, has Jesse Marcel Jr. and Robert Porter.

Sheriff George Wilcox's daughters recall the July 1947 days at the county jail, mostly through what their parents told them, and say themselves that they have since read the books. Robert Shirkey, a base operations officer, says he glimpsed the debris and an I-beam with markings being carried to the B-29 for Fort Worth; his account of bodies comes from others. Walter Haut, the base public information officer who issued the "flying disc" release, gives a detailed account of that day, and says he saw none of the material; twelve years later the sealed affidavit drafted for him says he was shown the craft and the bodies.

*Source: National Archives, RG 341 (via the Internet Archive, public domain). Whisper transcript.*
