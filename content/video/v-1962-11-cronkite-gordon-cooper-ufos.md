An excerpt of Walter Cronkite's November 1962 interview with Gordon Cooper, six months before his Mercury-Atlas 9 flight, released by NASA in PURSUE release 03. Cooper, who later said he had seen UFOs as a pilot, is cautious here: well-qualified witnesses, no logical explanation yet, and the possibility of life elsewhere.

*Source: NASA, released by the Department of War in PURSUE (NASA-UAP-D023), via DVIDS. Whisper transcript.*
