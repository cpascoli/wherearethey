Part of the Apollo 17 post-flight debriefing at the Manned Spacecraft Center, released by NASA in PURSUE release 04. Most of it is a technical review of the flight by the lunar module pilot, Harrison Schmitt: flight plan, cue cards, food and health. At 16:54 he turns to the light flashes, seen almost continuously when dark-adapted but not during the hour of the blindfolded experiment designed to study them. AARO describes the flashes as cosmic rays passing through the eye.

*Source: NASA, released by the Department of War in PURSUE (NASA-UAP-D029), via DVIDS. Whisper transcript.*
