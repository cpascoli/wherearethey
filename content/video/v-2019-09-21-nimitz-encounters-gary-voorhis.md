Gary Voorhis, the USS Princeton's Aegis and Cooperative Engagement Capability technician in November 2004, interviewed for the documentary *The Nimitz Encounters* in 2019. He describes the radar recalibration that sharpened the unknown tracks, days of watching distant specks through binoculars, and what he says was a longer, clearer intercept video on the classified network that vanished before he could copy it. He also describes men arriving by helicopter and data recordings being signed over under chain of custody, with the remaining tapes wiped.

He is candid about the limits of memory after 14 years, and lists a U.S. test program among the possible explanations. His claim here to have watched the intercept video live differs from his later statement to Popular Mechanics that the Princeton was too far away to see Fravor's intercept. David Fravor has rejected the ten-minute-video and seized-data accounts. Transcribed locally with Whisper.
