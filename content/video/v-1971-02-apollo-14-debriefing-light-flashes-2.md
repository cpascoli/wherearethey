The continuation of the Apollo 14 debriefing, released by NASA in PURSUE release 04: about half an hour in which NASA scientists go through the light flashes Alan Shepard, Stuart Roosa and Edgar Mitchell counted during a dedicated observation period, and test their descriptions against lights shown in a darkened room. The crew describe streaks, pinpoints, "novas" and diffuse glows, all inside the eye; the scientists take the fact that they appear without dark adaptation as evidence that they are not real light. They are now understood as cosmic rays striking the retina.
