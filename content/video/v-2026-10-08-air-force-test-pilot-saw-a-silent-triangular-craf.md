Part one of Ryan Graves's two-part interview with retired U.S. Air Force F-111 and test pilot Dan Isbell on the Safe Aerospace Podcast (Americans for Safe Aerospace). The first hour covers his career, from F-111 nuclear alert at RAF Upper Heyford and the Air Force Test Pilot School to F-22 program work and directed-energy weapons. The last half-hour covers his encounter of 26 December 1980 with a silent, colour-shifting triangular craft over a golf course near the base, and why he kept it secret for 43 years.
