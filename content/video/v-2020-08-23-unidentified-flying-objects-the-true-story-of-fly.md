The 1956 documentary feature *Unidentified Flying Objects*, built around the story of Albert M. Chop, a civilian who joined the Air Force public-information staff. It includes Delbert Newhouse recounting his 1952 Tremonton sighting on camera, and the Tremonton and Great Falls films. Much of the rest is dramatised. This upload is a third-party copy.
