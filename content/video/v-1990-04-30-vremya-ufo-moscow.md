A news report from *Vremya*, Soviet Central Television's main evening news, broadcast on 30 April 1990 and published by the state broadcast archive Gosteleradiofond on its "Soviet Television" channel. Correspondent Aleksandra Livanskaya visits the Yaroslavl Highway north-east of Moscow, where sightings had been recurring since March. She interviews traffic police officers and Major Vladimir Stroynetsky at his unit. Transcribed locally with Whisper (Russian).
