On Chrissy Newton's *Rebelliously Curious* podcast, Rep. Eric Burlison says his office is still trying to verify David Grusch's claims after Grusch left his staff, including the footage of so-called Nordic beings that Grusch has spoken of. Burlison says he has not seen it and makes no claim it exists until he has evidence; no such footage has been made public, and no letter to Lockheed Martin or the agencies has been published.
