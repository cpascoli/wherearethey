A Wall Street Journal investigation, drawing on interviews with officials involved in AARO's historical review under Sean Kirkpatrick, reported that the Pentagon itself had sometimes fed UFO mythology: doctored saucer photos handed out near Area 51 to shield stealth-fighter tests, and a fake "Yankee Blue" alien-technology briefing given to new commanders of classified Air Force programs for decades. The findings were left out of AARO's 2024 report; the Pentagon said a second volume would cover them. FOIA searches later found no record of the 2023 memo the Journal described.
