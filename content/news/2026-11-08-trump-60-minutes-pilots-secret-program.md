In his post-midterm *60 Minutes* interview, aired 8 November 2026, President Trump retold the Air Force pilots' account he gave Newsmax in October (now "three times", not four, an F-22's speed), again without naming the pilots or giving a date or place. Asked about the whistleblowers' claim of a secret program hidden even from presidents, the core of the legacy-program allegation, he said he had not heard of one.
