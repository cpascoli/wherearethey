Avi Loeb, who chairs the White House UAP Science Advisory Council, called the pilots' reports Trump described on 60 Minutes reliable but puzzling, and said a round object outrunning an F-22 should have produced a shock wave that was not observed. He gave no details of the pilots, dates or sensor data; Trump himself did not identify the pilots or say when or where the encounter happened.
