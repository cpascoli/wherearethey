Seven pages of AFOSI investigative records, released by the FBI to The Black Vault and published on 26 November 2026, are the first official account on file of the December 2024 drone incursions that closed Wright-Patterson's airspace. They describe quadcopter-type small drones (sUAS), some in synchronized formations, seen by security forces and by AFOSI agents themselves, and a reported loss of radio contact near them; they also record one sighting found to be mistaken, and in every case say the operators were not identified. The records give no explanation, and describe the craft throughout as small drones.
