Liberation Times put questions to the FBI about the 2026 triangle sighting by federal agents at a sensitive site in the western United States, released in PURSUE release 07 as an AARO memorandum and an FBI interview report. The Bureau would not say why the agents were posted there with thermal equipment, whether the object was identified, whether the case is still open or whether any imagery exists, and referred only to the released documents. The answer adds no new facts about the object; it leaves open the questions the two records do not settle.
