Rep. Eric Burlison's office announced that Jake Barber, the Air Force veteran who came forward in January 2025 with an account of helicopter missions to retrieve UAP, has joined his staff as a senior adviser on UAP. The release describes Barber's accounts as firsthand but does not corroborate them; his retrieval claims remain unverified on file.
