Sean Kirkpatrick, AARO's first director, disputes the weight given to the federal agents' triangle sighting released in PURSUE release 07, and alleges that Pentagon leadership had AARO's resolutions removed from cases before they were published as unresolved. The Pentagon has not confirmed that allegation; the War Department says only that it is leaving the files unassessed, and confirmed that the triangle images are illustrations.
