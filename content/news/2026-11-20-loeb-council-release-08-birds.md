On the day of PURSUE release 08, Avi Loeb, who chairs the UAP Science Advisory Council, published the council's first look at the release's three videos, applying the wingbeat-flicker test it used on the earlier tranches: most of the objects in the two Middle East videos flicker at bird wingbeat rates. It is a preliminary analysis by the council, not a reclassification by AARO or the Department of War, which list both videos as unresolved; Loeb repeated it on NewsNation the next day.
