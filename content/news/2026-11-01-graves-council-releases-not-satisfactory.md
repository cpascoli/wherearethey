A CBS Sunday Morning cover story on the UAP Science Advisory Council, which CBS says was established by the Trump administration, reports that former Navy pilot Ryan Graves has joined it, and has Graves criticising the PURSUE releases as short infrared clips rather than the high-end sensor data pilots rely on. Chair Avi Loeb says the council is working first from what has been released and needs high-quality data; member Matthew Szydagis says there is still no definitive evidence.
