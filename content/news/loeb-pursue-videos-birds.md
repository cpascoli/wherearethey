Harvard astrophysicist Avi Loeb's UAP Science Advisory Council extended its analysis of PURSUE video PR135, where six fast infrared dots turned out to flicker at bird wingbeat rates, to every sensor video released so far. They found likely birds in about 8% of them. The method measures how fast an object's brightness varies, which works without knowing its distance. It is the council's analysis in a preprint, not an official reclassification by AARO or the Department of War.
