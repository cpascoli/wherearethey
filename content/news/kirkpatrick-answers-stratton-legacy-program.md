The founding director of AARO, Sean Kirkpatrick, answered the central allegation of Jay Stratton's new memoir *Out of the Shadows*: that a "Legacy Program" retrieving and reverse-engineering non-human craft exists, and that Kirkpatrick was controlled by it. Kirkpatrick called the claim absurd and accused Stratton, without naming a statute, of national-security violations. Former Senate staffer Kirk McConnell said Stratton had not shared such information with the committees he staffed, and a Senate source who dealt with both men said he had seen evidence for neither side's accusations.
