The Soviet Union's state research programme on UFOs and anomalous phenomena. It was split between the Ministry of Defence (Setka MO) and the Academy of Sciences (Setka AN) and ran from 1978 to 1990. Military units were ordered to report sightings, and most reports were traced to rocket and satellite launches and to high-altitude research balloons.

A few cases were investigated urgently because they seemed to affect military units, among them the October 1982 events at a Strategic Rocket Forces division in Ukraine. The programme's two coordinators, Col. Boris Sokolov and Yuli Platov, published its history in the journal of the Russian Academy of Sciences in 2000.

*Sources: Platov & Sokolov, Vestnik RAN 70(6), 2000*
