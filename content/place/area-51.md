Area 51 is a highly classified U.S. Air Force facility at Groom Lake in the Nevada desert, used since the 1950s to test secret aircraft such as the U-2 and later stealth designs. The CIA publicly acknowledged its existence in June 2013, in response to a Freedom of Information Act request.

Its secrecy has made it the centre of UFO lore. The most famous claim is Bob Lazar's 1989 account, in interviews with George Knapp, of working on recovered alien craft at a nearby site called S-4; his account is unverified, and he is outside this archive's sourcing criteria as a witness.
