Skinwalker Ranch is a 512-acre property in Utah's Uinta Basin with a long local reputation for strange lights, cattle mutilations and other reported phenomena. Robert Bigelow bought it in 1996 and his National Institute for Discovery Science studied it for years; Brandon Fugal acquired it in 2016.

A senior DIA scientist, James Lacatski, visited in the mid-2000s and reported a strange encounter of his own, which he says led to the creation of the Pentagon-funded AAWSAP in 2008; Bigelow's team documented glowing orbs, creatures and UFO sightings there. AARO's 2024 historical review discusses these investigations. Since 2020 the ranch has been the subject of the History Channel series *The Secret of Skinwalker Ranch*.

*Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skinwalker_Ranch) · AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024)*
