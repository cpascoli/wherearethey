In 2019 a 15-page document began circulating online that presents itself as notes typed by the physicist Eric Davis of a 16 October 2002 conversation with retired Vice Adm. Thomas R. Wilson, a former director of the Defense Intelligence Agency. According to the notes, Wilson tried to gain access to a covert programme reverse-engineering recovered craft but was refused by its gatekeepers.

The document was reportedly found among the papers of the late Apollo 14 astronaut Edgar Mitchell. Neither Davis nor Wilson has confirmed its authenticity. In May 2022 Rep. Mike Gallagher had the notes entered into the record of a House Intelligence subcommittee hearing, which made them a public congressional document but did not establish that their contents are true.

*Sources: Wilson–Davis notes (House record, 2022)*
