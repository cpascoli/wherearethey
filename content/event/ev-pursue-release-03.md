Release 03 of PURSUE, the U.S. government's public catalogue of declassified UAP records hosted by the Department of War, was published on 12 June 2026. It added 72 records: 29 from the FBI, 18 from the CIA, 12 from the Department of War, 11 from NASA, 1 from an intelligence-community agency and 1 from other U.S. government sources.

The release contains 53 documents, 10 images, 6 videos and 3 audio recordings, describing events from 1949 to 2026. Every record is listed in this archive's Index under Official records, with the agency's own description and a link to the official file.
