An all-day public forum on UAP transparency held on 25 June 2026 in the Senate's Kennedy Caucus Room and organized by the Disclosure Foundation (formerly the UAP Disclosure Fund). Speakers included members of Congress, former officials, scientists and whistleblowers.

The national security panel, recorded and published by the Foundation in August 2026, discussed the Langley drone incursions, UAP around ships and test ranges, special access program oversight, whistleblower protections and the PURSUE releases.
