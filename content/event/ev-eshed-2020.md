In December 2020, Haim Eshed, the retired general who led Israel's space-security programme for nearly three decades, told the newspaper *Yediot Aharonot* that the U.S. government had for years been in contact with extraterrestrials of a "Galactic Federation", under agreements allowing experiments on Earth, and that there was a joint underground base on Mars with American astronauts. He said President Trump knew and had been "on the verge" of revealing it.

The claims, made while promoting his book *The Universe Beyond the Horizon*, drew international headlines and scepticism. Israel Space Agency chairman Isaac Ben-Israel said Eshed had gone "too far", NASA said it had yet to find signs of extraterrestrial life, and some commentators suggested parts of the interview were distorted in translation.
