On 22 March 2018, the Stanford immunologist Garry Nolan and colleagues published a whole-genome analysis of "Ata", a six-inch mummified skeleton found in the Atacama Desert of Chile. They found that the remains were human, a foetus with novel mutations in genes linked to bone growth and dysplasia.

The study answered claims made in Steven Greer's 2013 film *Sirius*, which had presented Ata as a possible extraterrestrial being. Nolan later summed up the result as "human a hundred percent", just "a bad roll of the dice" of mutations. The episode is often cited as an example of testing an extraordinary claim with ordinary science.
