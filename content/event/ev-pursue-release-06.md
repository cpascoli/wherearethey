Release 06 of PURSUE, the U.S. government's public catalogue of declassified UAP records hosted by the Department of War, was published on 18 September 2026. It added 75 records: 67 from the Department of War and 8 from local law enforcement.

The release contains 59 documents, 15 videos and 1 audio recordings, describing events from 1952 to 2025. Every record is listed in this archive's Index under Official records, with the agency's own description and a link to the official file.
