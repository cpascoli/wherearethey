In June 2021, the *South China Morning Post* reported on work by a People's Liberation Army researcher, Chen Li of the Air Force Early Warning Academy, describing artificial-intelligence methods for analysing a rapidly growing number of reports of "unidentified air conditions", framed as a challenge to China's air-defence security. As relayed by *The Independent*, the source was a 2019 report to scientists in Beijing, not a 2021 paper.

The original Chinese report has not been located: its title, venue and full text are not on file, and what it says is known only through the press. It shows that at least one PLA research institution studies the problem; it is not evidence of a national Chinese UFO programme. The story came out weeks before the U.S. ODNI published its preliminary assessment on UAP.
