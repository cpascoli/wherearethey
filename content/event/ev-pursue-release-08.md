Release 08 of PURSUE, the U.S. government's public catalogue of declassified UAP records hosted by the Department of War, was published on 20 November 2026. It added 43 records.

Eight concern AAWSAP, the DIA program run through Bigelow's BAASS from 2008: Senator Reid's 2009 letter asking for a restricted special access program for “the AATIP”, DIA's finding that nothing justified one, the DIA Director's 2010 memo on the program's $22 million and its verdict that the reports were of limited value, an earlier positive review by the office that ran it, and BAASS's 539-page ten-month report, with its own investigation of the 2004 Tic Tac. Six CIA memos trace the agency's 1952 study of flying saucers from the July Washington sightings to the proposal that became the Robertson Panel, including the DCI's suggestion to discuss their psychological-warfare uses. Air Force files from 1949 to 1966 include Kelly Johnson's 1953 sighting with his Lockheed test crew and the November 1957 reports from Levelland, White Sands and Kirtland; three CENTCOM sensor videos from 2020, 2023 and 2026, and an AARO slide on an unresolved radar track over the western United States in 2023, complete it.

Every record is listed in this archive's Index under Official records, with the agency's own description and a link to the official file.
