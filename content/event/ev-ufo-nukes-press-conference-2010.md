On 27 September 2010, researcher Robert Hastings, author of *UFOs and Nukes*, brought seven former U.S. Air Force officers and airmen to the National Press Club in Washington to describe UFO incidents at nuclear-missile and weapons-storage sites from the 1960s to 1980: Malmstrom, Walker and F.E. Warren Air Force Bases and RAF Bentwaters. Each spoke about his own experience, though several were relaying what guards or other personnel had reported.

Their accounts are testimony. The Air Force's position, read out by Salas, is that no UFO it evaluated was ever a threat to national security. For Malmstrom, the wing's own records say rumours of UFOs around Echo Flight were disproven.
