Release 07 of PURSUE, the U.S. government's public catalogue of declassified UAP records hosted by the Department of War, was published on 23 October 2026. It added 28 documents.

Its headline is a 2026 incident at a sensitive national security site in the western United States: an AARO memorandum and an FBI interview report in which federal special agents, with a third official at a separate post, describe a silent, matte black triangular craft that climbed over a ridge, hovered and dived into a canyon. AARO links it to other incidents in the region. The release also adds AAWSAP's working files, from the DIA program described in the *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon* series, to the contract records released in September: BAASS's monthly reports, its database plan and its report on its Skinwalker Ranch fieldwork; Cold War correspondence on recovering space material; the personnel record of Howard McCoy, the Wright Field intelligence chief behind Projects Sign and Grudge; and Air Force intelligence reports from the 1950s, including four from Catalina Island.

Every record is listed in this archive's Index under Official records, with the agency's own description and a link to the official file.
