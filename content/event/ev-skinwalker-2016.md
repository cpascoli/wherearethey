In 2016 Robert Bigelow sold Skinwalker Ranch to Adamantium Real Estate LLC for around $500,000. The Utah real-estate developer Brandon Fugal announced in March 2020 that he was the owner behind the purchase.

Under Fugal the ranch became the subject of the History Channel series *The Secret of Skinwalker Ranch* (from 2020), in which a team of scientists and investigators uses sensors and experiments to test the property's reputation.
