Jesse Michels hosts *American Alchemy*, a YouTube channel of long-form interviews with witnesses, former officials and researchers in the UAP field. Thirteen of its interviews are filed here, including former officials such as Eric Davis and Gregory Rogers and military witnesses such as Mario Woods.

Guests vary widely in credentials, so each one is checked against this archive's sourcing rule; the interviews are filed as the guests' own accounts.
