*The Flying Saucers Are Real* (Fawcett, 1950) expanded Donald Keyhoe's article in the January 1950 issue of *True*, one of the most widely read magazine pieces of its time. Written as a first-person investigation, it follows Keyhoe, a retired Marine aviator, as he interviews pilots, officials and engineers about the sightings since 1947, the death of Capt. Thomas Mantell chasing an object over Kentucky in 1948, and the Air Force's shifting statements, quoting from Project Sign's report.

Its conclusion is that the saucers are interplanetary craft observing the earth and that the Air Force knows more than it admits but is preparing the public for the truth. It was the founding text of the cover-up thesis in American ufology. Ruppelt, who ran Blue Book soon after, wrote that Keyhoe generally had his facts right but not his interpretation of them, and the Mantell case is now usually attributed to a classified Navy Skyhook balloon. The book's copyright was not renewed and it is in the public domain in the United States; the full text is in this archive's search.
