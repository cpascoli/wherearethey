*Crash: When UFOs Fall from the Sky* (New Page Books, 2010) surveys reported UFO crashes worldwide, from Roswell to cases in the United States, Latin America and elsewhere, and assesses which have some evidence and which are hoaxes or misidentifications. Randle is candid that many crash stories collapse under scrutiny.
