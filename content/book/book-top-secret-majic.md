*Top Secret/Majic* (Marlowe & Company, 1996) is Stanton Friedman's defence of the MJ-12 papers: the "Eisenhower briefing document" and related memos that surfaced in the 1980s describing a secret committee of scientists and officials set up after Roswell. Friedman, who spent years in presidential and other archives checking their details, argues there are no substantive grounds for dismissing them, and recounts winning the sceptic Philip Klass's $1,000 challenge over their typeface.

The FBI's own file records the Air Force calling the briefing document fabricated, and most researchers, including many who accept Roswell, regard the MJ-12 papers as forgeries. Friedman himself showed that some later MJ-12 documents were hoaxes.
