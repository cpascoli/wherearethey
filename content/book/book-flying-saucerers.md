*Flying Saucerers* (Alternative Albion, 2007) is a social history of British ufology by the historian David Clarke and the researcher Andy Roberts. Rather than the sightings themselves, it studies the people around them: the clubs, magazines, contactees and investigators who made up the British UFO scene from the 1950s onward, and how their beliefs and methods shaped what the public came to think UFOs were.
