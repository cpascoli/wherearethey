*Project Blue Book Exposed* (Marlowe & Company, 1997) is Kevin Randle's history of the Air Force's UFO investigation from 1952 to 1969, using its files to argue that Blue Book was often more interested in explaining sightings away than in investigating them, and that its most important cases were handled elsewhere. It draws on the declassified Blue Book microfilm released to the National Archives.
