*The Clinton UFO Storybook* (2016) is Grant Cameron's account of the Clinton administration and UFOs, built from documents he obtained through FOIA requests to the Clinton Library and other archives: Laurance Rockefeller's lobbying of the White House, the requests to Webster Hubbell, John Podesta's interest in declassification, and the reaction to the 1997 Phoenix lights. The documents are real; their significance is the author's reading.

*Sources: [Goodreads author blog](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/13756060-the-clintons-and-ufos) · Citizen Hearing (2013)*
