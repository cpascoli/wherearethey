*Glimpses of Other Realities* (1993; second volume, *High Strangeness*, 1998) collects Linda Moulton Howe's interviews and reporting from the years after *An Alien Harvest*: eyewitness accounts of animal mutilations, abductions, crop formations and encounters, and the claims of informants who said they had worked in secret programmes. It records what her sources told her; the informants are mostly unnamed or unverified, and the book's conclusions are Howe's.
