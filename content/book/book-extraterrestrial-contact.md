*Extraterrestrial Contact* (Crossing Point, 1999) is Steven Greer's first book, a collection of his essays and talks from the 1990s on the work of his Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence (CSETI): his "CE-5" field expeditions to signal and invite contact with extraterrestrials, his campaign to brief officials, and his argument that governments are hiding contact and advanced energy technology. It is the foundation of the movement he went on to lead with the Disclosure Project.

The book is Greer's own account and advocacy. His claims of contact, of briefing senior officials and of suppressed technology have no independent confirmation, and critics, including former associates, have challenged his CE-5 claims and his films. Greer is filed outside this archive's criteria.
