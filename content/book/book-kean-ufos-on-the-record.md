The standard reference book of official testimony before 2017. Its contributors are pilots and officials speaking on the record, the same sourcing standard this archive uses. It grew out of the 2007 National Press Club panel that Kean organised.

Most chapters are written by the witnesses themselves rather than retold: among them Col. Charles Halt on Rendlesham, Maj. Gen. Wilfried De Brouwer on the Belgian wave, Gen. Parviz Jafari on the 1976 Tehran intercept, Júlio Guerra of the Portuguese Air Force, and former Arizona governor Fife Symington on the 1997 Phoenix lights and why he mocked the sighting in public. Kean has posted two chapter excerpts with the publisher's permission.

*Sources: Wikipedia (Leslie Kean); The New York Times (2017). · [lesliekean.com](https://www.lesliekean.com/ufos/contributors/)*
