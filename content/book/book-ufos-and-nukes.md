Robert Hastings's compilation of interviews with former U.S. military personnel about UFO incursions at nuclear weapons facilities, including Malmstrom in 1967; Hastings says he has interviewed more than 160 veterans. First published in 2008, with a second edition in 2017, it underpins his 2010 National Press Club press conference, where seven of the veterans spoke.

Its contents are the veterans' testimony as recorded by Hastings. The Air Force records for Malmstrom say rumours of UFOs at Echo Flight were disproven, and sceptics argue the book revisits old reports without new evidence.
