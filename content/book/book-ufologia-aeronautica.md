*Ufología aeronáutica* ("Aeronautical ufology", first published 2010; new edition 2021 with Juan Castillo Cornejo) is a Spanish-language book by Rodrigo Bravo, a Chilean Army aviation captain and pilot who has worked with CEFAA, Chile's official committee for the study of anomalous aerial phenomena. It deals with UFO reports from aviation, the field in which CEFAA collects pilot and air-traffic reports. The ISBN given is that of the 2021 edition.
