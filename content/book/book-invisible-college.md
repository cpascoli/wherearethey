*The Invisible College* (Dutton, 1975) takes its title from the informal network of scientists who studied UFOs privately while keeping quiet in public. In it Jacques Vallée develops the idea he had opened in *Passport to Magonia*: that the phenomenon acts on human belief over long periods, a kind of "control system" that shapes culture, and that close encounters, contactee messages and apparitions belong together. It is a book of hypotheses rather than evidence, and an influential one: the control-system idea recurs in later writing on the subject, including Diana Pasulka's.
