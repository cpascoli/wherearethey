*A.D. After Disclosure* (2010; New Page Books, 2012), by Richard Dolan and the television writer Bryce Zabel, is a speculative book: it imagines how a government acknowledgement of extraterrestrial contact might come about and its effects on politics, religion, science and the economy. It is a thought experiment, not a report of events.
