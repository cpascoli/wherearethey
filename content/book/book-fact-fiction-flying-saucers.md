*Fact, Fiction, and Flying Saucers* (New Page Books, 2016), by Stanton Friedman and Kathleen Marden, answers the sceptics: it takes on the arguments of debunkers such as Donald Menzel and Philip Klass, government statements, and also hoaxers within the UFO field, and restates the authors' view that the evidence for extraterrestrial visitation has been distorted and ridiculed rather than refuted.
