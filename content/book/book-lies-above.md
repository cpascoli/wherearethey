An argument, by the cybersecurity researcher David Burkett, that the Pentagon has misled the public and Congress about UAP for seventy years, by ridicule, by investigations built to debunk and by testimony that, he argues, does not survive comparison with the record. It is the author's reading of the published record and others' testimony, not a first-hand account. The sources it cites are linked by QR code and listed on the author's site.
