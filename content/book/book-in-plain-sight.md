Coulthart later interviewed David Grusch for NewsNation (June 2023). He openly advocates for disclosure, so his book is reporting and advocacy, not testimony.

An edited extract published by news.com.au before release opens with Annie Farinaccio's account of a 1991 night drive from the U.S. Naval Communication Station at North West Cape, Western Australia, with two Australian Federal Protective Service officers: a long, diamond-shaped craft followed them at no more than 100 feet, and she says American military police later questioned her on the base and told her it was a weather balloon. The account is hers, relayed by Coulthart.
