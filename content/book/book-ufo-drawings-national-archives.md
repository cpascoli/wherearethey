*UFO Drawings from the National Archives* (Four Corners Books, 2017) reproduces sketches that members of the public, pilots and police sent to the British Ministry of Defence with their UFO reports, taken from the MoD files now held at the National Archives in Kew. The historian David Clarke, who helped bring those files into the open, introduces them and gives the context of each report. The drawings are witnesses' impressions, kept as part of the official record; the book does not claim they show anything beyond what the observers believed they saw.
