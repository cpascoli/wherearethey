*Anatomy of a Phenomenon* (Regnery, 1965; Ace paperback) was Jacques Vallée's first UFO book, written while he was a young astronomer working with J. Allen Hynek at Northwestern University. It makes the case for treating UFO reports as data for scientific study, surveying sightings across history and countries and analysing their patterns, at a time when the Air Force's Project Blue Book was dismissing them. It was followed in 1966 by *Challenge to Science*, written with his wife Janine Vallée, and in 1969 by *Passport to Magonia*, in which his thinking took a very different turn.
