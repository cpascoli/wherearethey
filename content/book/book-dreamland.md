*Dreamland* (Interstellar, 2019) is Bob Lazar's autobiography, published thirty years after he first told his story on KLAS-TV in Las Vegas, and the same year as Jeremy Corbell's documentary *Bob Lazar: Area 51 & Flying Saucers*. George Knapp, the reporter who broke the story in 1989, wrote the foreword. Lazar retells how he came to work at S-4, a site near Groom Lake, on nine flying discs he says were of alien origin; his descriptions of the "sport model", its antimatter reactor and "gravity amplifiers" powered by element 115; and the harassment and legal trouble that followed.

The book is his account and nothing more: no official record confirms any part of it. The schools where he says he studied have no record of him, and Los Alamos and EG&G denied employing him, although a 1982 Los Alamos phone book and a local newspaper profile placed him at the laboratory. Figures in this archive are divided: Knapp still reports his claims, while Garry Nolan does not believe them. Lazar is filed as disputed.
