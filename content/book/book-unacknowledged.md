*Unacknowledged* (Crossing Point, 2017) accompanied Steven Greer's crowd-funded documentary of the same name. It argues that a network of "unacknowledged special access projects", run largely by contractors outside government control, holds recovered extraterrestrial technology and suppresses free-energy inventions, and it draws on the witnesses of his 2001 Disclosure Project.

Its claims are Greer's. His earlier film *Sirius* (2013) had been criticised for presenting the Atacama skeleton as a possible extraterrestrial; a 2018 genome study showed it was a human foetus with genetic mutations. The term "unacknowledged special access program" is real; Greer's account of what such programmes contain has no documentary support.
