*Charlie Red Star* (Dundurn, 2017) tells the story of the 1975 wave of sightings around Carman, Manitoba, when residents, police and journalists watched a red light, nicknamed "Charlie Red Star", that seemed to return night after night. Grant Cameron, who saw it as a young man and began his UFO research because of it, reconstructs the wave from local reports and interviews.
