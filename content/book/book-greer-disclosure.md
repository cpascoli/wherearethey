*Disclosure* (Crossing Point, 2001) is the printed companion to the Disclosure Project's press conference at the National Press Club on 9 May 2001. Edited by Steven Greer, it gathers edited transcripts of the interviews he and his team recorded with dozens of witnesses who held military, government or aerospace roles: among them the FAA's John Callahan on the 1986 JAL 1628 case, Robert Salas on Malmstrom, Clifford Stone on crash retrievals and Larry Warren on Rendlesham. Several of those witnesses have files in this archive.

Its value is that it put many first-hand accounts on record under the witnesses' own names; its weakness is that the testimony was gathered and edited by an advocate, with little documentary support, and that it mixes credible witnesses with claims that are uncorroborated or disputed. Greer's own conclusions about suppressed energy technology and extraterrestrial contact are his alone.
