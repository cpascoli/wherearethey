*Case MJ-12* (HarperTorch, 2002) is Kevin Randle's investigation of the Majestic-12 papers. Unlike Stanton Friedman, Randle concludes that the documents, from the 1984 "Eisenhower briefing document" to later leaks, are not authentic, and traces the people and disinformation around them. It is a significant critique from within the pro-Roswell camp.
