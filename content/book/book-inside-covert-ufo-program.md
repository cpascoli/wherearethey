Book 2 in the authors' AAWSAP series. The archive doesn't hold the book itself, which is copyrighted. It can be bought or borrowed through the links above. The archive does hold the Pentagon's clearance file for the manuscript (DOPSR case 22-SB-0151) and the AAWSAP contract records and DIRDs from PURSUE.

The DOPSR letter names a working title, not the published one. Its identification with this book rests on the "Book 2" label, the same three authors and the 2022 copyright line on the manuscript. Clearance means DOPSR found nothing classified to remove; it does not confirm that the content is accurate.

According to Kelleher, where *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon* reported the program's effects on humans, this book covers UFO performance: lift, propulsion, power generation and what the authors call spatial-temporal translation, drawn from historical cases. Its most-cited passage is chapter 9, in which Lacatski states that the U.S. holds a craft of unknown origin and accessed its interior, which he has confirmed on WEAPONIZED and to 8 News Now without going further. AARO's 2024 report disputes the wider picture: it says the program was cancelled for lack of merit, KONA BLUE was never approved, and no craft or bodies were ever collected.

*Sources: DOPSR case 22-SB-0151 (released under FOIA 24-F-0088); Google Books; Liberation Times (October 17, 2023); WEAPONIZED #38 (2023) and WEAPONIZED with Lacatski (2025); KLAS 8 News Now (November 2025); AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (2024).*
