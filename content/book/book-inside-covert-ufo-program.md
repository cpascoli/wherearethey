Book 2 in the authors' AAWSAP series. The archive doesn't hold the book itself, which is copyrighted. It can be bought or borrowed through the links above. The archive does hold the Pentagon's clearance file for the manuscript (DOPSR case 22-SB-0151) and the AAWSAP contract records and DIRDs from PURSUE.

The DOPSR letter names a working title, not the published one. Its identification with this book rests on the "Book 2" label, the same three authors and the 2022 copyright line on the manuscript. Clearance means DOPSR found nothing classified to remove; it does not confirm that the content is accurate.

*Sources: DOPSR case 22-SB-0151 (released under FOIA 24-F-0088); Google Books; Liberation Times (October 17, 2023); KLAS 8 News Now (November 2025).*
