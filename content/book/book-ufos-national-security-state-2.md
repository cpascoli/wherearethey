The second volume (Keyhole Publishing, 2009) continues Richard Dolan's history through the last two decades of the Cold War, from the sightings, investigations and FOIA releases of the 1970s to the end of the Soviet Union, arguing that official secrecy about UFOs deepened in those years. Like the first volume, it is a documentary history with an interpretive thesis.
