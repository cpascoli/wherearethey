*Roswell UFO Crash Update* (Global Communications, 1995) is Kevin Randle's response to the Air Force's 1994 report, which identified the Roswell debris as the remains of a Project Mogul balloon array. Randle argues that the Mogul explanation does not fit the witness accounts or the records.
