*Flying Saucers and Science* (New Page Books, 2008) is Stanton Friedman's case, as a nuclear physicist, that interstellar travel is feasible and that some UFOs are extraterrestrial craft. He discusses propulsion and the physics of star travel, cites Project Blue Book Special Report No. 14 and other studies whose unexplained residue he says was ignored, and covers crash cases and government secrecy. It is advocacy by a scientist, not peer-reviewed research.
