*Dimensions* (Contemporary Books, 1988) opens Jacques Vallée's trilogy on alien contact. It returns to the themes of *Passport to Magonia* with new cases, setting modern encounters beside folklore and religious visions, and argues that the phenomenon may come from dimensions beyond ordinary space-time rather than from other planets. It also recounts his conversations with Steven Spielberg, whose character Lacombe in *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* was modelled on him. It was followed by *Confrontations* (1990) and *Revelations* (1991).
