*Invasion Washington* (HarperTorch, 2001) is Kevin Randle's book on the July 1952 Washington, D.C., sightings, when radar at National Airport and Andrews Air Force Base tracked unknown targets on two weekends and fighters were scrambled. The Air Force attributed the returns to temperature inversions; Randle examines the records and witness accounts against that explanation.
