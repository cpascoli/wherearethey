*UFOs, Area 51, and Government Informants* (2013), by Grant Cameron and T. Scott Crain, examines insiders and informants who fed UFO researchers stories about crash retrievals and secret programmes, and asks how far such sources can be trusted and whether some were part of managed leaks.
