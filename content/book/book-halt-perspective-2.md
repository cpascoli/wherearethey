*Halt Perspective 2* (Haunted Skies Publishing, 2021) follows Col. Charles Halt's 2016 book on Rendlesham. Written with John Hanson, compiler of the *Haunted Skies* series of British sighting records, it has Halt commenting on many other unexplained UFO reports Hanson gathered over 25 years. It is a commentary on witness reports rather than a new first-hand account.
