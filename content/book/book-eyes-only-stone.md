*Eyes Only* (2011) is Clifford Stone's self-published account of his twenty-two years in the U.S. Army and of the claim he made public at the Disclosure Project's 2001 press conference: that he served with a unit sent to crash sites to recover craft, debris and bodies, some of them alive, while the public was told UFOs did not exist.

The claims are his alone. No official record, document or other named witness corroborates them, and AARO's 2024 historical review found no evidence of crash-retrieval programmes. The book is first-hand testimony as Stone tells it.
