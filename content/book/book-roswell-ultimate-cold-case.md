*Roswell: The Ultimate Cold Case* (New Page Books, 2020) is Thomas Carey and Donald Schmitt's latest summary of their Roswell investigation, presented as a cold-case review: the witnesses, the Air Force's successive explanations and the evidence they say points to the recovery of a non-human craft and crew. As with their earlier books, it rests largely on testimony gathered decades after the event.
