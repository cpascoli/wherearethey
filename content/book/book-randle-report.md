*The Randle Report* (M. Evans, 1997) reviews the state of UFO research in the 1990s: whether investigation is worthwhile, what older cases such as Roswell still have to teach, and whether the 1967 Shag Harbour event in Nova Scotia was a genuine crash. It is a researcher's assessment, including of the field's own weaknesses.
