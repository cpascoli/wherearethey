Science and Counterintelligence Have Complementary Perspectives on UAP
Avi Loeb, Medium, 21 November 2026 (text from the author's Medium RSS feed)

(Image Credit: Avi Loeb/Gemini)

Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) are of interest to both science and counterintelligence. However, the study of UAP stems from distinct perspectives in these fields. As a result, it is easy to separate the two. Let me explain.

Counterintelligence treats UAP as a national security and secrecy issue, while science treats them as an empirical data problem. Although both disciplines analyze UAP, they operate with fundamentally different goals, standards of evidence, and rules regarding transparency. Federal agencies focus on protecting restricted airspace and classified technology. When a UAP is spotted near a military base, counterintelligence officers investigate whether it was sent by an adversary for surveillance or if UAP rumors are being used as a cover for secret domestic aerospace testing. Since revealing how a UAP was detected might expose sensitive radar or satellite capabilities, the officers withhold data from the public. Scientific initiatives like the Galileo Project ignore geopolitical motives and focus strictly on physics and data quality. Scientists apply math, trigonometry, and atmospheric physics to calibrate sensor readings, such as calculating parallax from triangulation hardware to determine an object’s true speed and altitude (as reported here). If an observation lacks multiple, calibrated instruments, scientists classify it as inconclusive rather than speculating on its origin. Whereas deception may be actively used in counterintelligence operations to conceal sensitive defense projects, it is strictly forbidden in science where falsifying or obscuring data invalidates the research.

Counterintelligence knows about secret black projects that it wants to hide from view. It would like to find out who else may have noticed traces of these advanced technologies. In such a case, counterintelligence will suggest that the associated UAP might be of alien origin.

Science is driven by raw curiosity and seeks data on UAP in order to find out whether any of these anomalous objects might be of alien origin. A terrestrial UAP is a lower priority for scientists. The main reason they discuss terrestrial objects is to separate the extraterrestrial wheat from the terrestrial chaff.

The most common aerial phenomena recorded by the Galileo Project are clouds and flying animals, also known as birds. Recently, I was approached by UAP enthusiasts who told me that they are being followed by aliens. When a Galileo Project affiliate analyzed their videos, he found that the so-called UAP in the videos are clouds, satellites and planets. That the witnesses imagine aliens following them anywhere they go is understandable, since clouds, satellites and planets can indeed be seen from any location on Earth if one waits long enough.

The 8th tranche of UAP data was released yesterday here by the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE). It included limited digital data in the form of only 3 videos. As discussed in my essay here and in my interview on NewsNation here, most UAP in these videos are flickering as expected from birds that are flapping their wings. Such birds were flagged in 11% of all PURSUE videos in the first scientific paper from the UAP Science Advisory Council, available here. This frame-by-frame analysis benefitted from independent scientific data collected by the Galileo Project, as reported recently here.

The above two papers represent the work of science. The scientists involved are not aware of any secret black project and in fact would prefer not to study at all flying objects of terrestrial origin. But the scientific analysis of the data suggested birds.

Counterintelligence officers would not care less about identifying UAP that might be birds or clouds. All they care about is whether anyone, be it crackpots or scientists, noticed advanced technologies that are of national security relevance. If anyone classifies secret technologies as UAP, they will do their best to cover the evidence and reduce its credibility by arguing that these UAP might be of alien origin. The same may hold for biologics, namely relics of human pilots from adversarial nations, also known as terrestrial aliens.

Given this perspective, the Venn Diagram of counterintelligence and science has no overlap among the two. Counterintelligence asks: is this a threat, and whose is it? Its job is to detect foreign surveillance, protect sensitive facilities, and deny adversaries information about U.S. capabilities. A UAP over a weapons range is, from that perspective, first a possible adversarial platform and the response is to characterize it, attribute it, and prevent recurrence. Science asks: what is this, physically? Its job is to establish whether the phenomenon is real, measure its properties, and explain it. Attribution to a nation is one hypothesis among many, including a sensor artifact, a weather effect, or something genuinely new. The scientific question isn’t closed by “not a threat.”

Of course, crackpots will insist on a conspiracy theory by which the counterintelligence officers are in possession of alien technology. This interpretation would be a blessing for counterintelligence which wants to hide secrets about advanced technologies. This resonance between the agenda of crackpots and counterintelligence leads to a vicious cycle of misinformation filling the airways and muddying the waters on social media. The resulting nonsense is welcomed by both conspiracy theorists and counterintelligence officers, but is the poison that can kill any rational scientific deliberations.

As a corollary to the above situation: when scientists attempt to clear the fog, they are treated as a threat by both conspiracy theorists and counterintelligence who prefer to maintain the fog in order to leave their narratives alive and well.

It is straightforward to separate rational scientists from crackpots or counterintelligence officers by how they respond to collecting new scientific data. If they regard new data as a blessing irrespective of what the evidence entails, they are driven by the scientific method. If they are willing to admit that most UAP in the latest release of 3 videos are birds, they are genuine rational thinkers without a hidden agenda.

Counterintelligence does not lend more weight to careful scientific analysis than to the work of crackpots, because it already knows what needs to be hidden from view. On the other hand, genuine scientists demand physical or digital evidence rather than storytelling: if the evidence exists only in anecdotal eyewitness reports or counterintelligence channels, science can’t evaluate it, and the claim stays in the category of testimony. The two fields can share a subject, but they can’t share a standard of proof, and most of the public confusion about UAP comes from treating one standard as if it were the other.
