Title: UFOs: Avi Loeb says the observations Air Force pilots told Trump are puzzling at best
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b9XXtlL1XfE (Jesse Weber Live, uploaded 2026-11-10)

(NewsNation) — Harvard physicist Avi Loeb addressed President Donald Trump’s claims about UFOs, describing the information he received from the Air Force pilots as puzzling.

Trump, who shared his thoughts on the subject during a “60 Minutes” interview Sunday, said he’s been briefed on several unusual sightings reported by Air Force pilots during meetings held throughout both of his terms in office.

Trump’s UFO claims after meeting Air Force pilots

“They’re great, great pilots,” Trump told interviewer Ross Douthat. “They’re the real deal. And they’ve told me things that are very strange. They said that in certain cases. They said yes, there’s something going on out there. Because they’re flying an F-22, which is the best there is, the fastest, the best plane that anyone’s ever seen. And something goes by them at three times their speed, and it’s round. It’s not aerodynamic, it’s round.”

Appearing on “Jesse Weber Live,” Loeb said the information from the pilots is very reliable.

“These are people who are well-trained,” he said. “And they do not hallucinate. They use their best instruments.”

Loeb stated officials could gather more detailed information by deploying improved sensors in the areas where the objects were observed.

“We can actually figure out what these objects are and what they are made of,” he noted.

Objects Air Force pilots saw must move faster than the speed of sound: Loeb

Asked by Weber how the objects could be faster than fighter jets, Loeb acknowledged that propulsion itself is not the determining factor.

“The puzzle is if they move faster than the speed of sound such an object, since it’s round, should create a shock wave, a blast wave in the air, and that was not observed,” he said.

“And there is a lot of friction in objects the faster they move through air. So there must be something to do with the way the objects move through air that we don’t fully understand, in addition to the propulsion. It’s a work in progress.”

Trump added that he is not a firm believer in UFOs but acknowledged the topic is one of the questions he is asked about most often in private conversations. He also said that he has seen no evidence of a secret government program hidden even from presidents, despite claims made by whistleblowers who have publicly discussed such efforts.
