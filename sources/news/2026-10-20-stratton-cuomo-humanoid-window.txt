Ex-government UAP hunter pens book, describes aliens he’s seen
(NewsNation) — Former Pentagon UAP authority Jay Stratton spent much of his career hunting for alien intelligence, but it turns out extraterrestrial visitors may have been keeping tabs on him, too.
The author of the newly published memoir, “Out of the Shadows: Revealing the Truth About Non-Human Intelligent Life,” tells “CUOMO” that he arose early one morning to see something strange peeking into his Washington, D.C.-area home.
“What I witnessed initially was this humanoid-looking creature … looking at my son through the window,” Stratton said Tuesday. He said the being had a “very large head, very small black eyes, very small nose — if it had one — and a small slit for a mouth.”
After locking eyes with the thing, Stratton says, he gave chase.
“By the time I stepped outside, it was completely gone. Completely gone,” he said.
UFO hunter says encounters began in the 2010s
Stratton says these types of encounters began in the 2010s, years after he began investigating “unidentified anomalous phenomena” for the Pentagon’s military intelligence arm. He served as director of the Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force, which emphasized the national security risks surrounding UFOs.
In his book, Stratton recounts his efforts to investigate UAP that seemed drawn to American military assets. He also supports the theory that federal departments and contractors are in possession of nonhuman technology under a secretive “legacy” program that is competing with adversarial nations that have obtained their own fragments.
Each nation is trying to unlock the capabilities of the technology, and “the country that wins that race is the superpower for the next hundred years,” Stratton said.
Stratton, who retired in 2022, says federal agencies reviewed his manuscript to ensure it did not contain classified material. He was featured in the recent documentary, “The Age of Disclosure.”
