Burlison Names UAP Whistleblower Jake Barber Senior Adviser

November 18, 2026 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. — Congressman Eric Burlison (MO-07) announced today that U.S. Air Force Veteran and UAP whistleblower Jake Barber has joined his office as a senior adviser on unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP).

Barber brings experience in military aviation, special operations, and defense contracting. In January 2025, he came forward publicly with firsthand accounts of UAP-recovery operations.

“Jake brings firsthand experience and an investigative background that will strengthen our efforts to get answers,” said Congressman Burlison. “President Trump has opened the door to greater transparency, and Congress needs to keep pushing. We have an opportunity to follow the evidence and uncover information the American people deserve to see.”

Barber will assist with congressional investigations, witness engagement, and efforts to independently verify UAP-related evidence.

“Testimony is evidence. It helps us understand where to look and what questions to ask,” said Barber. “But our goal is to build on that testimony with technical measurements, multiple independent observations, and reliable documentation. We're interested in evidence that can stand on its own.”

His appointment comes amid President Trump's ongoing disclosure efforts through the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE), which has resulted in the release of hundreds of government records.

Burlison is also working to advance his UAP Disclosure Act, which passed the House as part of the fiscal year 2027 National Defense Authorization Act. The legislation would establish an independent review board to oversee the preservation and release of government UAP records.
