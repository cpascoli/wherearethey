I.O. NAF Port Lyautey, F.M. ser 1-R-52 of 15 August 1952 (Enclosure 2)
STATEMENT OF LT D. M. DOBOS
On a routine night training flight in R5D 50865 on 7 August 1952, the following incident was observed. I was in the pilot's seat. LT Foster was in the co-pilot seat and LT Orr was standing in the aisle between us two.
After a normal takeoff from runway 030, and a climb to 5,000 feet on a heading of 270 degrees, we decided to reverse course and go to Sidi Slimane Air Force Base. On a heading of 110 degrees, LT Foster and I observed a brilliant white light traversing the right front windshield and continuing to our rear. It seemed to be at our altitude which at that time was 4500 feet. From first sighting until the object was to our rear, the time was approximately two seconds. I turned, keeping the object in sight until I was on a heading of 270 degrees. The object was flying in the same direction. As I started to climb toward it, the object climbed upward at a terrific rate of speed, first a shallow angle, then a very steep angle, continuing away from us until it was only a faint bright light. Altitude of the object at that time I estimate to be 15,000 feet. It was at the top of high scattered cirrus clouds.
During this time, LT Foster contacted Port Lyautey tower, giving our position and the object's position. At first the tower did not see the object, but when we said it was on a course of 270 degrees and definitely gave our position as slightly south of the field, he also sighted the object and kept track of it as we did. We talked to the tower stating size, brightness, speed and altitude.
After the object had almost disappeared on a heading of 270 degrees, it turned sharply across our path. I again turned toward toward the object, continuing my climb toward it. At this time I noticed a very bright disc center--brighter than white phosphorus--with two definite blinking red lights. The red blinking lights appeared to be on wingtips, but I could see no wings--only the bright glow of alternately blinking lights. At this time, the object seemed to hover and descend downward as I continued to climb toward it. It then started up and away from us.
I was for following the object, but LT Foster suggested that we turn away from it and continue to Sidi Slimane because it would have been impossible to catch up, due to the terrific rate of speed and acceleration characteristics.
The glow was so bright that I do not believe it could have been made by one of our jets flying at night. The tower informed us that there was another aircraft in the vicinity; however we located this other aircraft north of the field. I definitely recognized the plane, seeing its blinking red and green lights, and comparatively slow movement.
We then continued on to Sidi Slimane where we practiced GCA approaches. We returned to base at about 2325.

STATEMENT OF LT W. FOSTER - 7 AUGUST
At 2020 I observed a brilliant white light passing from port to starboard. I was on a heading of 110 degrees. I remarked to the other pilot who had seen the same thing. The object was travelling at an unusual speed. We turned to the heading it was traveling. Our speed was increased to approximately [illegible] knots. The light grew dim very rapidly.
