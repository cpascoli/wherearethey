THE RAF DEPENDANTS FUND

General

1. The RAF Dependants Fund was established on 1 Jul 67 with an initia! grant of £50,000
from the RAF Central Fund.

2. The purpose of the RAF Dependants Fund is to provide a single tax-free payment for the
immediate need of widows, widowers, dependant children, or other nominated dependants of
subscribing members of the Fund who die while serving on a regular RAF engagement or on specially
designated Full Time Reserve Service.

3. The RAF Dependants Fund is a registered charity. The governing Trust Deed, Constitution
and Rules have been registered in accordance with the Charities Act.

Scope of the Fund

4. All officers, airmen and airwomen, including members of the PMRAFNS, who are serving
on regular engagements may apply for membership of the Fund. Specially designated Full Time
Reserve Service (FTRS) personnel may aiso apply for membership. Eligibility will cease on the last
day of paid service regardless of age.

Grant Payment

5. Because of the charitable status of the RAF Dependants Fund, the basic grant payable on
death will be at the discretion of the Managing Trustees and will be related to the current financial
state of the Fund. The amount to be paid will be reviewed by the Managing Trustees annually, or as
dictated by exceptional circumstances, and promulgated to the RAF.

Subscription

6. The subscription is 25p per month per member. Six months advance warning will be
given to members in the event of any increase. Subscriptions will be collected monthly in arrears by
AFPAA(Inns) and paid to the Fund.

Income Tax
7. Subscriptions to the Fund do not qualify for income tax relief.
Closure During Emergency

8. The Managing Trustees may close the Fund to new subscribers when war has been
declared or when, in their sole opinion, war is imminent. In such a case, the dependants of those
who were subscribers at the date of closure may be considered for assistance from the Fund for such
period and in such circumstances as the Managing Trustees in their sole opinion shall decide. Should
the Fund be so closed, the Managing Trustees will determine the day on which it shall re-open and
will notify all serving personnel of the date of such re-opening. .

9. Eligible personnel may join the Fund at any time. Application for membership is to be
made on RAF Form 7230 (RAF Dependants Fund - Application/Voluntary Withdrawal), a composite 3-
part form. The purpose of the various parts is as follows:

a. Flysheet. The Flysheet contains basic information on membership of the Fund. It
is to be detached and retained by the applicant on submission of the Form 7230.

b. Original. The original is used as the authority for the deduction of subscriptions
from pay.

c. Duplicate. The duplicate is a card on which the subscriber agrees to be bound by
the rules of the Fund. It becomes the Fund’s permanent record of membership and
beneficiary.

Effective Date of Membership

10. Membership of the Fund will commence from the date on which the applicant signs the
Form 7230 provided that the applicant is eligible for membership in accordance with para 4 and
provided there is no undue delay in the Form 7230 reaching the Secretary of the Fund. The
Managing Trustees’ decision on the effective date of commencement will be final.

Action in Personnel Services Flight and Disposal of Form 7230

11. Form 7230 Is to be signed by OC PSF certifying the eligibility of the applicant. Should an
applicant's dependant be under 18 years of age, the applicant is to nominate on Form 7230 a
Responsible Adult through whom any monies can be paid.

12. After certification by OC PSF, both the original and duplicate copies of Form 7230 are to
be forwarded to the RAF Dependants Fund at the address shown in para 25. All applications from
FTRS personnel must be accompanied by Form 7455 duly completed and authorised by OC PSF.

Action by the Secretary of the Fund
13. "The Secretary of the Fund is responsible for:

a. Ensuring the computer input coding is correct on the original copy of the Form 7230
and forwarding the form to AFPAA(Inns) as the authority to commence the deduction of
subscriptions.

b. Retaining the duplicate copy of the Form 7230 as a permanent record of Fund
membership and details of the beneficiary.

c. Amending details of beneficiaries from RAF Forms 7233 received from units.
Withdrawal of Membership

14. Withdrawal of Membership. Members of the Fund who do not subscribe to the RAF
Dependants (Income) Trust can withdraw from the Fund by completing Form 7230 and inserting
Code ‘2’ as shown in para 4 on Form 7230. The original and duplicate copies of the Form 7230 are to
be forwarded to the Fund for action and onward transmission to AFPAA(Inns). Personnel who
withdraw from the Fund will not be eligible for any refund of subscriptions already paid.

(NB - Fund membership is a pre-requisite to joining the RAF Dependants (Income) Trust and,
therefore, those personnel who subscribe to both the Fund and Trust cannot withdraw from the Fund
without also ceasing membership of the Trust using Form 7329 - see AP3392, Vol 2, Leaflet 2406.

Change of Nominated Dependant or Change of Name of Subscriber

15. Change of Nominated Dependant. RAF Form 7233 (RAF Dependants Fund and RAF
Dependants (Income) Trust - Notice of Amendment), raised in duplicate, is to be used to notify the
Fund of a change of nominated dependant. After signature, the original copy of Form 7233 is to be

filed in the individual's Form 445A and the duplicate sent to the Fund. To avoid ambiguity, it is
essential that subscribers to both the Fund and RAF Dependants (Income) Trust complete both
sections on the Form 7233 relating to the Fund and Trust.

16. Change of Nominated Dependant on Marriage. When a subscriber marries, the
Trustees will automaticaily assume that the spouse is to become the nominated beneficiary. Where
the individual wishes a person other than their spouse to be the beneficiary, details are to be sent to
the Secretary of the Fund on Form 7233. In the event of separation, divorce or re-marriage, it is
imperative that subscribers notify the Secretary of the Fund of any change of beneficiary, if
appropriate. If in doubt, subscribers are invited to contact the Secretary of the Fund to discuss
beneficiary options.

17. Change of Name of Subscriber. Form 7233 is to be used to notify the Secretary of
the Fund of the change of surname of any subscriber. If this also involves a change in the name of
the nominated beneficiary, the two occurrences may be included on the same form.

Continuity of Membership during periods of Non-Effectiveness for Pay

18. Fund members who become non-effective for pay due to secondment to other forces
must make private arrangements with the Secretary of the Fund for payment of monthly
subscriptions for the period of secondment. Subscribers going on the unpaid portion of
Maternity/Paternity Leave must make similar arrangements with the Secretary of the Fund. As no
loss can be borne by public funds in respect of subscriptions to the Fund collected through RAF Pay,
failure to pay subscriptions privately during the period of non-effectiveness for pay will result in the
termination of the subscriber’s membership.

19, Illegal Absence. Any subscriber to the Fund who is declared illegally absent will
automatically cease to be a member of the Fund. Such individuals may re-apply to join the Fund on
becoming effective for pay again.

20. Detention. When a subscriber to the Fund is awarded detention, membership of the
Fund will be allowed to continue uninterrupted with subscriptions being deducted from .the
individual’s RAF pay in the normal manner.

definitely self-luminous. The outer edges of both objects were not well defined, giving
the impression of being either jagged or fuzzy. There were darker areas inside the shape
but not easily defined. With no point of reference it was impossible to be definite about
height or size, but the impression was that they were not very high, maybe a few
hundreds of feet. The whole sighting lasted no more than about five seconds.
