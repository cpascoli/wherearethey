Morfa Nefyn, where we arrived on Wednesday, the August 12. We stayed at a
Bed&Breakfast, called Llys Olwen Country Guest House, Lon Tan y Bryn, Morfa Nefyn,
Pwliheli, LL53 6BT.

We got a room in the first floor and the view from the window leaded to the coast. The
windows itself began about 60 cm from the ground. The bed is opposite to the windows
and has had a similar high of 50 cm.

About at 10 p.m., whe went to bed. | had set the alarm clock, because we wanted to
leave Morfa Nefyn the next day. We had opened the window. It was quiet, because there
is no road.

| couldn't sleep, but | had my eyes closed. It was 3 minutes after 11 p.m., when a bright
light shown through the window. At first, | thought it could be a car or a spotlight.
However, it didn’t move.

This light was very bright and went twice off for a very short moment and came back.
Because | have to wear glasses (- 4.5 dioptres), | couldn't recognise what it was, only
that the light was very glaring so that | had to hold my hand in front of my eyes.

| was wondering why it didn’t move - if it were a car or if it were a spotiight where does it
come from and who is making a joke to wake up the B&B’s guests ?

| put on my glasses, got up and went to the window.

| saw more than 3 lights about 70 meters far. Because it was night, | can’t give you a
correct distance. | attached a picture, please look at the end.

These lights moved very fast so that | couldn’t count them. Each of them had a size of a
basketball (approximate, perhaps a bit bigger). Some of them looked like a translucent
bowl, other like a ball made from translucent glass where light came out. They moved
repeatedly up and down and rotated repeatedly on their own axis with a speed which |
have never seen before. One light stood still in a height of about 1 meter over the
ground. Light shown down to the ground and turned up to the sky, so that | saw the light
beam shown up into the sky, then it turned again on its own axis, moved up and down.
The maximum height was approximate 1.50 meters from the ground.

distance was about 10 meters. | was very surprised, because | couldn’t see any rail
construction or something like that and a movement like this is not possible for objects,
normally. And again, the lights began to move repeatedly up and down. There, where
the other lights were before (Position’1 at my picture) was nothing suddenly.

About 20 seconds later, 1 light (it was shining weakly) moved again to the left behind
bushes with the same speed and vanished.
After the last light had vanished, | went to bed. It was 7 past 11 p.m.

The time between when | was laying in the bed and | saw the light through the window
and when | stood on the window was about 1 minute.

While looking at these lights from the window, it seemed to me that each of them lost its

That friend of mine slept very deep and while standing on the window, | called her and

have some defence significance; namely, whether there is any evidence that the United
Kingdom’s airspace might have been compromised by hostile or unauthorised air activity.

Unless there is evidence of a potential threat to the United Kingdom from an external source,
and to date no UFO report has revealed such evidence, we do not attempt to identify the
precise nature of each sighting reported to us. We believe that rational explanations, such as
aircraft lights or natural phenomena, could be found for them if resources were diverted for
