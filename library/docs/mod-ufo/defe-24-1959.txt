Subject: Unidentified Flying Object.

Date: 8 December 1993 Ref:

Duty Sergeant,

As per standing orders I submit report of U.F.O. reported to me by

A) Monday 6th December, 1993, at 16.10 hrs. Duration of sighting
between 5 and 10 seconds.

B) Described as a sphere, cut in half, flat side uppermost {like a
bowl), solid, white in colour, almost fluorescent. Size, as large
as a single storey house. No sound or smell, but had 2 vapour

trails behind, one on top of the other. Vapour did not linger but
dispersed in seconds.

occupant.
D) Observed with naked eye only.
E) First noticed to east of observer, over King Edmunds School,

Vaughan Close, Rochford.
F) Viewed from the ground approx 200 ft up.

G) When first sighted seemed to be approx 1/4 mile away, but passed
within 200 ft of observer.

H) Moved in a perfectly straight line, from east to west.
Disappeared over railway line at rear of Banyard Way, in
Rawkwell.

J) Dry, very clear sky, but light failing as dusk approached. Fierce

fire several miles away in Eastwood and thick black smoke blowing
north eastwards, but not over location of sighting.

RK) Housing area. Railway line close to location. Airport approx 1
mile away.

L) Reported by observer to Southend Airport and Ministry of Defence,
Foulness 6.12.93, shortly after the sighting. Reported to
Rochford Police Station 1530 hrs 7.12.93.
