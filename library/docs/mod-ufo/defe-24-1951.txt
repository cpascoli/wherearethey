1. I report with reference to the attached teleprinter message with
regard to the action to be taken following reported sightings of
unidentified flying objects.

2. At 2120hrs. on Wednesday, 31st ocameee cio 0
both resides and works as a farmer at!

Nr.Lichfield, was working in a field at the farm when he observed what
he described as the following:-

3. Either three or four bright white lights which were close together

and remained on. The lights were observed coming from the direction of
Hopwas Woods and when almost overhead of the observer, stopped and

remained stationary, momentarily, at an estimated height of 400 feet.

The lights then moved away towards the direction of Botany Bay, Whittington,,.
which is located on the A51 trunk road linking Lichfield and Tamworth.

4, SERINE tescribed the object upon which the lights were carried as
flat and possibly rounded, of a very bright colour. It made nonoise and
there was no apparant odour. The object was described as being larger than
a helicopter.

5. Approximately ten minutes following this sighting , Beene) saw what
he described as a set of red and white flashing lights approaching from
the same direction as outlined in respect of the first onject. These
lights approached and went overhead of the observer, moving away in the
same direction as previously stated. The observer is unable to give any
indication as to either size or shape of the object on which the lights
were carried. He states that no noise or odour was apparent and that the
object was flying higher than that of his first sighting. The second set
of lights moved at a faster pace than the first. GERI observed the
sightings with naked eyes.

6. The objects were first sighted approximately three quarters of a mile
away from the observer at a point over Hopwas Woods, near to the television
mast located near to the A51 at Packington.

7. The objects were described as moving towards, overhead and away from
the observer at one height and in a straight line.

8. In respect of the prevailing meteorological conditions, it was a fine,
clear evening, with no cloud or haze.
