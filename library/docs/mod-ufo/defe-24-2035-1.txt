Tam writing to acknowledge receipt of your letters of 13", 15", 28" and 29" July.

Following my colleague BERIMON letter of 12 May in answer to your previous correspondence,
it would appear that you have misunderstood the Ministry of Defence’s limited interest in reports
-of ‘unidentified flying objects’. It may therefore be helpful if I clarify our position.

The MOD does not have any expertise or role in respect of 'UFO/flying saucer’ matters or to the
question of the existence or otherwise of extraterrestrial lifeforms. While we remain totally open-
minded, to date we know of no evidence which substantiates the existence of these alleged
phenomena.

Reports of sightings of ‘UFOs’ received by MOD are examined solely to establish whether they
contain any evidence that the United Kingdom’s airspace might have been compromised by
hostile or unauthorised air activity. Once it is established a report does not contain such evidence
we do not attempt to establish the precise nature of the sighting, as this would go beyond our
specific defence remit.

With regard to the identification of possible planets you have viewed through your telescope, we

have no expertise in this field. I note you have also written to the Royal Observatory at
Greenwich who, I hope, will be able to assist you.
