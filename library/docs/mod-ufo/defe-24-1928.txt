At 1930 hours on Thursday, 18 February 1988 I was off duty with
my wife and & year old daughter. We were travelling in my
vehicie along Toller Lane, Bradford towards Bingley.

When approximately at the junction with Leylands Lane, my
daughter remarked that she could see a star that was moving.
My wife locked at the object and said that it looked like a
line of shining white lights and was going in the same
direction as ourselves. I leant across the car and saw

the object myself. It appeared to be fairly high in the
sky. 1 could see @ line of white lights travelling at the

usual speed of an aeroplane.

My family requested that I stop to look more closely and i
decided tc stop in Thorne Lane off Bingley Road. This road
overlooks the whole area and judging by the speed of the
object I thought 1 would get there before it did.

However, as 1 passed the junction with Haworth Road, my wife
who had watched tne object continually, remarked that it
suddenly turned left and accelerated quickly away.

When I stopped in Thorne Lane some seconds later the object
was nowhere in sight.

During the period whilst it was in sight I glanced at it on
three occasions. It was the size of a small passenger plane
and it was difficult to judge its shape. The line of lights
suggested it was long and thin.

I could heer no noise over the car noise. It was observed
with the naked eye and no photographs were taken.

The weather was ary and windy, there were a few clouds in the
sky that were moving quite fast with the wind. The object
flew over @ housing estate the only large buildings would be
the Bradford koyai Infirmary and Sharpe's Printers.

On first seeing the object it could have been an aeroplane
even though we could not see any coloured lights but when the
object changed direction suddenly and accelerated we were sure

it was nov.

I have attached a map showing where my vehicle travelled during
the sighting. The dotted line is the approximate route taken
by the object.
