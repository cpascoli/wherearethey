REPORT OF LOW FLYING AIRCRAFT/UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECT/S

I am providing you with the following report of a sighting of an
Unidentified Flying Object by members of the public on Saturday 30 June,
1990.

A. Date, time and duration of sighting:

Saturday, 30 June, 1990, at about 02.40 am, sighting for 10
minutes.

B. Description of aircraft/object:

(a) Oblong front, front lit up with like search lights, flat
around the back with two red lights to rear, no engine
noise.

(b) Dome in the front, hammer head shape, round at the front went
into a thin body, two wings at the back, lights all along the
front, white lights to the front.

c. Exact position of observers:

Sitting on bank of gravel pit fishing - outside location.
D. How observed:

Observed with own eyes.

Angle of sight:
Not known.
Distance:

200/300 yards distance away, Moreton gravel pit off Norton Bridge
(Map ref 536065).

Movements:

(a) No speed, just sitting there over the top of me, no noise,

(b) Hovered over slowly and just stayed, it didn't shoot off, it

Meteorological conditions during the observations:
(a) Clear morning - stars moving.

Nearby objects:

(a) Pit surrounded by fields, no power cables, no lights in the
